## Concepts covered

**[From lazy to rich: from the kernel to feature learning](../../concepts/lazy-to-rich-kernel-to-feature-learning.md)** — the work **makes this transition the explanation of grokking**. The "memorising solution" of the earlier works is identified with an early lazy dynamics: the network fits the sample as a linear model with the initial NTK, and later rebuilds its features, and it is precisely the gap between the two stages that constitutes the delayed generalisation ([`p4-3`](#p4-2)). The laziness is governed by the output scale $\alpha$: at $\alpha\to\infty$ the model linearises exactly, and at a small $\alpha$ the features are learned at once and the grokking disappears.

**[The neural tangent kernel (NTK)](../../concepts/neural-tangent-kernel-ntk.md)** — the work gives the notion the **role of a measure of task difficulty**. The alignment of the initial NTK with the labels (CKA) determines how much feature learning is necessary; a hard task is defined formally as $\boldsymbol{y}^{\top}\boldsymbol{K}^{-1}\boldsymbol{y}\gg1$, and the same quantity sets how far the parameters will travel — that is, the difficulty for the kernel and the breakdown of the linearisation turn out to be one and the same ([`p17-1`](#p17-1)).

**[Initialisation scale](../../concepts/initialization-scale.md)** — the work **re-explains** its role: a large initial weight norm induces grokking not because the generalising solutions lie on spheres of smaller norm but because it linearises the model. The check is direct: the output scale and the label scale give the same effect as the weight norm at initialisation, and the relative displacement of the parameters is governed by the product $\sigma^{2}\alpha$ ([`p13-3`](#fig-7)).

**[Weight norm minimisation](../../concepts/weight-norm-minimization.md)** — receives a **counterexample**. On modular arithmetic in a two-layer MLP without weight decay the weight norm *grows* during the training, and the network groks all the same ([`p3-3`](#p3-3)). The final generalising solution has a larger norm than the memorising one from which the network started — a direct objection both to Omnigrok and to the explanation through circuit efficiency.

**[Variants of regularisation](../../concepts/regularization-variants.md)** — the work shows that weight decay is **neither necessary nor sufficient** for grokking, but explains why it accompanies it so often: by Lewkowycz and Gur-Ari (2020), under weight decay the NTK evolves at order $O(\kappa)$, that is, weight decay pushes the network out of the lazy regime. Hence a prediction the authors call a quantitative confirmation: the linearity of the generalisation time in the weight decay observed by their predecessors ([`p19-4`](#p19-4)).

**[The Goldilocks zone](../../concepts/goldilocks-zone.md)** — the notion is applied **to the sample size**: there must be enough data for generalisation to be possible, but not so much that the training loss immediately tracks the test one. Appendix 8.3.2 shows all three regimes on one task, and appendix 17 a grid of "task difficulty × amount of data" in which grokking occupies an intermediate band.

**[Data fraction / critical dataset size](../../concepts/data-fraction-critical-dataset-size.md)** — the work refines the status of the threshold: being within the Goldilocks zone in data is **necessary but not sufficient** for grokking; the second condition remains a lazy start, the third a mismatch of the kernel with the task.

**[The emergence of features / feature learning](../../concepts/feature-emergence-feature-learning.md)** — here feature learning acquires a **measurable signature in the decomposition of the loss**: the test MSE splits into a variance error, an alignment error and the power in the linear modes, and grokking is visible as a rise and then a fall of the last ([`p7-1`](#p6-4)). This is a rare case in which "the network has learned features" is not a description of a picture but a term in a formula.

**[Emergence](../../concepts/emergence.md)** — the work joins the dispute about how real the jumps are: appendix 16 shows that "grokking in accuracy" may be a mirage of the choice of threshold on a regression task, whereas grokking in the loss is a strictly stronger condition. Hence a methodological requirement: study the loss, not the accuracy.

**[The memorisation phase](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — memorisation is redefined as a **lazy fit**: the network interpolates the training set by a kernel-regression solution on the initial features, and on test points this works worse than chance if the features do not match the task.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the task is used twice: as a counterexample to the weight-norm theories (a two-layer MLP, $p=23$, 90 % of the pairs, no weight decay) and as a check of transferability — on a single-layer transformer from Nanda et al. the single parameter $\alpha$ makes the grokking disappear.

**[Real data: vision, MNIST, CIFAR](../../concepts/vision-real-world-data-mnist-cifar.md)** — the grokking on MNIST, induced in Liu et al. by increasing the weight norm, is here **continuously removed by tuning $\alpha$ with no change of the weight norm** — an argument that the weight norm is merely one of the ways of controlling the laziness.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the notion receives a mechanism requiring neither regularisation nor a decay of the norm, and three conditions at once: a mismatch of the initial NTK with the task, a Goldilocks amount of data and a lazy start.

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the original observation; [Liu et al. 2022](../2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning/2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning.card.md) and [Omnigrok](../2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data/2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data.card.md) — the explanations through the weight norm, to which a counterexample is given here; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — the transformer setting on which the control through $\alpha$ is checked; [Varma et al. 2023](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — the authors agree with two of its three causes and argue outright with the central one: "efficiency" as a smaller parameter norm is refuted here.

**[When regularisation is necessary](../../concepts/regularization-necessity.md)** — the "not necessary" pole: a perceptron [groks on a polynomial regression without weight decay under vanilla gradient descent](#fig-4), and [at a growing parameter norm](#p3-3) — a counterexample at once to the necessity of regularisation and to the norm-based explanations.

**[The generalising circuit](../../concepts/generalization-circuit.md)** — the work limits the picture of competing circuits: the presence of two solutions is not enough for a delay, because tuning one laziness parameter makes the grokking disappear continuously ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)). The delay, then, is created by the regime of the dynamics rather than by the mere existence of two circuits.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). This work plays the role of a refutation in the corpus: it adds not so much one more explanation as a demonstration that two of the previous ones described a consequence rather than a cause.

**The counterexample is the firmest part.** A two-layer MLP on modular arithmetic with no weight decay whatever groks, and the weight norm **grows** in the process ([`p3-3`](#p3-3)). The final generalising solution has a larger norm than the memorising one from which the network started. This directly contradicts both "the generalising solutions lie on spheres of smaller norm" (Omnigrok) and "the generalising circuit is more efficient because it gives the same loss at a smaller norm" (Varma et al.). The objection is concrete and reproducible rather than conceptual.

**The re-explanation is stronger than the refutation.** The work does not deny its predecessors' observations but rereads them: a large initial norm really does induce grokking — because it linearises the model. The check is direct and convincing: the output scale and the label scale give the same effect, and the relative displacement of the parameters is governed by the product $\sigma^{2}\alpha$ ([`p13-3`](#fig-7)). The same holds for weight decay: it is not needed for grokking, but its frequency is explained — by somebody else's theorem, weight decay makes the NTK evolve at order $O(\kappa)$, that is, pushes the network out of the lazy regime.

**A prediction made before the experiment.** From the diagonalisation of the initial NTK follows a separation of sample complexities: a kernel method needs $P\sim D^{2}$ examples, a network with feature learning $P\sim D$. It was for this that the task was chosen: grokking was expected in the interval — and is observed. A second such place is the construction with eigenvector labels, in which the difficulty of the task is set by the index $j$, and the three predicted regimes (immediate generalisation, grokking, impossibility) are observed in order.

**Where the mechanism is admitted to be insufficient — by the authors themselves, and only in an appendix.** On the single-layer transformer of Nanda et al. the loss departs quickly from its linearisation because of the weight decay, **although grokking is present there**. Hence the conclusion of appendix 14: the lazy-to-rich transition is a "sufficient but not necessary" mechanism of delayed feature learning. In the main text and the abstract this caveat is not repeated, and the carry-over to the transformer is presented as a confirmation.

**The quantitative confirmation is weaker than it sounds.** The phrase that the theory "predicts exactly the quantitative effect of weight decay on the time scales of grokking" leans on somebody else's theorem about the order $O(\kappa)$ and on two linearities observed in other people's works. There is no experiment of the authors' own in the paper in which the predicted and the measured dependences are compared quantitatively — there is an experiment about the opposing directions of $\alpha$ and weight decay, which is weaker.

**Appendix 16 is methodologically more valuable than many sections.** It is shown that "grokking in accuracy" on regression tasks depends on the choice of threshold $\theta$: the same loss curves give a striking jump at one threshold and nothing at another. Grokking in the loss is strictly stronger than grokking in accuracy. For the corpus something concrete follows: the teacher–student setting of Liu et al. 2022a, which served as evidence of grokking outside algorithmic data, does **not** show grokking in terms of the loss — the training curve follows the test one throughout ([`p24-1`](#fig-18)).

**Trifles.** The learning rate $\eta=100$ in the counterexample is not commented on; the robustness of the result across $p$ and across width is claimed in words, with no table; and the formula of the three-index model contains the misprint `\beta_{3}cdotx`, carried over as it stands.

## Common misreadings and overstatements of this paper

Patterns of misreading or overstatement of this work, as observed in the citing papers of the corpus. Each entry says what exactly gets distorted in the restatement and leads to the authors' own paragraphs, where the qualification that goes missing still stands. The specific wordings are examined in the citing works' cards, in their "Common misreadings and overstatements" sections, where the "details" links lead.

**An inversion of the effect of the output scale (erroneous).** The work is credited with the claim that increasing the output scale accelerates grokking. In the authors' account it is exactly the reverse: [a larger scale α makes the dynamics lazier and lengthens the delay, whereas reducing α removes the grokking](#p4-2); in the limit α→∞ the network linearises and does not generalise at all. The sign of the effect is easily inverted when the result is paraphrased through somebody else's parametrisation. The details: [2509.21519](../2509.21519.provable-scaling-laws-of-feature-emergence-from-learning-dynamics-of-grokking/2509.21519.provable-scaling-laws-of-feature-emergence-from-learning-dynamics-of-grokking.card.md#mc-2310-06110).

**Kumar in the "weight norm" or "initialisation scale" camp (erroneous or a half-truth).** The work is placed in a list of explanations through weight-norm dynamics — whereas it is built as the chief counterexample to them: [in its settings grokking proceeds at a growing parameter norm, with no weight decay](#p3-3) ([fig. 1](#fig-1)). A related inaccuracy is the attribution "initialization scale/regimes": this work's lever is the output scale α, while the initialisation scale is the lever of Liu et al.; [the equivalence of the two levers](#fig-7) makes such an attribution a half-truth, but grouping it with the norm-based works erases the opposition of the conclusions. The details: [2604.13123](../2604.13123.spectral-entropy-collapse-as-a-phase-transition-in-delayed-generalisation/2604.13123.spectral-entropy-collapse-as-a-phase-transition-in-delayed-generalisation.card.md#mc-2310-06110); milder echoes: [2603.01968](../2603.01968.intrinsic-task-symmetry-drives-generalization-in-algorithmic-tasks/2603.01968.intrinsic-task-symmetry-drives-generalization-in-algorithmic-tasks.card.md), [2504.13292](../2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model/2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model.card.md).

**"Lazy-to-rich explains grokking" without the limits of applicability (an over-extension).** Survey paraphrases present the transition from the lazy regime to feature learning as a general explanation of grokking. The authors themselves admit in appendix 14 that the mechanism is sufficient but not necessary: on a single-layer transformer with AdamW and a cross-entropy the loss departs quickly from the linearisation although grokking is present — and that caveat is repeated neither in the main text nor in the abstract (see the section "What is shown and what is conjectured"). Counter-evidence has come from those who cite it too: [Prieto et al.](../2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability/2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability.card.md) — deeper than one hidden layer and with a cross-entropy the same settings do not generalise; [2504.13292](../2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model/2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model.card.md) — grokking persists under a calibrated initialisation scale. Milder echoes: [2509.17738](../2509.17738.flatness-is-necessary-neural-collapse-is-not-rethinking-generalization-via-grokking/2509.17738.flatness-is-necessary-neural-collapse-is-not-rethinking-generalization-via-grokking.card.md), [2510.04930](../2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking/2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking.card.md).

**"Were the first to show grokking without weight decay" (erroneous).** The priority formula "first showed that neither weight norms nor adaptive optimizers are necessary" overstates the contribution: grokking without weight decay is already visible in the canonical figure 1 of [Power et al.](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md), it is demonstrated without explicit regularisation by [Thilak et al.](../2206.04817.the-slingshot-mechanism-an-empirical-study-of-adaptive-optimizers-and-grokking/2206.04817.the-slingshot-mechanism-an-empirical-study-of-adaptive-optimizers-and-grokking.card.md), and by [Gromov](../2301.02679.grokking-modular-arithmetic/2301.02679.grokking-modular-arithmetic.card.md) under vanilla gradient descent with no regularisation at all. This work's contribution is a counterexample to the norm theories plus the lazy-to-rich mechanism, not the priority of the observation. The details: [2601.03162](../2601.03162.on-the-convergence-behavior-of-preconditioned-gradient-descent-toward-the-rich-learning-regime/2601.03162.on-the-convergence-behavior-of-preconditioned-gradient-descent-toward-the-rich-learning-regime.card.md#mc-2310-06110).

Examples of careful citation: [Prieto et al.](../2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability/2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability.card.md) — the limits of applicability (small networks, MSE) in every mention; [Miller et al.](../2310.17247.grokking-beyond-neural-networks-an-empirical-exploration-with-model-complexity/2310.17247.grokking-beyond-neural-networks-an-empirical-exploration-with-model-complexity.card.md) — "a form of grokking", "they argue"; [2605.06152](../2605.06152.grokking-or-glitching-how-low-precision-drives-slingshot-loss-spikes/2605.06152.grokking-or-glitching-how-low-precision-drives-slingshot-loss-spikes.card.md) — retains the MSE caveat in a survey phrase; [2405.17479](../2405.17479.a-rationale-from-frequency-perspective-for-grokking-in-training-neural-network/2405.17479.a-rationale-from-frequency-perspective-for-grokking-in-training-neural-network.card.md) — an exact paraphrase with an explicit "we do not require this transition".
