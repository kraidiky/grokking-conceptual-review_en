# Kazanskii 2026 — How to Tame Grokking: Representation Geometry as a Control Signal

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Maksim A. Kazanskii** (independent researcher; mkazanskii@gmail.com).

arXiv [2607.11666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11666), July 2026 (v1), 22 pages, 7 composite figures (22 files), 7 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. A single-author work: the geometry of the representations is claimed to be not a side effect of grokking but a control signal. The observation — a collapse of the effective dimension precedes grokking in every setting; the intervention — GeomDR, a spectral regulariser penalising the sum of the eigenvalues of the covariance of the hidden representations beyond the leading $d^{*}$ directions, switched on after an initial phase with a cosine warm-up. On addition and division mod 97 and on the composition of the permutations of $S_{5}$: an MLP is sped up by 23.5–52.5 times (362.1K → 6.9K steps on addition), a transformer by 1.48–2.01; the delay "collapse — grokking" is compressed from 264K to 2–3K steps; the dependence on the switch-on time is U-shaped; the spectrum can be estimated from 5–25 % of the representations; and combining it with Grokfast wrecks grokking altogether.

## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — a control of the time of the delayed generalisation by a direct intervention in the geometry: a spectral penalty on the tail of the covariance of the hidden representations, switched on after an initial free phase, speeds the attainment of the grokking criterion up by as much as 52.5 times on an MLP (362.1K → 6.9K steps) at a 100 % success rate, with a U-shaped dependence on the switch-on time ($t_{s}=2000$–$4000$ being the best; too early destroys the formation of the representations, too late leaves no time to have an effect) ([`p6-4`](#p6-4)). The analysis: there is no separate validation set (following Power et al.), so the best settings are selected on the very test set on which the speed-up is reported, and without an equal tuning budget for the baseline; the "up to 52 times" is counted from a baseline into which a failed run with an ascribed budget of 500K is folded — over the seeds that did grok successfully the baseline is 283.4K, that is, ~41×; and the internal disagreements are many (12.8K for the best permutations in table 1 against ≥149.3K in every row of table 2; $d^{*}=64$ as the best in the text against "$d^{*}\in\{16,32\}$ is faster and more stable" in the appendix; a baseline MLP of 333.0K in table 4 against 362.1K in table 1 on the same seeds).

**[The compression of representations](../../concepts/manifold-representation-compression.md)** — the collapse of the dimension as a harbinger and as a lever: the effective dimension (the participation ratio) of the deep layers collapses sharply before grokking (in the baseline the collapse is at ~15–25K steps and the grokking at ~250–500K — a gap of 264.3K steps), the unsmoothed curves show several sharp simultaneous rearrangements of $D_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and of the local neighbour distance $\rho$, and GeomDR compresses the "collapse — grokking" delay down to 2.2–3.0K steps, pulling the system towards the diagonal $t_{\mathrm{collapse}}=t_{\mathrm{grok}}$ ([`p5-4`](#p5-4)). The analysis: "the collapse precedes grokking in every setting" is measured quantitatively only for addition and the MLP, and the threshold "half of the initial dimension" is arbitrary and not checked for sensitivity; causality is not shown — GeomDR changes the training objective itself, and the speed-up may come from the added regularising pressure rather than from the dimension as such (there is no control with a penalty of the same strength on an unrelated quantity; the author admits this halfway); and the corpus's neighbouring works on dimension and geometry (2211.13239, 2605.06352, 2606.08985, 2606.32000) are not touched at all.

**[Variants of regularisation](../../concepts/regularization-variants.md)** — GeomDR as a new class: a penalty $L_{\mathrm{GeomDR}}=\sum_{j>d^{*}}\mu_{j}$ on the tail of the spectrum of the covariance of the activations (not of the weights), with three proved properties of the objective (a control of the effective rank, a bound on the low-rank approximation through Eckart–Young, and a contraction of the volume of the covariance ellipsoid — it is stated outright that these are properties of the definition rather than a proof of the speed-up); in the comparison the best weight decay gives 27.2K, GrokFast 372.1K, GeomDR 7.0K, while the combination GrokFast+GeomDR does not grok once (0/5) ([`p21-3`](#p21-3)). The analysis: "GrokFast improves on the baseline" — at 372.1K against a baseline of 362.1K only the success rate is improved (5/5 against 4/5); table 7 collapses rows with different numbers of runs (GeomDR 10/10, the rest 5 each); and the wrecking of the combination of two accelerators is the second such case in the corpus (after 2607.04333, where Grokfast on top of SupCon wrecked every seed) and deserves a separate check.

**[Spectral analysis](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — the spectrum of the covariance of the activations as the working axis: the measure is the participation ratio $D_{\mathrm{eff}}=(\sum\mu_{i})^{2}/\sum\mu_{i}^{2}$, the collapse threshold is 50 % of the initial value; the practical scalability — the spectrum is estimated from a random 5–25 % of the representations with almost no loss of the speed-up (a computational gain of 2.4–2.7×), whence the conjecture that it is compatible with mini-batches (not checked directly); the width ablation shows the limit — on the MLP GeomDR helps at all widths 32–256 (including the cases in which the baseline does not grok at all), whereas on the transformer at widths 64 and 128 it makes things worse (2/5 and 1/5 against the baseline's 5/5) ([`p18-4`](#p18-4)). The analysis: at a subsample of 1 % (28 examples at a dimension of 128) the sample covariance is degenerate by construction — the row is in the table, the discussion is not; the complexity $O(Nd^{2}+d^{3})$ per layer is honestly stated; and the text is unproofread (a missing "If" before a formula, "he Transformer", "$2$times", a sentence repeated word for word).

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong points — an intervention instead of an observation, and a limit of transferability admitted outright.** The geometry of the representations is made an object of direct intervention (unlike Omnigrok/Tickets/Grokfast/GrokTransfer, which turn the norm, the sparsity, the gradients and the embeddings); the control is confirmed directly — the curves diverge by the target dimension at a "broadly similar" accuracy; the size of the effect on the MLP is large and reproducible across grids of settings (connected regions of success rather than isolated points); the width ablation honestly shows where the technique breaks (the transformer at 64/128); the 5–25 % subsampling makes the technique practical; the code is open, and the use of an LLM for editing the text is disclosed.

**Selection on the test set, the counting of the baseline, and six internal disagreements.** There is no validation set, and the settings are selected on the test set; the "52×" rests on the ascribed budget of a failed run; there are six documented conflicts between the tables and the text (12.8K against ≥149.3K; $d^{*}=64$ against $\{16,32\}$; 2.3K against 3.7–3.9K for the collapse; 333.0K against 362.1K for the baseline; "4/6" with five seeds; "GrokFast improves" at a worse mean); and the causality of the dimension is not established — the theoretical appendix describes properties of the objective that hold for any data whatever.

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that the dimension of the representations causally controls grokking") — more precisely: a spectral penalty on the hidden representations speeds the attainment of the grokking criterion up by 1.5–52 times on three toy tasks; the magnitude depends strongly on the architecture and on the way the baseline is counted, and the corpus's neighbouring works on the dimension and the geometry of representations are not touched at all in a list of 26 entries.
