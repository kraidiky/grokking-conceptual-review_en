## Concepts covered

**[Effective theory / the statistical mechanics of learning](../../concepts/effective-theory-statistical-mechanics.md)** — the work **introduces the approach** into grokking research and sets its pattern: the dynamics of the embeddings is modelled not by a gradient descent on the true task loss but by a descent on an effective loss $\ell_{\rm eff}$ depending only on the representations and independent of the decoder ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)). The authors call this outright a step towards a "statistical physics of deep learning" relating the "microphysics" of a network's dynamics to the "thermodynamics" of its behaviour. What matters is what the work does not contain: the effective loss is not derived from the true one in the general case — appendix L derives it only for a linear regression in the adiabatic limit, and for a neural network it is postulated.

**[The Goldilocks zone](../../concepts/goldilocks-zone.md)** — the notion is **introduced here**. Representation learning proceeds only in an intermediate zone between memorisation and confusion ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)), and the authors liken it to "intelligence from hunger" in Darwinian evolution. In Liu et al. the zone is defined by the optimiser's hyperparameters (the learning rates of the encoder and the decoder, the weight decay) rather than by the weight norm: the carry-over into weight space is already Omnigrok, a later work by the same authors.

**[Structured representation learning](../../concepts/structured-representation-learning.md)** — the work gives the notion an **operational measure**. "Structure" is defined through $\delta$-parallelograms ($\mathbf{E}_ {i}+\mathbf{E}_ {j}=\mathbf{E}_ {m}+\mathbf{E}_ {n}$), and their fraction of all the admissible ones gives a representation quality index, RQI ([`p4-10`](#p4-4)). This is a case rare in the corpus in which the quality of a representation is not judged "by eye" from a PCA picture but computed as a number and set against the accuracy. The limitation the authors name themselves: in high dimensions the definition is too strict — a representation may be linear along one axis and arbitrary along the other $N-1$, giving $\text{RQI}\approx 0$ at a good generalisation.

**[Data fraction / critical dataset size](../../concepts/data-fraction-critical-dataset-size.md)** — the work gives the **first mechanistic explanation of the threshold**. The critical sample size is the smallest amount of data determining the representation uniquely: the threshold arises where the dimension of the kernel of the linear system $A(P)$ falls to 2 (a shift and a scale) and the representation becomes linear. The prediction is quantitative: a phase transition around $r_{c}=0.4$ for addition at $p=10$ and around $r_{c}=0.5$ for $S_{3}$ — and it is confirmed empirically ([`p6-1`](#p6-1)).

**[The learning rate](../../concepts/learning-rate.md)** — the work makes it an **axis of the phase diagram** rather than a mere hyperparameter. The key is not the absolute value but the *ratio* of the learning rates of the representation and of the decoder: a fast decoder with a slow representation gives memorisation, the converse gives comprehension, but too slow a decoder drags the generalisation out. It is out of this ratio that the four phases arise.

**[Phase transition](../../concepts/phase-transition.md)** — the work carries the notion over from physics in the literal sense: not a metaphor of a sharp jump but a phase diagram with boundaries between four regimes (comprehension, grokking, memorisation, confusion) defined by formal criteria in table 1 ([`sec-a`](#sec-a)). Hence a conclusion more important for the corpus than the diagram itself: grokking is not an inevitable property of a task but a **region in hyperparameter space**, wedged between comprehension and memorisation, and therefore removable by tuning.

**[The memorisation phase](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — acquires neighbours in the classification. Memorisation here is one of four phases, distinguished from grokking by a formal criterion (the training reaches 90 %, the validation does not) and from confusion by the training set having been memorised at all.

**[Neural collapse](../../concepts/neural-collapse.md)** — the work relates its effective theory to it in appendix I: the same loss reproduces the contraction of the representations of one class towards the class mean. But the authors themselves note a limit: the theory does **not** give an equiangular tight frame between the class means, and they merely conjecture that an explicit repulsion between the classes would help (an analogy with the Thomson problem).

**[Freezing a subnetwork / the lottery ticket](../../concepts/freezing-subnetwork.md)** — appendix K gives a **non-standard reading of the hypothesis**: the winning ticket here is neither weights nor a subnetwork but axes — the projection of the embeddings at initialisation onto the principal components of the already trained embeddings contains most of the future structure ([`sec-k`](#sec-k)). Training, on such a view, prunes the superfluous dimensions rather than creating structure from scratch. This is an observation rather than a tested hypothesis: the authors do not check it by an ablation.

**[The emergence of features / feature learning](../../concepts/feature-emergence-feature-learning.md)** — the work gives an observation that has become a mainstay of the corpus: the generalisation **coincides in time** with the appearance of structure in the embeddings ([`p2-1`](#fig-1)), and in a transformer the structure is visible to a plain PCA only by the moment of generalisation. The authors honestly caveat that no dimensionality-reduction method guarantees the detection of structure, so that "there is no structure" and "no structure was found" are not distinguishable here.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the work takes the task from Power et al. and **reduces it to a minimum**: instead of a transformer, the sum of two learnable embeddings fed into an MLP decoder, $(a,b)\mapsto{\rm Dec}(\mathbf{E}_ {a}+\mathbf{E}_ {b})$. Appendix B justifies that this suffices for all abelian groups, and appendix H extends the construction to non-abelian ones through matrix embeddings and multiplication.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the work is the first to show grokking **outside algorithmic data**: on MNIST, with the sample reduced to 1 thousand examples and the initialisation scale increased ([`sec-j`](#sec-j)). This moved the phenomenon out of the category of a curiosity of algorithmic tasks into a general property of learning — together with the conclusion that a sufficient regularisation (weight decay, dropout) removes the delay.

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the source of the task and of the observation explained here; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — independently analyses the same task from inside the network and arrives at a Fourier structure where a parallelogram is described here; [Varma et al. 2023](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — explains the same threshold through circuit efficiency, that is, gives a second, independent derivation of the critical sample size.

**[The role of gradient noise](../../concepts/gradient-noise.md)** — the phase diagrams of [appendix G](#sec-g) give an early data point against noise mechanisms: a large batch size (at a small learning rate) leads to comprehension, while a smaller one is apparently harmful — that is, mini-batch noise in this setting hinders rather than helps.

**[The phase diagram](../../concepts/phase-diagram.md)** — the work introduces the device itself: a macroscopic analysis through a map of quality over the hyperparameters ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)). The critical training-set size within this frame is not a separate phenomenon but the coordinate of a boundary on the diagram ([`p1-6`](#p1-6)).

**[The phases: comprehension, grokking, memorisation, confusion](../../concepts/comprehension-confusion-phases.md)** — the quartet itself originates here: the plane of settings is divided into comprehension, grokking, memorisation and confusion ([`p7-2`](#p7-2)). This is a labelling of hyperparameter space rather than of the course of training, and it must not be conflated with the three-part labelling of time.

**[Sparse solutions and hidden progress](../../concepts/sparse-solutions-hidden-progress.md)** — the work also shows the other side of hidden progress: it is not visible to every measure, and at the early steps neither PCA nor t-SNE finds any structure in the embeddings ([`p8-8`](#p8-8)). The absence of structure in a projection, then, is no argument against its presence.

**[Selective weight decay](../../concepts/selective-weight-decay.md)** — the work notes its difference from the original setting outright: the weight decay is applied only to the decoder and not to the embedding layer ([`p8-8`](#p8-8)). The caveat matters, because the conclusion about the usefulness of the penalty is obtained precisely under that split and on a transformer, whereas in the toy model the sign of the effect is different.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is markedly uneven: some of the results are derived before the experiment and confirmed, some rest on postulates honestly called postulates, and some are carried beyond what was checked.

**The strong part is the prediction of the threshold.** The critical sample size is derived from a consideration about the dimension of the kernel of a linear system: while there are few data the representation is under-determined; as soon as two degrees of freedom remain (a shift and a scale) the representation must become linear. Hence a phase transition around $r_{c}=0.4$ for addition at $p=10$ is obtained **before the experiment**, and it is confirmed ([`p6-1`](#p6-1)). For $S_{3}$ the same method gives $r_{c}=0.5$. This is a case rare for the corpus in which a threshold is not measured but computed.

**The effective theory is a postulate, not a derivation.** The formulation "we shall assume that the normalised embedding vectors obey a gradient flow" ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)) stands in the very definition. Appendix L derives the same loss from a true MSE loss, but under conditions that hold in none of the paper's experiments: a linear decoder, $\mathbf{r}^{\prime}=-\mathbf{r}$, the adiabatic limit $\eta_{A}\to 0$ and $\mathbf{A}_ {0}^{T}\mathbf{A}_ {0}\approx\mathbf{I}$. In the experiments the decoder is an MLP or a transformer, and the learning rates of the encoder and the decoder are swept within one range $[10^{-5},10^{-2}]$, that is, they are comparable. Appendix L should be read as a motivation for the form of the loss rather than as its justification for the system under study.

**The RQI is checked where its limitation does not bite.** The agreement between the predicted and the measured accuracy is obtained with one-dimensional embeddings. In appendix D the authors write themselves that in high dimensions the definition is too strict: a representation may be linear along one axis and arbitrary along the other $N-1$, giving $\text{RQI}\approx 0$ at a good generalisation — and that they do not expect a trivial carry-over. On $S_{3}$ this is already visible: the predicted accuracy turns out to be a **lower** bound on the measured one, whence the authors conclude that "there must exist some mechanism of generalisation besides the RQI". The conclusion of section 3 meanwhile remains stated without that caveat.

**What exactly is shown about the structure.** It is measured that the accuracy is predictable from the representation; the direction of causality is not measured — there is no intervention imposing structure in the work. It is worth remembering separately the authors' own caveat: neither PCA nor t-SNE guarantees the detection of structure, so that "there is no structure" and "no structure was found by the chosen method" are not distinguishable in this setting.

**The grokking on MNIST is obtained by tuning rather than found.** The sample is truncated from 50 thousand to 1 thousand examples and the initialisation scale is increased ([`sec-j`](#sec-j)). This shows that grokking is **inducible** outside algorithmic data — a weaker statement than "occurs on ordinary data", and it should be cited precisely so.

**An open question noted by the authors.** The decoder's weight decay acts oppositely in two settings: in the toy model it leads into memorisation or confusion, and on the transformer it speeds the generalisation up. The authors call this a difference, explain it by a hypothesis about the dimension of the embeddings and write that the question requires study. This is not a contradiction within the paper, but neither is it a settled question — and weight decay here is one of the two principal control parameters.

**"Alexander's principle".** The upper bounds on the RQI and the accuracy (equations 16–17) are derived from a proposition the authors themselves call "not a rigorous argument but a belief": that networks are capable only of reproducing what the data specify. Formal inequalities resting on a declared belief are a rare honesty, but when citing the bounds one should not forget their foundation.

## Common misreadings and overstatements of this paper

Patterns of erroneous or over-extended citation of this work observed in the citing papers of the corpus. Each entry describes what exactly is distorted in the paraphrase and leads to the authors' own paragraphs, where the lost caveat stands. The analysis of particular formulations is in the citing works' cards, in their "Erroneous citations" sections, to which the "details" links lead. The main source of errors is a conflation with Omnigrok: a follow-up work by the same group appeared six months later, and the features of the two works get mixed up in citation.

**A weight-norm Goldilocks zone ascribed to this work (erroneous).** The Goldilocks zone is introduced here [in hyperparameter space — learning rates and weight decay, between memorisation and confusion](#p1-2); the version of the zone in weight-norm space (a spherical shell) is Omnigrok. Paraphrases such as "a Goldilocks zone of weight norms (Liu et al., 2022)" build a chimera: the phase terminology from here, the weight zone from another work; and such a chimera inherits into the bargain the objection of [Miller et al.](../2310.17247.grokking-beyond-neural-networks-an-empirical-exploration-with-model-complexity/2310.17247.grokking-beyond-neural-networks-an-empirical-exploration-with-model-complexity.card.md) about sphericity, addressed to Omnigrok. The extreme case is a systematic bibliography error: in one paper all the Omnigrok material (the l2 analysis, the MNIST setup) is hung on this work because Omnigrok is absent from its reference list. The details: [2604.13123](../2604.13123.spectral-entropy-collapse-as-a-phase-transition-in-delayed-generalisation/2604.13123.spectral-entropy-collapse-as-a-phase-transition-in-delayed-generalisation.card.md#mc-2205-10343), [2510.04930](../2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking/2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking.card.md#mc-2205-10343), [2602.02859](../2602.02859.late-stage-generalization-collapse-in-grokking-detecting-anti-grokking-with-weightwatcher/2602.02859.late-stage-generalization-collapse-in-grokking-detecting-anti-grokking-with-weightwatcher.card.md#mc-2205-10343), [2405.17479](../2405.17479.a-rationale-from-frequency-perspective-for-grokking-in-training-neural-network/2405.17479.a-rationale-from-frequency-perspective-for-grokking-in-training-neural-network.card.md#mc-2205-10343).

**Loss of the hedge about the causality of the structure (an over-extension).** The authors' formula is "good evidence" that generalisation is related to the structure of the representations; causality is not established by intervention, and the representation-quality measure is checked in a narrow setting. Paraphrases such as "shows that structured representations lead to generalization" present evidence as a demonstrated mechanism. Milder echoes: [2509.20829](../2509.20829.explaining-grokking-and-information-bottleneck-through-neural-collapse-emergence/2509.20829.explaining-grokking-and-information-bottleneck-through-neural-collapse-emergence.card.md), [2506.21551](../2506.21551.grokking-in-llm-pretraining-monitor-memorization-to-generalization-without-test/2506.21551.grokking-in-llm-pretraining-monitor-memorization-to-generalization-without-test.card.md).

**An erasure of priorities (erroneous, mild).** Two things introduced here migrate to other works: [the first demonstration of grokking outside algorithmic data — MNIST, section 4.3](#p9-2) — goes to Omnigrok (only [2601.03162](../2601.03162.on-the-convergence-behavior-of-preconditioned-gradient-descent-toward-the-rich-learning-regime/2601.03162.on-the-convergence-behavior-of-preconditioned-gradient-descent-toward-the-rich-learning-regime.card.md) explicitly returns it to the pair of works); and [the critical sample size, predicted by the theory before the experiment](#p6-1), is attributed to Varma, Zhu or Omnigrok. The details: [2504.03162](../2504.03162.beyond-progress-measures-theoretical-insights-into-the-mechanism-of-grokking/2504.03162.beyond-progress-measures-theoretical-insights-into-the-mechanism-of-grokking.card.md#mc-2205-10343); milder echoes: [2511.04760](../2511.04760.when-data-falls-short-grokking-below-the-critical-threshold/2511.04760.when-data-falls-short-grokking-below-the-critical-threshold.card.md), [2301.05217](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — a pointed "echoing the results on toy models" for a result shown on a transformer.

Examples of careful citation: [Varma et al.](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — the result plus an explicit limit ("such a theory does not explain semi-grokking"); [2306.13253](../2306.13253.predicting-grokking-long-before-it-happens/2306.13253.predicting-grokking-long-before-it-happens.card.md) — the most detailed correct paraphrase, preserving the taxonomy of phases; [2511.04760](../2511.04760.when-data-falls-short-grokking-below-the-critical-threshold/2511.04760.when-data-falls-short-grokking-below-the-critical-threshold.card.md) — the only accurate formula of the Goldilocks zone ("between memorization and confusion", with no weights); [2601.03162](../2601.03162.on-the-convergence-behavior-of-preconditioned-gradient-descent-toward-the-rich-learning-regime/2601.03162.on-the-convergence-behavior-of-preconditioned-gradient-descent-toward-the-rich-learning-regime.card.md) — a correct MNIST priority.
