## Concepts covered

**[Kolmogorov complexity](../../concepts/kolmogorov-complexity.md)** — the work proposes the LMN as a **neural-network counterpart** of Kolmogorov complexity: a quantity that, unlike the $L_{2}$ norm, reads naturally as an amount of information — "if we compress a network into input-dependent linear maps, the compressed information is the LMN times the size of a single map" ([`p2-1`](#p2-1)). The justification of this particular form is direct: modern networks are precisely a gluing together of local linear computations, and a measure ought to count those rather than the length of the weight vector.

**[The compression of representations](../../concepts/manifold-representation-compression.md)** — the thesis "grokking is compression" is called here the common denominator of the corpus's **already existing** explanations: all of them have a memorising solution that is learned earlier and a generalising solution that is more efficient ([`p1-3`](#p1-3)). The contribution of the work lies not in the thesis but in the quantity by which the compression is measured: the LMN decreases steadily between the memorisation and the generalisation ([`fig-2`](#fig-2)).

**[Weight norm minimisation](../../concepts/weight-norm-minimization.md)** — a direct objection to $L_{2}$ as a measure of complexity, and a constructive one. The telling argument: a deep **linear** network represents only a linear map, and the LMN gives 1 for it, whereas $L_{2}$ can be anything whatever ([`p2-1`](#p2-1)). The empirical argument: in the compression phase the LMN is related to the test loss almost linearly, while $L_{2}$ is related in a complicated non-linear way ([`p3-8`](#p3-8)).

**[Spectral analysis](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — the method of counting: a linear-connectivity matrix $\mathbf{L}$ is built from the $r^{2}$ of a linear regression of the output curve between pairs of inputs, and the number of clusters in it is estimated softly — through the von Neumann entropy of the normalised eigenvalues, $\mathrm{LMN}=2^{S_{\mathrm{NL}}}$ ([`p3-3`](#p3-3)). A discrete count of clusters is replaced by a spectral quantity precisely for the sake of robustness.

**[The memorisation phase](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — the training is divided into three phases by operational points: memorisation (up to 100 % on the training set), generalisation (up to 100 % on the test set) and **refinement** — the remainder ([`p3-8`](#p3-8)). The third phase is not decorative here: it is precisely there that an unexpected behaviour of the LMN on XOR is discovered.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** and **[groups](../../concepts/group-composition-non-commutative-s5.md)** — the test tasks: addition modulo 31, the composition of permutations in $S_{4}$ and a bitwise XOR of five bits; three-layer fully connected networks with SiLU activations, AdamW, an 80–20 split ([`p3-7`](#p3-7)). Note the difference from the corpus's customary set: it is not a transformer and not $p=113$.

**[Sparse parity](../../concepts/sparse-parity.md)** — the bitwise XOR yields the work's one observation that nobody predicted: after the grokking the LMN does not level off but **rises and falls again** ([`p4-1`](#p4-1)). The explanation offered is arithmetical: the turning points 15 and 20 are $5\times 3$ and $5\times 4$, that is, three or four memorised patterns for each of the five bits, a symmetric and an asymmetric handling of the pairs $(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the notion acquires a measurable "hidden progress": between the memorisation and the generalisation the network changes nothing outwardly, yet the LMN decreases ([`p3-8`](#p3-8)). This places the work alongside the progress measures of Nanda et al., but with a quantity not tied to the task.

**[Double descent](../../concepts/double-descent.md)** — mentioned twice and in different senses: as somebody else's line of explanations of grokking (Davies et al.) and as the shape of the LMN curve on XOR (a "double descent"). The second is a coincidence of shape, not the same phenomenon; the work does not say so.

**[Circuit efficiency](../../concepts/circuit-efficiency.md)** — the network efficiency of Varma et al. is named among the quantities describing compression and is assigned to the "indirect proxies" that admit no direct informational reading ([`p1-3`](#p1-3)).

It is worth saying separately what the work does **not** contain. The LMN is computed on a particular set of inputs, and its value depends on the choice of that set and on which layers it is measured between: the authors write outright that only the first hidden layer is meaningful, while the embeddings and the second layer show nothing ([`p7-1`](#p7-1)). There is no check on other architectures and tasks — that is named as a plan. Nor is there an intervention: the LMN measures here but changes nothing, so the claim "grokking is compression" is supported by a correspondence of curves.

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the task; [Liu et al. 2022](../2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning/2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning.card.md) and [Omnigrok](../2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data/2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data.card.md) — works by the same first author, whose weight norm this work contests; [Varma et al. 2023](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — circuit efficiency as one more proxy; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — progress measures tied to the task; [DeMoss et al. 2024](../2412.09810.the-complexity-dynamics-of-grokking/2412.09810.the-complexity-dynamics-of-grokking.card.md) — a year later, the same idea of complexity as the key quantity, but estimated by file compression rather than by a number of linear maps.

**[The complexity–error trade-off](../../concepts/model-complexity-error-tradeoff.md)** — the work gives the trade-off a measurable coordinate: the *number of linear maps* (LMN), which generalises the counting of the linear regions of a ReLU network ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)). Grokking then reads as a transition to a solution of lower complexity at the same error.

**[The information bottleneck](../../concepts/information-bottleneck.md)** — the frame is drawn on as a neighbouring one, with its caveat preserved: it presupposes a compression phase after a fitting phase, *although the compression story is sensitive to technical details* ([`p5-1`](#p5-1)). The place of an uncomputable information measure is taken by a computable measure of complexity ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)).
## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is short (eight pages including the appendices) and declared preliminary; it is worth reading as a proposal of a quantity rather than as an explanation.

**The quantity is defined carefully and is checkable.** The LMN is not postulated but derived: the linear connectivity of two inputs is measured through the $r^{2}$ of the output curve, the connectivity matrix is reduced to a number through the von Neumann entropy, and the definition is checked against the limiting case — $c$ equal clusters give exactly $\mathrm{LMN}=c$ ([`p3-5`](#p3-5)). For any linear or affine function $S_{\mathrm{NL}}=0$. This is more than is usually offered when a new metric is introduced.

**The main observation is a correspondence of curves.** The LMN decreases between the memorisation and the generalisation on all three tasks ([`fig-2`](#fig-2)), and decreases more linearly with respect to the test loss than $L_{2}$ does. But "more linearly" is nowhere expressed as a number: the work gives neither a correlation coefficient nor an $R^{2}$ for comparing the two quantities — even though the LMN itself is defined through $r^{2}$.

**The argument against $L_{2}$ is stronger than the empirics.** The example of a deep linear network ([`p2-1`](#p2-1)) is a conceptual counterexample, and a convincing one: $L_{2}$ can grow while the computed function stays unchanged. It depends on neither the task nor the split, and it alone suffices to count $L_{2}$ a proxy rather than a measure.

**The XOR finding is an honestly presented conjecture.** The double descent of the LMN after the generalisation is called previously undescribed; the explanation offered (a symmetric and an asymmetric handling of the pairs of bits) rests on the coincidence of the turning points with $5\times 3$ and $5\times 4$ ([`p4-1`](#p4-1)), and the authors explicitly leave the mechanistic check for the future. The coincidence of the numbers is elegant, but it is one observation on one task.

**The dependence on the choice of layer is admitted.** The LMN is meaningful only between the first hidden layer and the output: the embeddings have not yet been processed by the network, and the second hidden layer almost repeats the output logits ([`p7-1`](#p7-1)). The quantity, then, is not a property of the whole network but of a chosen slice — and the choice is made by the result.

**The claim of a "neural-network Kolmogorov complexity" remains a claim.** The argument is that the LMN counts local linear computations and is therefore closer to information than the weight norm. The work gives neither generalisation bounds through the LMN nor a comparison with complexity estimates by compression; a check on a broad range of tasks is named as a plan.

**Its place in the corpus.** Two works in the corpus approach grokking from the side of complexity: this one and DeMoss et al. The difference is instructive. Here the complexity is computed from the **function** — how many different linear maps the network realises on the data — and does not depend on how the weights are written down. There the complexity is computed from the **description** — the size of the compressed file of coarsened weights — and therefore depends on the coarsening procedure and the archiver. Both measures give the same picture: a rise during the memorisation, a fall during the generalisation. That two independent formalisations of "simplicity" agree is an argument for the thesis "grokking is compression" itself; a comparison between the works is not yet in the corpus, since they appeared a year apart and do not cite one another.
