# Ootani 2026 — Grokking Is Conditional and Fragile: A Fully-Tractable, Multi-Seed Study at 12K Parameters

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Yoshiyuki Ootani** (independent researcher; info@ootanl.com).

arXiv [2607.05104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05104), July 2026 (v1), 14 pages, 7 figures, 4 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. A single-author work: grokking is measured as a multi-seed rate (a grok-rate) rather than as a single outcome, on the fully enumerable transformer Glimmer-1-Base of 11 856 parameters (a hidden dimension of 16, two layers) fine-tuned on modular arithmetic with $M\leq 10$. Six findings: the coverage threshold follows the cardinality of the output (the modulus) more strongly than the structure of the composition; the inverse U of Omnigrok in weight decay (20 %→90 %→0 %) is reproduced at 12K parameters; a numerical blade — the number of CPU threads (a pure change of the order of the reduction) and CPU-against-GPU flip 49/300 and 19/100 seeds without shifting the aggregate rate (McNemar $p\geq 0.39$); the dim-16 model does NOT form the textbook Fourier circle of embeddings; a decomposition into specialists wins by coverage rather than by supervision (10/10 against 0/10 at an equal budget, the scratchpad monolith also 0/10); and three of the author's own single-run stories are refuted by a multi-seed control.

## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — a change of the unit of evidence: not "the model groks task X" but a grok-rate over ten or more seeds within a fixed numerical setting, with pairing by seed in the comparisons; a task's rate is not constant — the same $(a{+}b)\bmod 10$ groks 2–3 times out of 10 at one weight decay and 9 out of 10 at another, and three dramatic single-run stories ("a capability wall", "the threads switch the grokking", "the GPU suppresses the grokking") turned out to be seed sampling ([`p11-2`](#p11-2)). The analysis: "grokking" here is operationalised by a threshold of 0.70 on the best held-out accuracy — that is a measure of generalisation with no delay time in it, and by the paper's own figure 1 the training accuracy leads the held-out one "by a few tenths at most", both cross-entropy curves sit on a plateau of $\ln M$ and collapse together — the phenomenon is closer to a delayed learning than to the grokking of Power et al. with its long memorisation phase; and the setting is non-standard in another way too — it is a fine-tuning of a pre-trained checkpoint rather than a training from scratch.

**[Numerical stability](../../concepts/numerical-stability-fix.md)** — a numerical blade of a new kind: changing the number of CPU threads (a pure permutation of the summation order under the non-associativity of floating-point addition; 4 and 16 threads give bit-identical weights, so the pure perturbation is 1 against 4) flips the outcome in 49 of 300 paired seeds (a mean absolute shift of the best accuracy of 0.17, a maximum of 0.85), and CPU against GPU with TF32 disabled flips 19 of 100, and neither perturbation shifts the aggregate rate (McNemar $p=0.39$/$1.0$, Newcombe intervals within $\pm 10$ points) ([`p8-1`](#p8-1)). The analysis: there is an explicit difference from the Softmax Collapse of Prieto et al. — there a numerical failure systematically suppresses grokking, whereas here the perturbation permutes *which* seeds generalise without changing *how many*; the reading "the generalising basin is so shallow that sub-ULP differences decide the entry" is declared a reading rather than a measurement; and the flip rate is propped up by the 0.70 threshold — the distribution of the shifts in the seeds that did not flip is not shown, and within 16 % the seeds that stood at the threshold are not separable.

**[Data fraction / critical size](../../concepts/data-fraction-critical-dataset-size.md)** — the coverage threshold is sharp, almost all-or-nothing, and its position follows the cardinality of the output: the composite mod-7 and mod-9 tasks cross at 5–10 % coverage, while both mod-10 tasks (a sum of products and a product of sums) cross in the single band 20–40 %; the ordering by $M$ is reproduced on a domain ten times smaller, and a $2\times 2$ crossing at $n=2000$ gives 60–70 points for the modulus against $\leq$10 for the structure; a "task too hard" turns out to be a coverage threshold rather than a capacity limit ([`p5-4`](#p5-4)). The analysis: the ordering table is computed on 3–5 seeds per cell — against the author's own protocol of "ten or more"; the ordering is clean only well above the threshold (at 30 % coverage it is non-monotone: $M=5$ gives 0/10, $M=6$ gives 10/10 because of the structure of the residues at the divisors of ten); the difference by structure of $\leq$10 points is itself admitted to be noise at ten seeds; and the decomposition into specialists wins by the same mechanism, read as a memorisation of dense subdomains (the adder: 0.97 on the seen pairs against 0.10 on the unseen).

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — the inverse U of Omnigrok is reproduced on a model two to three orders of magnitude smaller than the original: a grok-rate of 20 % at a decay of 0, 27 % at 0.01, 90 % at 0.1 and 0 % at 1.0 (mean best accuracies of 0.42/0.40/0.82/0.02; $(a{+}b)\bmod 10$, a coverage of 80 %, ten seeds each) — a positive control showing that the rate measurement responds to a directed intervention, which licenses the reading of the blade's null results as genuinely null ([`p7-2`](#p7-2)). The analysis: the corollary that matters for the corpus is that every "the model groks task X with probability p" is implicitly conditional on the regularisation; the control reproduces a qualitative dependence rather than a law; and at a threshold of 0.80 the rates read 20/17/70/0 %, so the conclusion is robust to the threshold.

**[Seed variance and reproducibility](../../concepts/seed-variance-reproducibility.md)** — the conclusion is stated as sharply as it can be: *"single runs lie in this regime, systematically"* ([`p11-2`](#p11-2)). Hence the design of the work — a multi-seed analysis on a model of 12 thousand parameters, where a full enumeration is still available.

**[Negative results](../../concepts/negative-results.md)** — the work presents a negative result as a contribution in its own right rather than as a caveat ([`p10-1`](#p10-1)). The threshold of significance is the same seed variance, without which "no effect is visible" is indistinguishable from an unlucky draw.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong points — the protocol and the refutation of the author's own stories.** The unit of evidence is a grok-rate at fixed threads and architecture, with pairing by seed and the thresholds 0.60/0.70/0.80 recomputed for every rate (the objection of Schaeffer et al. about a mirage is taken seriously); the numerical blade is backed by power (300 and 100 paired seeds) and shown on two independent perturbations with a bit-level check of the equivalence of 4 and 16 threads; three single-run stories are dissected on the author's own data as seed sampling; and the use of AI agents in running the experiments and writing the text is disclosed honestly.

**What is observed is not quite grokking, and some of the numbers are subtler than the headline.** There is a plateau and a sharp transition with no long memorisation phase (admitted in the text against the author's own abstract); "conditional and fragile" is a statement about the variance at an unchanged rate; the ordering by cardinality rests on 3–5-seed cells plus one ten-seed shift; "the pipeline groks 10/10" counts a composite accuracy secured by memorising subdomains (as the author reads it himself); the mechanistic positive result (the periodicity of the logits) is partly definitional and admitted as such — only the negative result about the embeddings is independent, and its force is limited by the setting (7–10 embedding points, a dimension of 16, and weights inherited from the pre-training).

**How the work will be cited** ("the number of threads and the choice of CPU/GPU switch grokking"; "a decomposition groks where a monolith cannot") — more precisely: at an unchanged aggregate rate it is the membership of the individual seeds that flips — a well-powered null result for the mean rate; and the pipeline at an equal budget attains a high composite accuracy through a dense coverage of the subdomains, with the mechanism of the success read by the author himself as memorisation.
