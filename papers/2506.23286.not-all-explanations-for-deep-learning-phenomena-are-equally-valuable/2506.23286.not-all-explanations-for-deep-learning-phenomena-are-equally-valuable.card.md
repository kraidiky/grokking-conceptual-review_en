## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the work does not study grokking but takes it as one of three showcases of an "edge case" and passes judgement on it from outside. It gives the corpus four things. The first is an explicitly declared position: grokking is included in the analysis because it captured the community's interest "while not being a practical concern in the training of frontier models" ([p. 1, para. 5](#p1-5)). The second is a summary of the descriptive features that had accumulated in the literature by 2025 and are not part of the original definition: a larger gap in time between strong training and test performance (Liu et al. 2022a); a near-perfect training performance rather than stagnation at some arbitrary level (Charton 2024); a sharper jump of the test performance "at least under a certain measure, such as accuracy" (Nanda et al. 2023) — with the authors stating outright that the definition of Power et al. 2022 is itself "loose" ([p. 3, para. 4](#p3-4)). The third is a list, gathered into a single paragraph, of the arguments for practical insignificance: small algorithmic datasets, a weakening of the effect as the dataset grows, the failure of attempts to see ordinary grokking in plausible scenarios, its inducement by an inflated initialisation and by hidden encodings, and the dependence of its spectacle on the choice of measure ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)). The fourth is an equally gathered list of what grokking has given the wider field ([p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2)). What the work does not contain: any search of its own for grokking in applications — the argument for insignificance rests entirely on a survey of others' works and on the absence of countervailing reports; any new definition or measure; and not one specific published hypothesis about grokking is named as a narrow ad hoc hypothesis — the negative example on which the critique rests the authors manufactured themselves ([fig. 4](#fig-4)). Figure 2 is a drawn schematic rather than a measurement ([fig. 2](#fig-2)).

**[Double descent](../../concepts/double-descent.md)** — the first of the three phenomena analysed and the only one for which the work sets out both a prehistory and a debunking. The prehistory: the U-shaped curve of test error in capacity and the unexpected second descent as the *number of parameters* grows further (Belkin et al. 2019, with a pointer to earlier observations in Loog et al. 2020) ([p. 3, para. 1](#p3-1), [fig. 1](#fig-1)). The debunking consists of two moves: the number of parameters may be a poor measure of complexity in nonlinear models (Curth et al. 2024b), and under a suitable regularisation — including an implicit one, through early stopping and SGD — the effect is no longer to be expected (Nakkiran et al. 2021; Hastie et al. 2022), which is what explains its absence from empirical scaling analyses of language and vision models ([p. 3, para. 2](#p3-2)). The positive list: layerwise learning-rate heuristics, a complexity measure separating training from test complexity, a framework for measuring the generalisation gap, and a rethinking of the role of memorisation, of the underdetermination of the solution, of benign overfitting and of the bias–variance trade-off ([p. 3, para. 3](#p3-3)). Double descent moreover serves in the paper as the only developed example of how an edge phenomenon ought to refine a broad theory: the discordant observation strikes at the bias–variance trade-off, and it is that theory, rather than the phenomenon itself, that is to be refined ([p. 6, para. 2](#p6-2)). What the work does not contain: a measurement of double descent of its own — figure 1 is drawn by hand, and the left half of figure 4 is a reproduction of someone else's setting (MNIST-1D after Greydanus & Kobak 2024).

**[The sparse subnetwork / the lottery ticket](../../concepts/sparse-subnetwork-lottery-ticket.md)** — the third phenomenon analysed, adduced as the cleanest example of an "edge case with useful consequences". The prehistory is given after Frankle & Carbin 2019, with a caveat that the hypothesis, by the empirical and theoretical evidence accumulated, holds up ([p. 4, para. 3](#p4-3)). The practical insignificance is derived not from the absence of the phenomenon but from the impossibility of exploiting it: a ticket is found only by training a dense network, pruning after training and rewinding to the initialisation; the approach is sensitive to hyperparameters, and the gain from sparsity may be unrealisable on present-day hardware. Inserted here too is the self-assessment of the hypothesis's author: Jonathan Frankle says he does not consider lottery tickets the most interesting problem, but that much follows from that paper about "how we do science" ([p. 4, para. 4](#p4-4)). The positive list: an influence on pruning techniques, on quantisation, on parameter-efficient fine-tuning and on vision and language models ([p. 5, para. 1](#p5-1)). What the work does not contain: experiments of its own ([fig. 3](#fig-3) is a schematic) and any relation of the lottery ticket to grokking, which the corpus pursues as a separate line; in this work the three phenomena stand side by side but are not brought together.

**[Emergence / emergent abilities](../../concepts/emergence.md)** — the work only mentions the notion, but in two places and in two roles. In the argument against grokking: the observation of Kumar et al. 2023 that the spectacle of the effect rests on the choice of measure is called an echo of a similar argument about the emergent abilities of large language models (Schaeffer et al. 2024) ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)). In the conclusion: the distinction between "genuinely concerning behaviour" and "contrived artefacts" in emergent abilities (Berti et al. 2025) is called the same kind of assessment of practical significance as that made in the paper itself ([p. 9, para. 4](#p9-4)). What the work does not contain: any analysis of emergence of its own and any measurements whatever on language models.

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — the work passes a positive verdict on the notion and makes it grokking's chief bequest to the wider field: grokking "suggests that if we observe surprising qualitative jumps over the course of training, then we need to revisit and improve our internal progress measures of performance so as better to capture the true, smooth, underlying improvement" (with a reference to Nanda et al. 2023) ([p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2)). This is the only place where the paper says what exactly grokking has taught the field rather than what it has done for it. What the work does not contain: not a single progress measure — neither introduced nor applied; on its own figure the test accuracy and the number of prime parameters are plotted, and the second quantity is designed from the outset to be worthless.

**[The initialisation scale](../../concepts/initialization-scale.md)** — a load-bearing detail of the argument for grokking's practical insignificance. Grokking on other kinds of data is declared synthetic precisely because it "is done synthetically, by a heavily inflated scale of the network's initial parameter values" (Liu et al. 2022b) ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)). The same scale operates in the authors' own experiment, but from the other side: in debunking their parody measure they explain its behaviour by most of the parameters being initialised as $Unif(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{\text{num params}}},\frac{1}{\sqrt{\text{num params}}})$, which rounds to a non-prime and grows only later ([p. 14, para. 3](#p14-3)); "initialisation scale" also stands in the questionnaire of table 1 among the confounding factors to be controlled ([appendix A](#sec-a)). What the work does not contain: any sweep of the scale or any measurement of it whatever — the quantity is taken from others' works and from the defaults of third-party code.

**[The canonical grokking proving ground](../../concepts/canonical-grokking-testbed.md)** — the work uses the proving ground twice and in different ways. It reproduces it for its own experiment: "the grokking experiment — the setting of Power et al. 2022 based on the implementation at https://github.com/teddykoker/grokking", averaged over 10 seeds ([p. 14, para. 3](#p14-3)). And it advances an argument in defence of the proving ground itself as a research area: the phenomena of deep learning "are by design isolated in their simplest setting" and are tractable at the most modest resources, which gives the resource-poor an opportunity to contribute ([p. 8, para. 4](#p8-4)). What the work does not contain: not one parameter of the setting — neither the operation, nor the modulus, nor the fraction of training data, nor the optimiser, nor the number of steps; the run cannot be checked against others', and there is no loss plot for comparison with the accuracy at all ([fig. 4](#fig-4)).

**[Real data and vision](../../concepts/vision-real-world-data-mnist-cifar.md)** — the kind of data serves here as the dividing line of the argument. The work asserts that grokking "can technically be induced on other kinds of data too", but only synthetically, and that "any known studies *not* using this mechanism report standard generalisation" ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)); the absence of double descent from the scaling analyses of vision transformers (Zhai et al. 2022) is adduced as a parallel ([p. 3, para. 2](#p3-2)). The work's own experiment with real data is a single one — the left half of figure 4 on MNIST-1D after Greydanus & Kobak 2024 — and it is about double descent rather than grokking ([p. 14, para. 3](#p14-3)). What the work does not contain: any check of its own of the claim about other kinds of data, or any account of the corpus's works showing delayed transitions in vision and language settings at an ordinary initialisation.

**[The lazy→rich transition](../../concepts/lazy-to-rich-kernel-to-feature-learning.md)** — a mention only, in the positive list: "This prompted a more holistic assessment of the training dynamics through several points of view, including *the transition from lazy learning to feature learning* (Kumar et al. 2023; Lyu et al. 2024)" ([p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2)). The mechanism of the transition is not set out, and elsewhere in the paper it serves as an argument against the phenomenon — the same work of Kumar et al. is cited as having shown that the spectacle of the effect rests on the choice of measure ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)).

**[The slingshot effect](../../concepts/slingshot.md)** — a mention only, in the same list: "*slingshots in the parameter norm* (Thilak et al. 2024)" ([p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2)). The work analyses neither the mechanism, nor the optimisers, nor the loss spikes.

**[Structured representation learning](../../concepts/structured-representation-learning.md)** — a mention only, closing that same list: "*representation learning* (Liu et al. 2022a)" ([p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2)). Remarkably, the same work of Liu et al. 2022a is cited in the paper three times and in three different roles — as the source of the feature "a larger gap in time", as evidence of the effect's weakening as the dataset grows, and as a point of view on the training dynamics.

**[StableMax / perp-Grad](../../concepts/numerical-stability-fix.md)** — the work adduces this line as a model of what it wants from the study of phenomena: "On the methodological side, grokking prompted Prieto et al. 2025 to attend to the numerical instabilities of the *Softmax* function and to develop a more stable replacement" ([p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2)). This is the paper's only example of a working technique having come out of an edge phenomenon. What the work does not contain: neither an analysis of the mechanism nor an assessment of the replacement's range of applicability; in the same paragraph Prieto et al. are credited with a second achievement that is not theirs (see "Erroneous citations").

**[Phase transition](../../concepts/phase-transition.md)** — the word stands in the paper exactly once, and it stands inside a parody: composing a deliberately worthless hypothesis, the authors write that "phase transitions in test accuracy coincide with changes in the density of prime numbers within the parameter vector" ([p. 6, para. 1](#p6-1)). There is no language of phase transitions as a theory in the work — no order parameters, no critical exponents, no finite-size scaling — and the authors cannot be credited with a view of grokking as a phase transition.

**[The data fraction / the critical dataset size](../../concepts/data-fraction-critical-dataset-size.md)** — a mention only, but in a load-bearing place of the argument: "As the dataset grows, the grokking effect is in general less pronounced (Liu et al. 2022a)" ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)). From this observation a conclusion about the limitedness of the setting is drawn; the work measures neither a critical size nor any dependence, and does not mention the laws of critical size accumulated by the corpus.

## Points we could not reconcile

**Grokking is declared both practically insignificant and practically instructive — in adjacent paragraphs.** The prehistory says that the effect "suggests that the customary practice of early stopping may prevent a better solution from being found" ([p. 3, para. 4](#p3-4)) — a direct practical consequence addressed to a practitioner. A paragraph below, that same grokking is placed among the phenomena that "by definition" bear a limited relation to practical settings ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)). Nowhere does the paper reconcile these two statements.

**The section on "wider value" returns to grokking the practical advice taken away by the section on "practical insignificance".** [p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2) says that "recent work has shown that certain desirable quantities may continue to improve after standard validation performance has plateaued — namely adversarial robustness … and out-of-domain performance (Murty et al. 2023)". This is a statement about when to stop training, and it is not about algorithmic toys. The work's thesis — that edge phenomena give no practical prescriptions — is here refuted by its own list.

**The denial of grokking beyond algorithmic data and its acknowledgement stand in two adjacent paragraphs of one subsection.** [p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1) asserts that "any known studies *not* using this mechanism [an inflated initialisation] report standard generalisation". [p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2) adduces a delayed rise of adversarial robustness — an effect shown by [Humayun et al. 2024](../2402.15555.deep-networks-always-grok-and-here-is-why/original/2402.15555.deep-networks-always-grok-and-here-is-why.md#p1-2) precisely at an ordinary initialisation on CIFAR10, Imagenette and GPT (although the authors did not cite them; see below). The reference propping up the denial, meanwhile, points to a work that observes grokking rather than denying it.

## What is shown, what is taken from the literature and what is conjectured

A position paper does not require experiments, but this work has a figure of its own, and the strength of its chief argument depends on what exactly is shown in it. Three layers are worth separating.

**Shown by measurements of their own** — only figure 4, and only one thing: the number of primes among the network's rounded parameters tracks the test performance in two settings reproduced from the literature, averaged over 10 seeds ([p. 14, para. 3](#p14-3)). The experiment is conceived as a parody, and the authors debunk it themselves in the same place: the count of primes is a consequence of the non-uniformity of the primes over the shifting distribution of parameter magnitudes. The caption, meanwhile, is stronger than what is shown ([fig. 4](#fig-4)): "closely tracks" does not hold up on either half. On the grokking half the test accuracy rises smoothly from about epoch 30 and reaches half its final value by ~2000 epochs, whereas the prime count up to that point holds a flat shelf and jumps only on the final rise. On the double-descent half the prime count is nearly flat throughout the first fall of the accuracy, spikes only at the local hump (~165 hidden units) and settles to zero while the accuracy goes on falling. Grokking in the figure is shown only in accuracy — the very measure whose choice is declared two paragraphs above to be the source of the spectacle ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)); there is no loss plot for comparison.

**Taken from the literature** — the whole factual part of the argument for the practical insignificance of all three phenomena ([p. 3, para. 2](#p3-2), [p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1), [p. 4, para. 4](#p4-4)) and the whole positive part ([p. 3, para. 3](#p3-3), [p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2), [p. 5, para. 1](#p5-1)). The abstract calls this "an analysis of research outcomes", but no sample of works and no selection rule is declared: this is a narrative review. Standing apart is a blog entry (Pope 2023), adduced on a par with papers. The argument that "attempts to see ordinary grokking in plausible scenarios have so far been unsuccessful" is an argument from the absence of reports and rests on a single reference (Dziri et al. 2024) ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)); by the time the paper appeared the corpus contained countervailing reports — [Zhu et al. 2024](../2401.10463.critical-data-size-of-language-models-from-a-grokking-perspective/2401.10463.critical-data-size-of-language-models-from-a-grokking-perspective.card.md), [Golechha 2024](../2405.12755.progress-measures-for-grokking-on-real-world-tasks/2405.12755.progress-measures-for-grokking-on-real-world-tasks.card.md), [Lv et al. 2024](../2409.09281.language-models-grok-to-copy/2409.09281.language-models-grok-to-copy.card.md), [Abramov et al. 2025](../2504.20752.grokking-in-the-wild-data-augmentation-for-real-world-multi-hop-reasoning-with-transformers/2504.20752.grokking-in-the-wild-data-augmentation-for-real-world-multi-hop-reasoning-with-transformers.card.md) — and not one of them is discussed in the paper.

**Declared as a position and admitted to be subjective** — the key claim that current practice does not reflect this point of view. The yardstick of value is meanwhile taken ready-made: the "sociotechnical pragmatism" of Watson et al. 2024, by which knowledge is valuable in so far as it has downstream utility — the paper neither derives nor checks it but declares it "broadly compatible" with the field's motivations ([p. 5, para. 3](#p5-3)). The authors write themselves that "this claim is ultimately subjective", and prop it up only with the mismatch between typical works and their own recommendations of section 5 ([section 5.2](#sec-5-2)). The notion of utility is meanwhile deliberately left flexible, and in the "Impact Statement" the fairness of outcomes is included in it, whereupon there is no way of checking whether the recommendations are followed — that is, the yardstick itself is denied the falsifiability the paper demands of theories ([p. 6, para. 1](#p6-1), [p. 7, para. 1](#p7-1)).

It is worth noting separately that the argument is close to circular: an "edge case" is defined by its absence from applications ([p. 1, para. 5](#p1-5)), and the conclusion that studying it is of little value is derived from that same definition ([p. 2, para. 1](#p2-1)). And that the group's own works stand in the load-bearing places: Curth et al. 2024b carries the debunking of double descent, Jeffares et al. 2024a appears in the positive lists of both phenomena, and Curth et al. 2024a and Jeffares et al. 2024b in the appendix on ensembles ([p. 14, para. 2](#p14-2)).

## Common misreadings and overstatements

Editorial notes on the places where this work conveys others' results erroneously or without the authors' caveats.

###### mc-2309-02390

**Varma et al. (2309.02390) — a work about grokking adduced as evidence of its absence.** The formulation: `"any known studies *not* using this mechanism report standard generalization (Varma et al. 2024, i.e. no grokking;)"` ([p. 4, para. 1](#p4-1)). In the PDF the reference is typeset as "(i.e. no grokking; Varma et al., 2024)"; the broken bracket in the stored Markdown is an artefact of the LaTeXML conversion rather than an authorial typo. The claim is addressed to works on other kinds of data that do not use an inflated initialisation. [Varma et al. 2023](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) do not study other kinds of data at all: all their settings are algorithmic, grokking is observed in them, and the whole work is built on it — including the ungrokking and semi-grokking predicted before the experiment ([Varma, p. 2, para. 1](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md#p2-1)). Moreover, in their discussion section they explicitly accept the observation of Omnigrok about grokking beyond algorithmic tasks at a large initial norm as consistent with their theory ([Varma, p. 10, para. 6](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md#p10-6)). The reference thus does not support what it is attached to; which work the authors had in mind for this claim cannot be established from the text.

###### mc-2501-04697

**Prieto et al. (2501.04697) — credited with someone else's result on delayed adversarial robustness.** The formulation: `"certain desirable quantities can continue to improve after standard validation performance has plateaued – i.e. adversarial robustness (Prieto et al. 2025) and out-of-domain performance (Murty et al. 2023)"` ([p. 4, para. 2](#p4-2)). [Prieto et al. 2025](../2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability/2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability.card.md) do not measure adversarial robustness: their subject is the numerical instability of the Softmax and its removal, and a delayed rise of robustness is mentioned by them exactly once, in a survey, with a direct pointer to the source — "Humayun et al. (2024) show that in many settings, neural networks undergo grokking-like transitions in their adversarial robustness" ([Prieto, p. 10, para. 1](../2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability/2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability.card.md#p10-1)). The pedigree of the error is traceable: the result belongs to [Humayun et al. 2024](../2402.15555.deep-networks-always-grok-and-here-is-why/2402.15555.deep-networks-always-grok-and-here-is-why.card.md), who introduce "delayed robustness" as a separate form of grokking and show it on a CNN with CIFAR10, a ResNet with Imagenette and GPT ([Humayun, p. 1, para. 2](../2402.15555.deep-networks-always-grok-and-here-is-why/original/2402.15555.deep-networks-always-grok-and-here-is-why.md#p1-2)); that work is absent from the bibliography of Jeffares & van der Schaar. The error is not innocuous for the paper's argument: the work to which the result belongs shows grokking at an ordinary initialisation on real data — that is, exactly what the preceding paragraph declares not to occur. In the same list Prieto et al. are cited correctly, as the developers of a stable replacement for the Softmax; the warning concerns the formulation rather than the work.
