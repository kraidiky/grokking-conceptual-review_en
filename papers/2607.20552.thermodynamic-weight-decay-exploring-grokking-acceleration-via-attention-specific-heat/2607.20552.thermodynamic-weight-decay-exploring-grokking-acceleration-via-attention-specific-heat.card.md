# Pandey 2026 — Thermodynamic Weight Decay: Exploring Grokking Acceleration via Attention Specific Heat

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Chitraansh Pandey** (independent researcher; chitraanshpandey@gmail.com).

arXiv [2607.20552](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20552), July 2026 (v1), 9 pages, 4 composite figures (5 files), 2 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. A single-author work: grokking is taken as a thermodynamic phase transition (the isomorphism between attention and the canonical ensemble is borrowed from Kim), the "specific heat" of the attention $C_{v}=\mathrm{Var}(QK^{\top}/\sqrt{d_{k}})$ as an observable diverging at the transition, and weight decay as a "supply of heat" ($T_{\mathrm{eff}}\propto\sqrt{d_{k}}/\lVert W\rVert^{2}$). The artefact is CvAdamW: AdamW with a dynamic $\lambda_{t}$ growing in proportion to the smoothed increase of $C_{v}$, behind a memorisation gate train_acc $\geq 0.99$; plus a scale-invariant z-version. On $(a+b)\bmod 97$ the peak of $C_{v}$ precedes the grokking in 10/10 seeds (a mean lead of 2400 epochs), and a cold start shortens the delay by 257 epochs on average (6.0 %; Wilcoxon $p=0.049$, but the $t$-test and the sign test do not agree); three failures of the detector are honestly documented.

## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the managerial idea: if the network itself announces the approach of a phase boundary, catch the signal online and supply exactly the missing energy; the headline demonstration — under a budget of 4000 epochs the baseline "never groks", a step intervention groks at 3333, and CvAdamW at 2802, so the contribution is presented as the "switching on" of generalisation rather than a mere speed-up ([`p4-2`](#p4-2)). The analysis: "the baseline never groks" is a property of the truncated budget and of the chosen seed rather than of the task: at 7000 epochs the baseline groks in all 10 seeds, and three of them fit inside 4000; the paired study gives a modest 6.0 % with divergent tests (the Wilcoxon test and the bootstrap are significant, the $t$-test, the sign test and the classical interval are not), set out honestly; and the nearest competitor in purpose — Grokfast, with a speed-up of several times over — is not compared against, nor are the corpus's precursors (2306.13253, 2606.12966, 2605.20441, 2605.08237).

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — the decay of the weights as the "executive organ" of the thermodynamics: from $T_{\mathrm{eff}}\propto\sqrt{d_{k}}/\lVert W\rVert^{2}$ it is derived that raising $\lambda$ reduces the norm and "literally heats" the system; CvAdamW modulates only the regularisation coefficient ($\lambda_{t}=\lambda_{\mathrm{base}}+\kappa\max(0,\mu_{t}-\tau)$, or the z-version with the single threshold $2.0$), driving it up to 1.5–2.6 in the $\kappa/\tau$ version against 0.8–1.1 in the scale-invariant one — a measured trade-off of "strength against universality" ([`p7-2`](#p7-2)). The analysis: the link "weight decay heats" is nowhere measured — that raising $\lambda$ really does raise $C_{v}$ is not checked, and the formula is given without a derivation; the simplest explanation of the 6 % gain is a noisy decay schedule with a mean above the baseline one, and there is no control of "simply more weight decay" (the author himself defers it to future work); and every spike of $\lambda$ in fig. 3 is accompanied by a collapse of the validation accuracy almost to zero followed by a jump upwards — the mechanism resembles a shake-up with a reset rather than a supply of heat, and the paper does not discuss this.

**[The slingshot effect](../../concepts/slingshot.md)** — the slingshot as the reason for the failure of discrete triggers: during a major rearrangement of the circuits train_acc dips transiently below 0.99, the memorisation gate closes exactly at the peak of $C_{v}$, and a one-shot trigger goes blind; a continuous proportional response has time to accumulate impulse before the gate closes — "the heat is supplied while the mountain is rising, not after" ([`p3-7`](#p3-7)). The analysis: this is the paper's only citation that works substantively (the slingshot 2206.04817), and the substantive contribution is the principle of continuity as a defence against the timing errors that are fatal to one-shot interventions; the other two failures (all the triggers firing at epoch 16 from the noise of the initialisation; a firing at epoch 240 from batch ripple) are honestly documented too, together with the design elements that remove them (the memorisation gate, an EMA damper with a window of 10 epochs).

**[Phase transition](../../concepts/phase-transition.md)** — $C_{v}$ as a harbinger: on the unperturbed baseline trajectory the peak of the smoothed specific heat after the memorisation precedes the grokking in 10/10 seeds (a mean lead of 2400 epochs, a median of 2396), but the moment of the peak does not correlate with the epoch of the grokking (Pearson $r=-0.099$, $p=0.79$) — the author himself calls the signal a qualitative harbinger and not a quantitative one ([`p6-1`](#p6-1)). The analysis: a lead of 2400 epochs against a mean grokking at 4313 is more than half of the run, and such a "harbinger" marks out the whole plateau rather than the nearness of the event; no signs of a divergence (a scaling with the size, critical exponents) were checked — there is only a rise and a maximum; in the left panel of fig. 4 the z-score is solid noise for all 7000 epochs, crossing the threshold a hundred times, with no distinguished feature at the epoch of the grokking — the caption "a sharper anomaly" is not borne out by the figure; the plateau is called the chance level, yet it sits at 0.50 with 97 classes (~0.01) — a trace of the commutativity of $(a+b)$ under a pairwise split; and the whole physics rests on a single preprint by Kim, never independently checked.

**[Negative results](../../concepts/negative-results.md)** — the failure modes are declared a contribution in their own right rather than a caveat ([`p8-2`](#p8-2)): three successive formulations of the detector failed, and each is analysed as fully as the successful one.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong point — the honesty of the report in the genre of "our optimiser is better".** Three failures of the detector are set out as a contribution (the noise of the initialisation, the micro-ripple, the blinding by the slingshot — each motivating an element of the design); the divergence of the statistical tests is presented transparently with no cherry-picking; the pairing by seed is observed and the per-seed differences are published; the limitations (one task, no comparison with other progress measures, a small $n$) are named outright; and the principle "a continuous proportional response is robust to timing errors" is a transferable lesson.

**The physics is borrowed and unchecked, the control is missing, and the second variant is worse than the baseline.** The isomorphism and the temperature formula are taken from a single preprint with no independent check; the "heating" by weight decay is not measured; the z-detector in the figure detects nothing (noise with hundreds of threshold crossings), and the gain is indistinguishable from "a noisy decay schedule with a mean above the baseline"; the continuous sensor — the second of the two variants — is on average worse than the baseline (4333 against 4313), which is presented as "the cold start is paradoxically stronger"; the results of the claimed $\kappa/\tau$ series on 5 seeds are given nowhere; how $C_{v}$ is computed (over the heads, the layers, the batch) is not said; and the "smooth" response in fig. 3 is three narrow spikes as wide as the step one.

**How the work will be cited** ("the specific heat of the attention predicts grokking and speeds it up") — more precisely: on one task the peak of $C_{v}$ does indeed precede the grokking in all 10 seeds, but is unrelated to the moment of the grokking; the speed-up is 6 % and significant only under some of the tests; one of the two variants of the method is worse than the baseline; and there is no control of "simply more weight decay".
