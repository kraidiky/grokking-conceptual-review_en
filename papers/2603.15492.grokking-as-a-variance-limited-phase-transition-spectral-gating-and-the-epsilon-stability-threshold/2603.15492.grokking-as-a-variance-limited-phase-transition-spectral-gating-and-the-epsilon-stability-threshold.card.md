## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — reformulated as a problem of **spectral accessibility**: the generalising solution is not so much found late as it becomes **reachable** late ([`p10-1`](#p10-1)). The delay is declared to be an era of accumulating gradient variance rather than a random walk ([`p4-5`](#p4-5)).

**[The edge of stability](../../concepts/edge-of-stability.md)** — the core of the argument. The step-stability condition is carried over to AdamW, where the effective step is different for each parameter, and a **spectral gating condition** $\lambda_{max}^{H}<\frac{2}{\eta}(\sqrt{v_{t}}+\epsilon)$ is obtained ([`p4-2`](#p4-2)). While the variance is small the ceiling is low, and the sharp generalising basin is simply inaccessible ([`p4-4`](#p4-4)).

**[Flat and sharp minima](../../concepts/loss-landscape-basins.md)** — a direct objection to the corpus: for algorithmic tasks the generalising solution is declared **sharper** than the memorising one ([`p2-4`](#p2-4)). The argument is topological: a trigonometric circuit rests on an exact interference and is fragile to a shift of frequency or phase, whereas a lookup table admits combinatorially many arrangements of the weights and therefore gives a broad flat attractor ([`p11-1`](#p11-1), [`p11-2`](#p11-2)). Measured: for the task at $P=41$ the sharpness of the generalising basin is 721 times above the initial stability threshold ([`p9-1`](#p9-1), [`fig-7`](#fig-7)).

**[The slingshot](../../concepts/slingshot.md)** — read not as an anomaly but as a **necessary injection of variance**: a spike of $v_{t}$ reduces the effective step and thereby raises the stability ceiling ([`p10-6`](#p10-6)). This removes the contradiction between Thilak et al. (instability drives grokking) and Prieto et al. (numerical instability impedes learning) ([`p10-4`](#p10-4)).

**[Optimisers](../../concepts/optimizer-adam-adamw-sgd.md)** — AdamW is described as an SDE with a "rectified diffusion"; the key difference from Riemannian Langevin dynamics is that the diffusion is divided by $D_{t}$ rather than by $D_{t}^{1/2}$, which makes the diffusivity **bounded** above by $\eta$ ([`p2-6`](#p2-6), [`p3-4`](#p3-4)).

**[Numerical stability](../../concepts/numerical-stability-fix.md)** — the parameter $\epsilon$ is placed at the centre of the analysis and endowed with three regimes: at $\epsilon\gg\sigma$ the diffusion dies out and AdamW degenerates into a slowed-down SGD ([`p3-8`](#p3-8)); at $\epsilon\to 0$ the weight norm diverges ([`p3-10`](#p3-10)); and grokking lives in the narrow band $\epsilon\approx\sigma_{noise}$ ([`p3-11`](#p3-11), [`p8-1`](#p8-1)).

**[Phase transition](../../concepts/phase-transition.md)** — a two-dimensional phase diagram of "task difficulty against optimiser stability" is built and three regimes are singled out ([`fig-1`](#fig-1), [`p4-10`](#p4-10)): a capacity collapse at $P<23$, a variance-limited regime at $29\leq P\leq 59$ and a stability advantage at $P\geq 67$.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the setting: an MLP with two hidden layers of width 128 on the task $a+b\pmod P$ with embeddings learned from scratch, full-batch AdamW ([`p4-6`](#p4-6)).

**[Critical dataset size](../../concepts/data-fraction-critical-dataset-size.md)** — instead of the data fraction the controlling quantity here is the **difficulty of the task**: the time to grokking is non-monotone in $P$, and a "valley of difficulty" with its peak at $P=41$ is found ([`p6-1`](#p6-1)). At $P\geq 67$ memorisation becomes dimensionally unstable, since its cost grows as $\mathcal{O}(P^{2})$ against the circuit's $\mathcal{O}(1)$ ([`p7-1`](#p7-1)).

**[Fourier features and circuits](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — the capacity collapse is explained through them: the task requires representing $P$ distinct Fourier modes, and at an insufficient embedding dimension the gradient signal becomes rank-deficient ([`p5-1`](#p5-1), [`p11-5`](#p11-5)). The boundary $V\propto P$ is found by a sweep ([`fig-10`](#fig-10)).

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — analysed as a radial equilibrium: the weight norm is described by an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process, and a stronger weight decay contracts the orbit and **speeds** grokking up by limiting the search volume ([`p7-2`](#p7-2), [`p7-3`](#p7-3)).

**[Implicit bias](../../concepts/margin-maximization-implicit-bias.md)** — the geometric theories (Musat, Pesme et al.) are accepted as a description of the destination of the path, but their explanation of the delay by a weak weight decay is rejected: an ablation with isotropic noise shows that what matters is not the magnitude of the drift but its **direction** ([`p10-3`](#p10-3)).

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — the formation of the circuit is tracked by three quantities: the ratio of forces $C_{push}/C_{pull}$, the Hessian trace by the Hutchinson estimator and the Fourier sparsity ([`p4-8`](#p4-8)). Under SGLD not one of them budges ([`p9-3`](#p9-3)).

**[From lazy to rich](../../concepts/lazy-to-rich-kernel-to-feature-learning.md)** — the picture of Kumar et al. is taken as the point of departure but declared incomplete: it describes *what* changes, whereas here a *kinetic* explanation of the timing is proposed ([`p2-2`](#p2-2), [`p11-1`](#p11-1)).

It is worth saying separately what the work does **not** contain. There is no check on other tasks and architectures: the whole work is an MLP on modular addition, and the carry-over to language models is named a matter for further work ([`p12-2`](#p12-2)). There is no number of seeds for any sweep and not a single spread band. And there is no direct measurement of the entry mechanism itself: the growth of $v_{t}$ and the rise of the ceiling are shown by curves, but no intervention (forcibly raising the ceiling and seeing whether the grokking comes earlier) is carried out.

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the original phenomenon; [Omnigrok, Liu et al. 2022](../2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data/2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data.card.md) — the Goldilocks zone taken as a reference point; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — the "clock circuit" whose sharpness is measured here; [Merrill et al. 2023](../2303.11873.a-tale-of-two-circuits-grokking-as-competition-of-sparse-and-dense-subnetworks/2303.11873.a-tale-of-two-circuits-grokking-as-competition-of-sparse-and-dense-subnetworks.card.md) and [Varma et al. 2023](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — the competition of circuits and their efficiency; [Thilak et al. 2022](../2206.04817.the-slingshot-mechanism-an-empirical-study-of-adaptive-optimizers-and-grokking/2206.04817.the-slingshot-mechanism-an-empirical-study-of-adaptive-optimizers-and-grokking.card.md) — the slingshot, reinterpreted here as an injection of variance; [Kumar et al. 2024](../2310.06110.grokking-as-the-transition-from-lazy-to-rich-training-dynamics/2310.06110.grokking-as-the-transition-from-lazy-to-rich-training-dynamics.card.md) — the transition from the lazy to the rich regime; [Prieto et al. 2025](../2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability/2501.04697.grokking-at-the-edge-of-numerical-stability.card.md) — numerical stability, with which the work removes a contradiction.

**[The role of gradient noise](../../concepts/gradient-noise.md)** — a theory of a "variance-limited" transition: the variance of the updates of an adaptive optimiser as the controlling parameter ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)). In the concept card it stands among the noise theories, against which the chain of full-batch settings in the corpus is set.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is experimental and short (twelve pages including the appendix), with one setting and one measurement for whose sake the whole thing was undertaken.

**The main measurement is made and it is unexpected.** The sharpness of the generalising basin at $P=41$ exceeds the initial stability threshold **721-fold** ([`p9-1`](#p9-1)). If the number is right, the flat-minima conjecture is simply inapplicable to algorithmic tasks, and that runs counter to most of the corpus.

**The explanation of the sharpness is convincing at the level of argument.** A trigonometric circuit rests on an exact interference and collapses under a small shift of phase; a lookup table admits combinatorially many equivalent arrangements ([`p11-1`](#p11-1), [`p11-2`](#p11-2)). This is plausible, and it also explains why the picture is the reverse for perceptual tasks.

**The negative experiment with SGLD is the most valuable part.** An isotropic noise matched by thermal scale raises the accuracy to 60–80 % but gives no flawless generalisation ([`p9-2`](#p9-2), [`fig-14`](#fig-14)). Hence the conclusion: what matters is not the amount of noise but its **direction**. The argument is strengthened by histograms of the update multipliers, in which AdamW has a heavy tail spanning several orders of magnitude ([`fig-12`](#fig-12)).

**The non-monotonicity in difficulty is shown and explained from both ends.** On the left is a shortage of dimension (the capacity collapse, the boundary $V\propto P$, [`fig-10`](#fig-10)), on the right a dimensional instability of memorisation as $P$ grows ([`p7-1`](#p7-1)). Together these give the "valley of difficulty" with its peak at $P=41$.

**The causality of the spectral gate is nevertheless not shown.** All the evidence is a coincidence of curves: the variance grows, the ceiling rises, the grokking sets in ([`p9-1`](#p9-1)). Not a single intervention — forcibly raising the ceiling at a small variance and checking whether the transition comes earlier, for instance — is carried out.

**Condition (5) is derived non-rigorously.** It is obtained by substituting the effective AdamW step into the scalar stability condition $\lambda<2/\eta$, which holds for gradient descent ([`p4-2`](#p4-2)). For an adaptive optimiser with a per-coordinate preconditioner such a carry-over requires a justification, and there is none.

**The SDE is derived under an adiabatic assumption that the work itself declares to be violated.** The remark to section 3.1 says outright that the transition is triggered by slingshots, at which adiabaticity fails ([`p3-4`](#p3-4) and following). The equilibrium description therefore does not cover precisely the event for whose sake it was built.

**The number of runs is stated nowhere.** Not a single spread band, not a single mention of seeds: all the curves and the whole phase diagram are one run per setting. For a work whose main result is a boundary on a map this is a substantial gap.

**There is one setting, and it is small.** A two-layer MLP on modular addition, a width of 128, embeddings from scratch ([`p4-6`](#p4-6)). Neither a transformer nor another task; of the carry-over it is only said that it is a matter for the future ([`p12-2`](#p12-2)).

**The work is overloaded with coinages.** "Spectral gating", "anisotropic rectification", "capacity collapse", "stability advantage", "signal starvation", "valley of difficulty" — six new names in twelve pages, and some of them name things already known (the capacity collapse is a rank deficiency, the stability advantage a consequence of circuit efficiency in Varma et al.).

**Its place in the corpus.** The value of the work lies in two measurements rather than in the frame: a sharpness of the generalising basin exceeding the stability threshold by hundreds of times, and the failure of isotropic noise at a matched thermal scale. The first calls into question the applicability of the flat-minima conjecture to algorithmic tasks, the second separates "heating" from "direction". The price: one small setting, one run per configuration, a non-rigorous carry-over of the stability condition, and the absence of an intervention that would make the mechanism causal.
