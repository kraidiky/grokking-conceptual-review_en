# Salah & Yevick 2024 — Controlling Grokking with Nonlinearity and Data Symmetry

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Ahmed Salah**, **David Yevick** (the physics department of the University of Waterloo).

arXiv [2411.05353](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05353), November 2024 (a single version, v1), 15 pages, 14 figures (29 panels), 7 display formulas. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 2, in the corpus — 2 (both by the same group). The paper controls grokking on modular addition through the **non-linearity of the network**: the parity of the activation function switches fast grokking on and off, an odd component delays it arbitrarily long, and the depth and width move the delay; along the way it proposes a factorisation of a composite modulus from the PCA patterns of the weights and the entropy of a layer's weights as a precursor of generalisation. ## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the subject of the paper from the side of control: not "why the delay" but "which knobs move it". The knobs declared are the profile of the activation function and the depth and width of the network ([`p1-5`](#p1-5)); the main established fact is that a fast onset of grokking requires an even activation, while an odd component delays it by an arbitrary number of epochs ([`p7-1`](#p7-1)). The paper gives no definition of grokking through an accuracy threshold; there are no run statistics (seeds, spread) anywhere.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the testbed is taken from Power et al., but the carrier is different: a two-layer MLP **without embeddings and without a transformer**, a one-hot concatenation of length $2P$ ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)). The paper demonstrates that the canonical phenomena of the testbed are reproduced in this minimal setting too.

**[Fourier features and circuits](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — the form of the map and the cosine weights are taken from Gromov ([`p3-2`](#p3-2), formulas (1)–(6)), but the observation runs against universality: the weights of the connections to some of the first-layer neurons are quasi-sinusoidal with a sharp Fourier peak while those to others are aperiodic, and the grokking is preserved all the same — "in apparent contradiction to (Gromov, 2023)" ([`p4-4`](#p4-4)).

**[Structured representation learning](../../concepts/structured-representation-learning.md)** — two observations on either side of the line "structure and generalisation": a circular pattern in the PCA planes of the weights arises only at a sufficient non-linearity (the third layer, $a<0.5$), yet grokking is present at every $a$ ([`p9-1`](#p9-1)) — that is, a visible structure is not necessary; and for a composite modulus the cluster patterns give its factorisation (20 = 5 × 4 and 20 = 2 × 10, [`p10-1`](#p10-1), [`p10-2`](#p10-2)) — a curious by-product of the structuring. What is missing: a mechanism connecting the pattern with the algorithm of the solution.

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — a background rather than a studied knob: weight decay is switched on in every experiment with a reference to its known influence ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)); the observation of its own is that many epochs after the grokking some of the last-layer weights fall "below 10-41" with no loss of test accuracy, while the surviving connections carry the largest share of the information ([`p4-4`](#p4-4)).

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — two are proposed: the autocorrelation of the weights (7) as a measure of the learned periodicity ([`p6-1`](#p6-1)) and the entropy of a layer's weights $S=-\sum_{i}w_{i}\mkern3mu log\mkern3mu w_{i}$, whose course — a fall, a rise during the grokking, a fall after the test accuracy reaches its maximum — is declared to predict further training ([`p12-1`](#p12-1)). Both measures are qualitative: there is not a single numerical prediction of a time in the paper.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is recovered from the PDF (a PDF-only submission); two physicist authors, and the preprint is published nowhere.

**The cleanest result is the parity of the activation as a binary lever.** Under $x+ax^{2}$ the delay falls as $a$ grows; the purely odd $x^{2}\mathrm{sign}(x)$ and $x^{3}$ give no grokking (with $x^{3}$ the test accuracy rises and collapses), while the even $|x^{3}|$ does ([`p7-1`](#p7-1), [`fig-6`](#fig-6), [`fig-7`](#fig-7)). Hence a workable formula: the odd component is the regulator of the delay.

**Grokking without a visible structure is the second most weighty fact.** Aperiodic weights for some of the neurons ([`p4-4`](#p4-4)) and grokking at every $a$ with no circular PCA patterns ([`p9-1`](#p9-1)) — two independent observations against the necessity of a structured representation.

**Everything else is single runs and hedged links.** There are no seeds and no error bars anywhere; "plausibly related" and "presumably" are honestly placed by the authors, but not one of the links is tested by an intervention. The factorisation of the modulus is shown on three examples with no statistics of reproducibility. The entropy measure is never once compared numerically with the corpus's progress measures.

**Internal roughnesses.** The "Data Symmetry" axis promised in the title is reduced to a single section §3.5 with no connection to the axis of non-linearity; the record "below 10-41" is corrupted in the PDF (probably an exponent) and the exact value cannot be recovered; and the maxima of the test accuracy in §3.5 (0.85, 0.984) come with no definition of whether this counts as grokking.
