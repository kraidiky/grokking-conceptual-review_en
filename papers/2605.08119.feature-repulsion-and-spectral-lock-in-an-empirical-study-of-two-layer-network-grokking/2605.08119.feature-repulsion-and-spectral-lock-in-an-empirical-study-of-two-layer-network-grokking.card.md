# Xu 2026 — Feature Repulsion and Spectral Lock-in: An Empirical Study of Two-Layer Network Grokking

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Yongzhong Xu** (an independent author; code — github.com/skydancerosel/grokking-integrability).

arXiv [2605.08119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08119), April 2026 (v1), 11 pages, 4 figures, 5 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. A single-author empirical check of two propositions of Tian 2025 (the Li2 frame, arXiv:2509.21519) in his own two-layer setting of modular addition ($M=71$, $K=2048$, MSE): theorem 6 (the repulsion of similar features — a sign rule on the off-diagonals of $B=(\widetilde{F}^{\top}\widetilde{F}+\eta I)^{-1}$) is confirmed both at $\sigma=x^{2}$ (a sign agreement of 0.865→0.985 on 5 seeds) and at ReLU (up to 1.000) — the mechanism is activation-general; while the spectral trace in the weight updates — a "rank-2 lock-in" of the sliding ratio $\sigma_{2}/\sigma_{3}$ of the Gram matrix of $\Delta W$ (firing 15/15 against 0/15, a separation of 229 fold) — exists only at $\sigma=x^{2}$ and disappears under ReLU (0/15, 1.4 fold): a dissociation of "mechanism against signature". The negative results are put in the abstract; the detector, meanwhile, lags 35 epochs behind the grokking.

## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — a two-layer toy variant of the phenomenon inherited from Tian: the training accuracy reaches 1 by epoch 25, the test accuracy crosses 0.5 at a median epoch 102 at a weight decay of $2\times 10^{-4}$ and never at $\eta=0$ (matched seeds, 15 runs) — Tian's figure 3 is reproduced quantitatively ([`p3-8`](#p3-8)). Analysis: the "memorisation→generalisation" gap here is 4–5 fold rather than orders of magnitude in steps as in Power et al. — the discrepancy in the scale of the delay is not discussed, and the paper is connected to the founding work only by the definition of the phenomenon; and the transfer to transformers and non-algebraic data is not discussed even in the caveats, although the title speaks of "two-layer network grokking" in general.

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — two candidates with opposite shortcomings: the level metric $\rho_{\mathrm{tian}}$ (the deviation of $FF^{\top}$ from the form $aI+b\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^{\top}$) leads the test by 84 epochs at the working point and separates 15/15 against 15/15, but on a sweep of 60 runs over $(M,p)$ it fires in 60/60 at an almost constant epoch regardless of the outcome, and under ReLU at epoch zero (the author himself reduces it to a necessary but not sufficient sign that "the feature dynamics has begun"); the lock-in detector $\sigma_{2}/\sigma_{3}$ is perfectly specific but fires 35 epochs *after* the test accuracy reaches 0.99 ([`p9-4`](#p9-4)). Analysis: as a warning of grokking the lock-in is useless — it is a description of what has already happened, although in the introduction the quantity is presented as computable by a practitioner during training; the comparison is only against $\eta=0$, and specificity against a slowly grokking run was not tested; and the detector has four fitted quantities, with the choice of window load-bearing (at $W\leq 10$ there are 3/3 false firings).

**[Spectral analysis (SVD/ESD/FVE)](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — a cheap online instrument: the top 5 eigenvalues of a sliding Gram matrix of the increments $\Delta W$ (a $W\times W$ matrix at a window $W=20$); at $\sigma=x^{2}$, between epochs 150 and 200, $\sigma_{2}$ holds while $\sigma_{3}$ collapses by two orders of magnitude — a rank-2 lock-in, exact at small windows ($\sigma_{3},\sigma_{4},\sigma_{5}$ settle on the floor together) and a geometric cascade at $W=30$ ([`p6-5`](#p6-5)). Analysis: an internal tension — "rank 2" is exact precisely at the windows at which the detector does not work; under ReLU the spectrum stays rank-one, but the natural analogue of the detector ($\sigma_{1}/\sigma_{2}$) is named and not tested — the conclusion "there is no signature" refers to one quantity rather than to the spectrum in general; and later $\sigma_{2}/\sigma_{3}$ is inconsistent between the tables (6.8 in the sweep against 300 in the main experiment at the same $\eta$, and 1949 at $\eta=5\times 10^{-5}$ — the non-monotonicity is not explained).

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — the lever of the whole setting: Tian's $\eta$ is precisely the weight decay (not the learning rate), and the "grok / no grok" comparison is a pair of runs with matched seeds at $\eta=2\times 10^{-4}$ against $\eta=0$; a sweep over five values gives grokking 5/5 everywhere except at $\eta=10^{-5}$ within 600 epochs, and an extended run at $\eta=10^{-5}$ groks at epoch 1527 against an expectation of $\approx 2000$ from Tian's $1/\eta$ law ([`p9-1`](#p9-1)). Analysis: the check of the $1/\eta$ law is one seed, and a discrepancy of a quarter is called a confirmation; and the claim that "the existence of the firing is preserved" in the slow regime is unsupported (the firing epoch is not given, and in the sweep table at $\eta=10^{-5}$ a later ratio of 1.8 stands).

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**What is valuable — the first independent check of Tian's theorem 6, and an above-average openness.** The sign rule of repulsion is confirmed quantitatively on a prediction made before the experiment (0.865→0.985, an IQR $\leq 0.015$ on 5 seeds); stage III is confirmed under ReLU as well, about which nothing follows from Tian's part on stage II; the dissociation "the mechanism is general — the signature is activation-specific" is reproducible (15/15 against 0/15) and is stated as a result rather than hidden; the code is released, and the negative results are in the abstract.

**What is checked is not the literal theorem, and without a null baseline.** Instead of the projector $P_{\eta,-j\ell}$ the full $P_{\eta}$ is taken, justified on 10 pairs at one point of one seed (the author himself limits the conclusion to the sign); the sign agreement on random pairs or in the $\eta=0$ control is not shown — it is unclear how far 0.865 differs from "by itself"; the generalisation to ReLU ("the mechanism is activation-general") is measured on one seed, presented in the abstract without a caveat, and there the rule is checked on nearly collinear pairs (a similarity of 0.95–1.00), where the sign is easier to predict; and the explanation through theorem 5 (concentrated against smeared memorisation) is an argument: the "concentration" of the features is not measured, and the conclusion is drawn from the very rank structure it is meant to explain.

**Numerical mismatches.** "Jumps from 0.91 to 0.955" — the 0.91 is taken from the ReLU table, while the $x^{2}$ table has 0.895 at epoch 100; "the ratio jumps from 22 to 212" — there is no 22 in the table (there are 27 and 43); the sign of the $\rho_{\mathrm{tian}}$ lead is written now as $-84$, now as $+84$; and the coincidence "the saturation epoch = the firing epoch" has the resolution of the checkpoint grid, with the point 175 chosen after the firing epoch 174 was known.

**How the work will be cited** ("a spectral detector of grokking by an eigenvalue gap") — more precisely, and this is stated by the author himself: on one toy setting with a quadratic activation a quantity was found that lags by 35 epochs and has a fitted window, and that stops working when the activation is changed.
