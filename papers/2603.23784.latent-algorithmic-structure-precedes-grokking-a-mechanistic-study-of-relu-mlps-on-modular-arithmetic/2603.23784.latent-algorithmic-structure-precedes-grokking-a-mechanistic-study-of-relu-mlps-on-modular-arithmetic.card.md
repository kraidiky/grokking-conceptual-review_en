# Swaroop 2026 — Latent Algorithmic Structure Precedes Grokking: A Mechanistic Study of ReLU MLPs on Modular Arithmetic

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Anand Swaroop** (an independent author).

arXiv [2603.23784](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23784), March 2026 (v1), 9 pages, 5 figures (6 files), 2 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. A single-author preprint with a strong central experiment: in a ReLU MLP on $a+b\bmod 97$ the input weights come out not as sinusoids but as almost binary *square waves* (intermediate values only at the sign-change boundaries), the Fourier phases of the output weights satisfy $\phi_{\mathrm{out}}=\phi_{a}+\phi_{b}$, and this relation holds even under heavy label noise, when the model does not grok at all. Substituting the extracted DFT frequencies and phases into a known ideal construction gives 95.5% accuracy out of a model at 0.23%, and out of the saturation points (99% on training, before the grokking) 0.65–0.88. The conclusion: grokking does not discover the algorithm but sharpens the one already encoded at memorisation.

## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — a reinterpretation of what grokking does: it does not discover the right algorithm but *sharpens* a latent structure already encoded to a substantial degree at memorisation — gradually binarising the input weights into cleaner square waves and aligning the output ones until generalisation becomes possible ([`p2-5`](#p2-5)). Analysis: the phenomenon of the title is never once shown on the paper's own runs — not a single training curve, and the grokking time is not measured; one run per noise level, with no seeds; the headline figure of 95.5% is taken from the final point of a model that did not grok, while the argument "the structure is there before the grokking" is carried by the more modest numbers of the saturation points (0.65–0.88) — and the abstract presents these two cases as one.

**[Fourier features and circuits](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — a qualitatively different shape of the weights from the canonical line: the input weights of the ReLU MLP are almost binary square waves (values $\sim\epsilon\pm A$, with intermediate ones only at the sign-change boundaries) rather than the cosines of Gromov/Nanda; the phase relation $\phi_{\mathrm{out}}=\phi_{a}+\phi_{b}$ is nevertheless confirmed almost perfectly (a circular correlation $r=0.9993$ for the structured neurons), and in a from-scratch construction a square-wave input with a cosine output ($\mathrm{Sq}\to \mathrm{Cos}$) is significantly more accurate than a purely cosine one at every width $N\leq 384$ ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)). Analysis: the quantitative check that "square beats cosine" is skewed — the amplitude is fitted to the medians of the half-periods of the square wave and imposed on the rival cosine (the fundamental harmonic of a square wave is $4/\pi$ times its level); there is no spectral check of the odd harmonics; the origin of the shape (ReLU, a weight decay of $1.0$ or their interaction) is honestly left open — with not a single experiment varying them; and "Clock and Pizza", with a different mechanism for a ReLU MLP on the same task, is not cited (there are only five works in the bibliography).

**[The memorisation phase](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — the strongest result: already at the saturation point (99% on training, with the actual validation accuracy near zero) extracting the frequencies and phases from the weights and substituting them into the ideal construction gives 0.65–0.88 accuracy at every noise level — the algorithmic layout (the frequencies and the phase sum) is encoded in a form sufficient for an almost-recovery of the solution as early as the memorisation ([`p7-3`](#p7-3)). Analysis: the extraction plugs into the model a construction known in advance to work (Gromov) — only the frequency and three phases per neuron are taken from the model, so what is shown is "the parameters are already right" rather than "the algorithm is already assembled"; and the actual saturation accuracy is given as $<0.0001$ — strictly below the chance level of $1/97$, an anomaly left without comment.

**[Label noise / random labels](../../concepts/label-noise-random-labels.md)** — the phase structure survives a corruption of the labels that destroys the generalisation: at $\alpha$ from 0.01 to 0.30 the validation accuracy of the final model falls along a sigmoid to 0.0023 and the mean periodicity decreases smoothly, yet $\phi_{\mathrm{out}}=\phi_{a}+\phi_{b}$ holds for the most periodic neurons at every $\alpha$ ($r^{\prime}\geq 0.998$), and the $\mathrm{Sq}\to \mathrm{Cos}$ extraction gives 0.9548 at $\alpha=0.30$ ([`p4-6`](#p4-6)). Analysis: at $\alpha=0.30$ seven structured neurons remain, while the extraction at 95.5% runs over all 256 — a workable phase layout is carried by neurons declared aperiodic as well; and the link to Doshi et al. (a coexistence of memorising and generalising neurons under corruption) is claimed, but their result on the shape of the weights is not compared with.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**What is valuable — the extraction from the saturation point and the square-wave shape.** Recovering 0.65–0.88 accuracy from weights whose actual accuracy is near zero is strong evidence of an early latent structure; the advantage of $\mathrm{Sq}\to \mathrm{Cos}$ over $\mathrm{Cos}\to \mathrm{Cos}$ at narrow layers is statistically significant and repeated on 40 seeds (the one place with seeds); and the bimodality of the periodicity, checked by zeroing the unstructured neurons (a drop of $<0.001$), is careful.

**The experimental base is narrow and key things are not measured.** One run per noise level, with no seeds and no training curves — grokking is taken as a given and not shown; the comparison of the wave shapes is skewed by the amplitude fit; "the mean distance to the nearest point" is not defined; the abstract promises an extraction of the amplitudes, but they are absent from the formulas of §4.1 (all the waves are of unit amplitude — the unstructured neurons are, on the contrary, amplified); and the frequency is taken only from $W_a$ while 7% of the neurons have divergent frequencies.

**Internal inconsistencies.** The "unstructured" neurons are now 37, now 44 (the seven intermediate ones are now excluded, now included); the caption of fig. 5 says "for all levels of $\alpha$" with six panels out of nineteen; the attributions are muddled (Doshi is now "the same ReLU architecture", now "predominantly quadratic activations"); the accuracy $<0.0001$ is below chance with no comment; and there is neither code nor appendices.

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that grokking discovers nothing and only sharpens a ready algorithm") — more precisely: on one run per noise level, at $p=97$, with no training curves, it is shown that the phases of the dominant Fourier components already at the saturation point give, when substituted into a known ideal construction, 0.65–0.88 accuracy.
