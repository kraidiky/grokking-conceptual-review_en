## Concepts covered

**[The neural tangent kernel](../../concepts/neural-tangent-kernel-ntk.md)** — the analysis runs at the level of the kernel's eigenfunctions rather than of the kernel as a whole: a coefficient of alignment of each eigenvector with the targets is introduced, and it shows which directions exactly begin to work at the transition to generalisation. The corpus held no analysis of that depth ([`p5-6`](#p5-6)).

**[The lazy→rich transition](../../concepts/lazy-to-rich-kernel-to-feature-learning.md)** — the work confirms the framework of Kumar et al., carrying it over to a setting with no imposed constraints. The timings: the kernel barely moves until the moment of full training accuracy, then enters a lazy regime where it does not move at all, and stirs only after the test accuracy has begun to rise. This is the direct opposite of what Zheng et al. (`2412.-----`) found on image classification, and the two works do not cite each other ([`p4-11`](#p4-11), [`p5-2`](#p5-2)).

**[Sparse parity](../../concepts/sparse-parity.md)** — the same canonical task as in Barak et al., but taken as a bench for a spectral analysis: the clean bits enter the target, the noisy ones do not, and the question asked is when the kernel's eigenfunctions begin to lean on the former ([`p1-3`](#p1-3)).

**[Feature emergence](../../concepts/feature-emergence-feature-learning.md)** — an observation about structure: the useful eigenfunctions are concentrated at the beginning of the spectrum and around the index corresponding to the input dimension, and that index shifts together with the dimension ([`p5-6`](#p5-6)).
