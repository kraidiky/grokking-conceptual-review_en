## Concepts covered

**[The spherical weight-norm constraint](../../concepts/spherical-weight-norm-constraint.md)** — the work **introduces the technique** and makes it its principal instrument: the reduced loss $\tilde{f}(w)$ is defined by minimising over the angular directions at a fixed norm, and in practice it is realised by rescaling the weights back to the original norm after every optimisation step ([`p2-4`](#p2-1)). From the same technique comes the main practical result too: training at a constant weight norm **almost completely eliminates grokking** in the original setting of Power et al. ([`p9-2`](#p9-2)).

**[The loss landscape and basins](../../concepts/loss-landscape-basins.md)** — the work gives the notion a quantitative form fit for comparing tasks. The landscape is reduced to two variables (the weight norm and the data size, or the weight norm and the untidiness of the representation), after which it can be drawn and set side by side for MNIST and modular addition. It is precisely this comparison that explains why one task groks and another does not ([`p9-9`](#p9-5)).

**[The Goldilocks zone](../../concepts/goldilocks-zone.md)** — the notion is carried over **into weight space**: the Goldilocks zone here is not a range of hyperparameters, as in the same authors' previous work, but a spherical shell of radius $w_{c}$ in which the generalisation is better than outside it ([`p2-7`](#p2-3)). Hence the "U" shape of the test loss in the weight norm. The borrowing is stated explicitly: the picture is taken from Fort and Scherlis (2019).

**[Initialisation scale](../../concepts/initialization-scale.md)** — the work makes it **the control parameter of grokking**. The answer to the question of why grokking is rarely observed is direct: standard schemes initialise the norm to no more than $w_{c}$, and if the scale is increased explicitly or implicitly, grokking appears ([`p3-3`](#p3-3)). All the paper's experiments are parametrised by a single multiplier $\alpha=w/w_{0}$ on the standard initialisation.

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — receives **a quantitative law rather than a heuristic**: if the training loss is negligible after the overfitting, weight decay contracts the norm exponentially, whence the time to generalisation $t\approx\ln(w_{0}/w_{c})/\gamma\propto\gamma^{-1}$ ([`p3-2`](#p3-2)). The law is checked by a sweep on two settings and holds over about two orders of magnitude (appendix C). A coincidence is noted too with the observation of Lewkowycz and Gur-Ari (2020) about $t\propto1/\lambda$ outside the context of grokking.

**[Real data: vision, MNIST, CIFAR](../../concepts/vision-real-world-data-mnist-cifar.md)** — the work **carries grokking beyond algorithmic tasks**: MNIST, IMDb reviews (an LSTM) and QM9 molecules (a GCNN). An essential caveat of the authors: on these tasks the signature of grokking is "typically less dramatic", and on IMDb the jump is called outright not sharp — the delay is there, the abruptness is not.

**[Data fraction / critical dataset size](../../concepts/data-fraction-critical-dataset-size.md)** — the threshold receives a **geometric reading**: the contours of constant test error have the shape of a thumb, and the tip of the thumb sets the minimum amount of data for generalisation ([`p6-3`](#p4-6)). The difference from the effective theory of the preceding work is named by the authors themselves: that theory applies only to algorithmic datasets with a known optimal representation, whereas the landscape analysis applies to any supervised learning task.

**[Structured representation learning](../../concepts/structured-representation-learning.md)** — the work answers the question of **why grokking is more pronounced on algorithmic data**: there the quality of the representation decides whether the accuracy will be at chance or perfect, whereas on MNIST it decides between 95 % and 100 %. Formally this is shown through the untidiness of the representation $m$: the test loss depends strongly on $m$ for addition and hardly at all for MNIST ([`p9-9`](#p9-5)).

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the notion receives **a mechanism reversible in both directions**: grokking can be induced (by increasing the initialisation, by truncating the data) and removed (by constraining the weight norm). This is a strong statement about the nature of the phenomenon: grokking turns out to be not a property of the task but a consequence of where the weight norm started relative to $w_{c}$.

**[The memorisation phase](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — overfitting here has a measurable signature in weight space: the norm grows and reaches its maximum precisely during the overfitting period, and then falls **below the initial value** when the model generalises ([`p6-7`](#p6-2)). The same observation is noted independently in Nanda et al.

**[Double descent](../../concepts/double-descent.md)** — the work removes an apparent contradiction: the "U" shape of the test loss does not conflict with double descent, because what is on the axis is the weight norm rather than the number of parameters. The authors add a caveat that is usually dropped in citation: the "U" shape is better regarded as empirically widespread than as provably universal.

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the setting in which grokking is eliminated here; [Liu et al. 2022](../2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning/2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning.card.md) — the same authors' previous work, whose "Goldilocks zone" is carried over here from hyperparameter space into weight space; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — its setting is taken for the transformer experiments, and the fall of the weight norm at generalisation is independently noted in it; [Varma et al. 2023](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — explains the same through circuit efficiency, in which the parameter norm plays the part of the price of a solution.

**[When regularisation is necessary](../../concepts/regularization-necessity.md)** — a version of "it depends on the initialisation scale": [at a large initialisation there is no generalisation without regularisation, at a small one the generalisation is fast even without it](#fig-2). The initialisation axis is this work's contribution to the card of the dispute.

**[Sparse solutions and hidden progress](../../concepts/sparse-solutions-hidden-progress.md)** — hidden progress is named here as one of the partial answers to the question of the cause of grokking, alongside a slow formation of representations ([`p1-8`](#p1-8)). The work neither rejects it nor deems it sufficient.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is built as one simple idea carried through five settings; its strength lies in a prediction, its weakness in the assumptions on which the pictures from which that idea is read are built.

**The genuine prediction is the law of the time.** From the LU mechanism, before the experiment, $t\propto\gamma^{-1}$ is obtained: if weight decay contracts the norm exponentially, then the time to generalisation is inversely proportional to $\gamma$ ([`p3-2`](#p3-2)). The check in appendix C confirms it on the transformer over about two orders of magnitude. This is a rare case in which an explanation of grokking yields a checkable quantitative consequence rather than only a paraphrase of observations. The second checkable part is the un-grokking: constraining the weight norm really does bring the training and test curves together in the original setting of Power et al. ([`p9-2`](#p9-2)).

**The domain of the law is narrower than it seems.** On MNIST $t\propto\gamma^{-1}$ holds only for $\gamma$ from 0.1 to 1.0. Outside that range the law fails in both directions, and both deviations are explained after the fact: a large weight decay "spoils the optimisation", a small one gives generalisation faster than expected because of an implicit regularisation. The range checked is one order out of two.

**The landscapes are obtained with quantities frozen.** In the landscape analysis only the direction of the weights is trainable: both the norm and the representation are fixed (table 1 of appendix D). That is, the pictures from which the dynamics is explained are built in a setting in which two of the three moving quantities are absent. On top of that, the "global minimum on the sphere" is in practice the point Adam reached in $10^{4}$ steps; how close it is to the true minimum is not checked.

**The key formula is derived from a thought experiment.** The grokking time $t=(L+h\tan\theta)/(\eta_{D}\gamma)$ and the explanation of the dependence on the data size are obtained from a "reduced-trajectory analysis" — as the authors themselves call it — which rests on two assumptions: a separation of scales and the **linearity of the evolution of the representation**. The second predetermines the conclusion: by construction the untidiness $m$ moves along the segment between a random and a linear representation, and whether real training moves along it is not checked. The authors admit a possible deviation.

**The carry-over to real data is weaker than the abstract makes it sound.** Grokking on MNIST is induced by the same two devices as in the group's previous work: the sample is truncated 60-fold and the initialisation is increased. On IMDb the authors write outright that the jump is "not so sharp" — and in footnote 5 they admit that the reduced training error there has a "U" shape instead of the predicted "L", putting it down to difficulties of optimisation. In other words, on one of the three real tasks the central premise of the mechanism is not satisfied empirically, and this is said only in a footnote. Under a cross-entropy loss (appendix E) the picture holds only qualitatively: an initialisation of $\alpha=100$ is needed, and the test accuracy levels off above chance.

**What should be cited cautiously.** The statement $w_{c}(\text{a bad representation})>w_{c}(\text{a good representation})$ — central to the explanation of why algorithmic tasks grok under a standard initialisation — is introduced in section 2 by a forward reference to figure 6(d) and has no derivation of its own: it is read off a landscape built under the assumptions listed above.

**Blemishes in the source.** Appendix D retains the misprint "the weight norm first grows and then grows" (by figure 7(a) the second should be "falls") and an unresolved LaTeX reference; both traces are carried over into the card as they stand.

## Common misreadings and overstatements of this paper

Patterns of misreading or overstatement of this work, as observed in the citing papers of the corpus. Each entry says what exactly gets distorted in the restatement and leads to the authors' own paragraphs, where the qualification that goes missing still stands. The specific wordings are examined in the citing works' cards, in their "Common misreadings and overstatements" sections, where the "details" links lead.

**"Grokking on MNIST was introduced by this work" (erroneous).** The work is credited with priority for extending grokking beyond algorithmic data — down to the formula "the MNIST classification task introduced in [Liu et al., 2022b]". The first demonstration belongs to the same authors' previous work: [2205.10343, section 4.3](../2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning/original/2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning.md#p9-2) begins outright with the words "We now demonstrate, for the first time…" — grokking on MNIST is shown there, with the sample truncated to 1 thousand examples and an increased initialisation scale. Omnigrok made that extension broad and systematic — MNIST, IMDb, QM9, plus the LU mechanism — but the word "first" does not appear in it. The details: [2311.06597](../2311.06597.understanding-grokking-through-a-robustness-viewpoint/2311.06597.understanding-grokking-through-a-robustness-viewpoint.card.md#mc-2210-01117).

**"Observed on real data" without the caveat about inducement (an over-extension; later supported).** The most widespread pattern: grokking "has been observed / found / documented" on image classification and other real data with a reference here — as though the phenomenon were met with there in ordinary training. The authors caveat three times over: grokking on these tasks is something they ["turn out to be able to induce"](#p1-3), it is obtained ["in a slightly non-standard setting where it is relatively weak"](#p1-9), and its signatures are ["typically less dramatic than for algorithmic datasets"](#p1-7). The extended reading has since gained support: [2506.21551](../2506.21551.grokking-in-llm-pretraining-monitor-memorization-to-generalization-without-test/2506.21551.grokking-in-llm-pretraining-monitor-memorization-to-generalization-without-test.card.md) reports a grokking-like dynamics of the memorisation-to-generalisation transition in the pre-training of large language models — already without an artificially inflated initialisation. Examples of careful phrasing: [Miller et al.](../2310.17247.grokking-beyond-neural-networks-an-empirical-exploration-with-model-complexity/2310.17247.grokking-beyond-neural-networks-an-empirical-exploration-with-model-complexity.card.md) — "via alteration of the initialisation and dataset size", [2601.03162](../2601.03162.on-the-convergence-behavior-of-preconditioned-gradient-descent-toward-the-rich-learning-regime/2601.03162.on-the-convergence-behavior-of-preconditioned-gradient-descent-toward-the-rich-learning-regime.card.md) — "induced … by scaling initial weights", Grokfast — "treated to exhibit the grokking phenomenon". The details: [2405.17479](../2405.17479.a-rationale-from-frequency-perspective-for-grokking-in-training-neural-network/2405.17479.a-rationale-from-frequency-perspective-for-grokking-in-training-neural-network.card.md#mc-2210-01117), [2504.13292](../2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model/2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model.card.md#mc-2210-01117), [2510.04930](../2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking/2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking.card.md#mc-2210-01117), [2511.01938](../2511.01938.the-geometry-of-grokking-norm-minimization-on-the-zero-loss-manifold/2511.01938.the-geometry-of-grokking-norm-minimization-on-the-zero-loss-manifold.card.md#mc-2210-01117), [2512.03437](../2512.03437.grokked-models-are-better-unlearners/2512.03437.grokked-models-are-better-unlearners.card.md#mc-2210-01117), [2405.20233](../2405.20233.grokfast-accelerated-grokking-by-amplifying-slow-gradients/2405.20233.grokfast-accelerated-grokking-by-amplifying-slow-gradients.card.md#mc-2210-01117), [2311.06597](../2311.06597.understanding-grokking-through-a-robustness-viewpoint/2311.06597.understanding-grokking-through-a-robustness-viewpoint.card.md#mc-2210-01117).

**"Grokking occurs at a small initialisation" (erroneous).** A single but published case of the central result being inverted: a citing work writes that by this paper grokking occurs at a small initialisation independently of weight decay. In the authors' account it is exactly the reverse: [a small initialisation (α=0.5) always generalises fast, independently of weight decay](#fig-2) — the delay is created by a large initialisation, and it is precisely by scaling the initial weights that grokking is induced here. The details: [2509.21519](../2509.21519.provable-scaling-laws-of-feature-emergence-from-learning-dynamics-of-grokking/2509.21519.provable-scaling-laws-of-feature-emergence-from-learning-dynamics-of-grokking.card.md#mc-2210-01117).

**"A decay of the weight norm is the explanation of grokking" as a universal mechanism (an over-extension; explicitly refuted).** The LU mechanism is paraphrased as a general explanation of grokking through a decay of the weight norm, and weight decay is elevated into its principal cause. The authors limited the generality of the mechanism outright: [the "U" shape is better regarded as empirically widespread than as provably universal](#sec-6). The universal reading is refuted experimentally too: [2310.06110](../2310.06110.grokking-as-the-transition-from-lazy-to-rich-training-dynamics/2310.06110.grokking-as-the-transition-from-lazy-to-rich-training-dynamics.card.md), section 3, builds a counterexample in which grokking proceeds at a growing weight norm; [2506.05718](../2506.05718.grokking-beyond-the-euclidean-norm-of-model-parameters/2506.05718.grokking-beyond-the-euclidean-norm-of-model-parameters.card.md) shows that a large initialisation with a non-zero weight decay does not necessarily give grokking, and carries the analysis beyond the Euclidean norm. The details: [2511.01938](../2511.01938.the-geometry-of-grokking-norm-minimization-on-the-zero-loss-manifold/2511.01938.the-geometry-of-grokking-norm-minimization-on-the-zero-loss-manifold.card.md#mc-2210-01117), [2311.06597](../2311.06597.understanding-grokking-through-a-robustness-viewpoint/2311.06597.understanding-grokking-through-a-robustness-viewpoint.card.md#mc-2210-01117).

**"Under a cross-entropy loss grokking is reproduced without problems" (erroneous).** A citing work asserts that "the appendix confirms that grokking occurred without any problems even with a cross-entropy" — with a reference to that very [appendix E](#sec-e), in which the authors write the opposite: for the cross-entropy an initialisation scale of α=100 is needed, the test accuracy first levels off above chance, and "the dynamics is not as clean as with the MSE loss". The details: [2310.19470](../2310.19470.bridging-lottery-ticket-and-grokking-understanding-grokking-from-inner-structure-of-networks/2310.19470.bridging-lottery-ticket-and-grokking-understanding-grokking-from-inner-structure-of-networks.card.md#mc-2210-01117).

**"Constraining the weight norm induces grokking" (erroneous, isolated).** The work has two opposite instruments, and a citing paper swaps them: what induces grokking is a large initialisation on its own, whereas ["training with a constrained weight norm is able to almost eliminate grokking"](#p1-9) — constraining the norm removes the delay rather than creating it. The details: [2408.08944](../2408.08944.information-theoretic-progress-measures-reveal-grokking-is-an-emergent-phase-transition/2408.08944.information-theoretic-progress-measures-reveal-grokking-is-an-emergent-phase-transition.card.md#mc-2210-01117).
