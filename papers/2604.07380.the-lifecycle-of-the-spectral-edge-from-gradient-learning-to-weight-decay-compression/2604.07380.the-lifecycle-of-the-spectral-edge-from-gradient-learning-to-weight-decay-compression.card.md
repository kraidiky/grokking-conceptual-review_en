# Xu 2026 — The Lifecycle of the Spectral Edge: From Gradient Learning to Weight-Decay Compression

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Yongzhong Xu** (independent; the code: github.com/skydancerosel/dyck_scan).

arXiv [2604.07380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07380), April 2026, 15 pages, 12 figures, 2 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 1, in the corpus — 1. A continuation of Xu's series on the spectral edge — the leading direction $v_{1}$ of the Gram matrix of the weight increments: a "two-phase life cycle" is claimed — before the grokking the edge is driven by the gradient and is functionally active, at the grokking point the gradient and the weight-decay components of the update become co-directed along $v_{1}$, and afterwards the edge is driven by weight decay and serves as an axis of compression. Hence a reinterpretation of weight decay: it does not "regularise in general" but selects the flat directions along which the parameters contract at no functional cost. The tasks are Dyck-1 (the depth of the bracket stack) and SCAN; the best methodological findings are an exact decomposition of an AdamW update into its gradient and weight-decay parts, and an intervention on the weight-decay schedule with a refutable practical corollary.

## Concepts covered

**[Spectral analysis](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — the edge is computed from the Gram matrix $X(t)\in\mathbb{R}^{W\times p}$ built from $W=5$ consecutive increments of the attention weights ([`p3-2`](#p3-2)); the novelty against the neighbours in the series is the dynamic decomposition: the fraction of the gradient energy on $v_{1}$ falls from 97.6 % (Dyck) and 88.7 % (SCAN) before the grokking to 5.3 % and 0.2 % at the grokking point ([`p3-5`](#p3-5), [`fig-1`](#fig-1)). The analysis notes: the "alignment" is never once measured as an angle — there are only energy fractions; the definition of the gap $g_{23}=\sigma_{2}^{2}-\sigma_{3}^{2}$ has silently changed from the $\sigma_{2}-\sigma_{3}$ of the earlier works in the same series; and in Dyck the "compression phase" is a single spike at one checkpoint (the late gradient fraction being 87 %).

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — the claimed reinterpretation: weight decay selects the flat directions of the landscape along which the parameters can be contracted at no functional cost, and the spectral edge is such a selected direction ([`p12-2`](#p12-2)); an intervention from a post-transition checkpoint at $\omega\in\lbrace0,0.5,1,2\rbrace$ shows that the accuracy survives without weight decay (0.973) while the linear readability of the representations falls as it grows ([`p5-7`](#p5-7), [`fig-6`](#fig-6)) — "weight decay is the engine of the compression, not of the generalisation". The analysis: the "reversal" of the compression is in fact a prevention (the $\omega=0$ branch simply does not fall), the table is a single seed, and the accuracy in it grows monotonically with $\omega$.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the co-direction of the gradient and the weight decay along $v_{1}$ is declared a "microscopic signature of a phase transition" ([`p4-1`](#p4-1)); the statistic is "24/24 grok with weight decay against 0/24 without" ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)). The analysis: there is no definition of grokking in the paper at all — no threshold and not a single training curve; "no alignment without grokking" is an identity (at $\omega=0$ there is no weight-decay component to speak of), and the previous work in the same series honestly concluded that the local geometry does not predict grokking — a caveat not mentioned here.

**[The loss landscape](../../concepts/loss-landscape-basins.md)** — the "ablation paradox": a perturbation along $v_{1}$ changes almost nothing (a KL of $\approx 0.00005$, a curvature of 0.078), while removing the pair $v_{1}+v_{2}$ is catastrophic against a zero effect of random pairs ([`p4-6`](#p4-6), [`fig-2`](#fig-2)); the resolution — the Davis–Kahan gap holds the *orientation* of the edge, while the small curvature makes motion *along* it harmless ([`p5-1`](#p5-1)). The separation of "orientation against displacement" is useful for the corpus; the estimate itself is never computed, and a control of the edge against the bulk Gram directions — the one thing that would separate the edge from the bulk — is absent.

**[Linear probing](../../concepts/linear-sparse-probing.md)** — "a non-linear recoding rather than a loss of information": a linear probe of the depth falls from $R^{2}=0.98$ to $0.86$, a quadratic one to 0.59, while an MLP probe holds $0.990\pm 0.003$ ([`p5-4`](#p5-4), [`fig-5`](#fig-5)); in the memorising model the linear $R^{2}$ is higher (0.95 against 0.71) — a mark of explicit storage rather than of better computation ([`p10-1`](#p10-1)). The practical corollary: train with a large weight decay for the sake of the grokking and then lower it for the sake of linearly readable representations ([`p13-1`](#p13-1)).

**[Fourier features](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — an analysis of a non-arithmetical task, a rarity in the corpus: the grokked Dyck model has a dual Fourier structure — the representations at the Nyquist frequency $\omega=12$ ("what to count" — the identity of the token) and the attention uniform-inverse, at $\omega=0$ ("how to count" — counting by averaging) ([`p9-2`](#p9-2), [`fig-7`](#fig-7), [`fig-8`](#fig-8)); the memorising model collapses into the $\omega=0$ of the representations (77 % of the energy). The parallel with modular arithmetic is claimed as "a single group character" — in Nanda et al. there are about five key frequencies.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The best of the work — cheap observables and a refutable corollary.** The decomposition of an AdamW update into its gradient and weight-decay parts is exact and is computed inside the optimiser; the separation of "orientation" from "displacement along" explains the divergence between the ablation and the perturbation; the intervention on the weight-decay schedule yields a checkable practical corollary for interpretability; and the dual Fourier structure of Dyck is an articulate mechanistic finding.

**The phenomenon itself is not shown in the work.** There is no definition of grokking: no accuracy threshold, no rule for choosing the checkpoints, not a single training curve — all twelve figures show derived quantities. Power et al. is not cited once in the text (it stands in the list alone); 9 of the 22 entries in the list are never mentioned in the text, 8 of the 22 are self-citations, and the load-bearing support (Xu's 2026g thesis with its "gap flow equation" and "19/20 predictions") is not in the corpus and is named in the reference under a title that is not its real one.

**The "two-phase life cycle" is confirmed only for SCAN.** In Dyck the gradient fraction runs 97.6 % → 5.3 % → about 99 % → 87 %: the dominance of the weight decay is a single spike rather than a phase, and the late 87 % is incompatible with the "compression phase" of the inset; the abstract describes the SCAN picture as the general one. "No alignment without grokking" is an identity: at $\omega=0$ there is nothing to align with, and the distinction "grokking/non-grokking" is identical to the distinction "with weight decay / without".

**The ablation and the controls.** The "$>4000\times$" and "$>10^{7}\times$" are obtained by dividing by the threshold $10^{-4}$ rather than by a measured quantity; the second panel of figure 2 (the ablation of $v_{1}$ alone: $\Delta\text{acc}\approx-0.005$ at the post-grok checkpoint) is not mentioned in the text; and there is no control against the bulk Gram directions. Flatness is a property of the grokked model rather than of the edge: the caption of figure 3 itself says "all the curves are nearly flat — the edge and the bulk alike", while the numbers "0.001 against 1.83" compare two different models and two different directions at once; and in §4.2 the value for the edge is attributed to the bulk.

**Internal disagreements of numbers.** 0.26–0.62 against 0.29–0.62 (the text against the caption of fig. 2); $0.86\pm0.01$ against the 0.665 in figure 5; the "$5.2\times$" of §8.4 against the "$4.1\pm0.7\times$" of §9; the "universality" of the three classes rests on one task per class, the row for the functional class contains not a single number, and Term 2 / Term 3 of the "predictive" balance are computed for no task at all.

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that at grokking weight decay takes over the spectral edge and turns it into an axis of compression") — more precisely: on two small tasks, over the attention weights and predominantly on a single seed, it is shown that the fraction of weight-decay energy on $v_{1}$ at one checkpoint jumps to 95–99.8 %, that removing the pair $v_{1}+v_{2}$ drops the accuracy while removing a random pair does not, and that when the training continues without weight decay the linear probe does not fall.
