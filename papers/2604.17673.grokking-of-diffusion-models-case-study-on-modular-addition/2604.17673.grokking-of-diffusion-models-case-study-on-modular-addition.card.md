# Kim, Park, Østby, Gu 2026 — Grokking of Diffusion Models: Case Study on Modular Addition

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Joon Hyeok Kim**\*, **Yonghyun Park**\*, **Mattis Dalsætra Østby**, **Jiatao Gu** (the Department of Computer and Information Science, University of Pennsylvania; \* — equal contribution).

arXiv [2604.17673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17673), April 2026 (v1), 31 pages, 42 figures (71 files), 3 tables; submitted to the DeLTa workshop at ICLR 2026. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. A claim to priority: grokking is shown for the first time in a generative diffusion model — a one-layer DiT trained with flow matching (Rectified Flow, an $x_{0}$ parametrisation) on modular addition carried over into images (the symbols are EMNIST letters, and the result is generated directly in pixel space). Two regimes: a token-like $N=1$ (the classical grokking picture plus a full recovery of the trigonometric identities after Nanda et al. with an FVE of 0.87–0.96) and a diverse $N=256$ (which needs an "FFN sandwich" architecture; the sampling trajectory splits into an arithmetic and a visual phase with a critical step $t^{\ast}$ predicted from a jump of the FVE entropy, $r^{2}=0.95$). All the runs are single ones, with no seeds and no spread.

## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — a transfer of the phenomenon to a generative diffusion model: in the token-like regime $N=1$ (one image per symbol) the picture is classical — the training accuracy quickly reaches the ceiling, the validation accuracy stays at chance for a long time, and then there is an abrupt transition to an almost complete generalisation ([`p5-5`](#p5-5)). Analysis: at $N=4$ the caption of figure 2 itself describes the validation accuracy taking off *before* the training accuracy saturates — that is not delayed generalisation, yet the $N=256$ picture is declared "consistent with the $N=4$ case"; the fraction of training pairs is taken at a single high value ($R=0.9$), there is no sweep over the control quantity of Power et al., and weight decay is not discussed at all; and all the curves are single runs without seeds.

**[Fourier features and circuits](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — the whole apparatus of Nanda et al. is reproduced in a diffusion model: the attention activations of an operand concentrate their energy on four frequencies ($w_{1},w_{3},w_{7},w_{9}$ at $P=23$), at the SA→FFN junction the one-dimensional components disappear and two-dimensional cross terms $\cos(w_{k}a)\sin(w_{k}b)$ prevail, and a projection of the FFN pre-activations onto the Fourier basis recovers the trigonometric identities of addition with an FVE of 0.87–0.96 ([`p6-4`](#p6-4)). Analysis: the departure from Nanda is declared and justified (the basis comes from the activations of a forward pass rather than from the weights, because the residual connections carry substantial generative information), but the basis is extracted from the very activations at position $c$ onto which the projection is made — an FVE close to unity is partly built into the way it is measured, and there is no control basis; and the recovery of the identities is obtained only in the $N=1$ regime, while for $N=256$ there are only FVE bars and an entropy.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the canonical testbed is carried into pixel space for the first time: $a+b=c\pmod{P}$, where the symbols are images of EMNIST (or Kuzushiji-MNIST) letters and the model draws the answer by denoising a Gaussian $32\times 32$ map in the place of the result; commutativity is closed off more strictly than usual — an unordered pair $\{a,b\}$ is dropped from training entirely ([`p4-3`](#p4-3)). Analysis: correctness is measured by an external ResNet18 classifier with an accuracy of "over 95%" — the model gives 94–95%, that is, it runs into the ceiling of the measuring device, and there is no separate estimate of the errors; and the multiplier $11\times P^{2}$ in the size of the validation set is not explained.

**[Mechanistic interpretability](../../concepts/mechanistic-interpretability.md)** — an object of analysis new to the corpus: not which algorithm is learned (it is taken ready-made from Nanda/Zhong) but *when along the sampling trajectory* it works — a causal intervention substitutes a wrong image at the position of the result, noised up to a chosen step, and below a critical noise threshold the model no longer overrides the wrong input; the moment of failure is predicted from a jump of the FVE entropy, purely from internal signals ([`p7-4`](#p7-4)). Analysis: the reading of the two phases ("arithmetic reasoning" against "visual denoising") is an interpretation of a coincidence of two curves, and there is no intervention in the paper that switches off the circuit as such; the definition of $t^{\ast}$ is inconsistent within the paper (the second derivative of the FVE in the text against the second derivative of the *entropy* of the FVE in the captions); and the $r^{2}=0.95$ is named once with no number of points (there are no more than seven).

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**What is valuable — the transfer of the distinction "structure before behaviour" onto the sampling axis.** The nearest neighbours in the corpus (2604.13082, 2603.23784) show that an internal structure forms earlier than it becomes visible in the behaviour — along the training axis; here the same distinction is carried over onto the axis of the sampling steps: the concentration of the FVE in the FFN pre-activation is already high while the image is still blurred — "the arithmetic is settled before the image is drawn". Two predictive hits are strong as well: the overriding of a wrong input at a sufficient noise level, and the agreement of the predicted and the observed moments of the transition across all the moduli and both datasets.

**The experimental base is single runs in a non-standard setting.** There is not a single mention of seeds, repetitions or spread; the data fraction is fixed ($R=0.9$) and weight decay is not discussed; the mechanistic core (the identities, tables 1 and 3) is obtained only in the token-like $N=1$ without the FFN sandwich, whereas the conclusion attributes the distillation of the rules precisely to the sandwich; and the necessity of the sandwich is justified by a single phrase, "widening does not help", with no plot, while appendix H itself weakens it — an ordinary two-layer model groks better (an almost complete generalisation against 94%).

**Traces of haste.** The $N=256$ accuracy is now 94%, now 95%; the patch size is $S=1$, $S=32$, $S=8$ and $S=16$ in different places about the same configurations; the time convention ($t=0$ is the clean image) is broken in the appendices; the references to the architecture figure point to figures 41 and 27 instead of 7; "Ablation Study 3" is missing; Li & He are cited twice under different years; and there is a typo "diffuesion" in the list of contributions.

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that diffusion models grok") — more precisely: on a one-layer DiT with a home-made FFN-sandwich architecture, on EMNIST at 90% training pairs and with single runs, a distinct delayed generalisation is shown in the token-like regime $N=1$, and in the $N=256$ regime by a curve with no numerical characteristics of the delay; the recovery of the identities is for $N=1$ only.
