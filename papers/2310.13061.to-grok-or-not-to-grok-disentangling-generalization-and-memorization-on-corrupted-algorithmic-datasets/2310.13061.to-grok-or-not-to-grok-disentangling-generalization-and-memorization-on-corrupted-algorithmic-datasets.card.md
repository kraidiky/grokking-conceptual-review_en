## Concepts covered

**[Label noise and random labels](../../concepts/label-noise-random-labels.md)** — the paper makes label corruption a control axis rather than a nuisance. The fraction of corrupted labels varies alongside the fraction of data and the strength of the regularisation, and the phases are laid out on that grid: coexistence, partial and complete inversion, memorisation, forgetting. Inversion is the name given to the test accuracy exceeding the training one, that is, the network correcting part of the corrupted examples ([`p4-5`](#p4-5), [`p4-6`](#p4-6)).

**[The memorisation phase](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — memorisation and generalisation do not succeed one another here, they coexist: the network gives a near-perfect test accuracy and at the same time holds the corrupted training examples learned. What separates them is not time but neurons ([`p4-6`](#p4-6), [`p6-1`](#p6-1)).

**[The necessity of regularisation](../../concepts/regularization-necessity.md)** — a direct objection: grokking on modular addition turns out to be "remarkably robust" to label corruption and sets in even without explicit regularisation, which the authors call a sharp departure from the common belief that grokking requires it ([`p4-5`](#p4-5)).

**[Sparse subnetwork / lottery ticket](../../concepts/sparse-subnetwork-lottery-ticket.md)** — the memorising neurons are not only distinguishable but removable: pruning them lowers the accuracy on the corrupted data and raises it on the clean data, that is, the subnetworks are separated operationally rather than hypothetically ([`fig-6`](#fig-6), [`p1-2`](#p1-2)).

**[Fourier features and circuits](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — the measure of a generalising feature is the periodicity of the weights, measured through the inverse participation ratio; the distribution of the IPR over the neurons is bimodal in the coexistence phase and shifts upwards under regularisation ([`p6-1`](#p6-1)).

**[Ungrokking](../../concepts/ungrokking.md)** — the forgetting phase: under a very large weight decay the accuracy collapses after the network has already generalised, and the cause is a collapse of the weight norms to arbitrarily small values. Forgetting is met exclusively in networks that have grokked ([`p5-3`](#p5-3)).
