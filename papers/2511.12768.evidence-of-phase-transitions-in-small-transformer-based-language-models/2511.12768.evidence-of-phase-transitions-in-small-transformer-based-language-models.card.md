## Concepts covered

**[Phase transition](../../concepts/phase-transition.md)** — the work poses three questions and answers all of them affirmatively: are the transitions peculiar to large models, are they visible **without** a logarithmic scale, and do they set in early ([`p1-4`](#p1-4))? The answer: a transformer of 3.6 million parameters gives a consistent discontinuity around epochs 230–250, visible directly on the raw training axis ([`p11-1`](#p11-1)).

**[The order parameter](../../concepts/order-parameter.md)** — the role is taken by an index of dispersion (the Fano factor) $D=\sigma^{2}/\mu$ over windowed word counts ([`p10-5`](#p10-5)). It matters that there are several parameters and that they are non-redundant: the dispersion tracks the variance regime while the KL divergence tracks the whole shape of the distribution ([`p13-4`](#p13-4)).

**[The emergence of abilities](../../concepts/emergence.md)** — the line of Wei et al. is adopted as a point of departure but reinterpreted: the discontinuities are sought not in a task accuracy but in the statistics of word usage, which is why they are visible without scaling up ([`p5-3`](#p5-3)).

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — a set of four measures is proposed: the dispersion, the KL divergence from a Poisson reference, the mean length of a correct word and the number of distinct words ([`p6-12`](#p6-12), [`p8-7`](#p8-7)). The key argument: the ordinary loss curves show **no** discontinuity at all ([`p7-4`](#p7-4)).

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — a direct correspondence: in both cases a long plateau gives way to a sharp internal rearrangement that is invisible in the loss curve ([`p4-4`](#p4-4)). The difference is named honestly: in grokking the transition is "memorisation → generalisation" under supervised training, here it is "exploration → consolidation" under unsupervised next-token prediction ([`p12-7`](#p12-7)).

**[Mechanistic interpretability](../../concepts/mechanistic-interpretability.md)** — the work of Agarwal et al. is taken as a mechanistic complement: there the internal circuits form at a stationary external accuracy, here the same is visible from outside in the statistics of the vocabulary ([`p4-5`](#p4-5)).

**[Effective theory and statistical physics](../../concepts/effective-theory-statistical-mechanics.md)** — the theoretical support is taken from Rubin, Seroussi and Ringel: two minima of an effective potential, an exchange of stability, a first-order transition ([`p5-1`](#p5-1)). The observed reversal of the dispersion is read as the system crossing a threshold at which the "generalisation minimum" prevails over the "memorisation minimum" ([`p5-2`](#p5-2)).

**[The basins of the loss landscape](../../concepts/loss-landscape-basins.md)** — the most curious observation: before the improvement the quality **temporarily worsens**. The incorrect vocabulary reaches its greatest diversity exactly at step 250, and the weak correctness established earlier collapses ([`p12-4`](#p12-4)). This is read as the crossing of a nucleation barrier between two stable states ([`p12-5`](#p12-5)).

**[Memorisation](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — before the transition the model emits short repeating fragments ("th", "yo") whose statistics are sub-Poissonian, that is, suspiciously regular; after the transition they scatter into rare independent noise ([`p6-12`](#p6-12)).

**[The emergence of features](../../concepts/feature-emergence-feature-learning.md)** — a particular analysis of the word "you": the counts of *y*, *yo* and *you* are traced step by step, and within the transition band itself the fragments merge into a stable three-letter form ([`p9-4`](#p9-4)). This is perhaps the most vivid illustration of the formation of a composite unit in the whole corpus.

**[Critical dataset size](../../concepts/data-fraction-critical-dataset-size.md)** — the setting is deliberately modest: 3.6 million parameters, the Tiny Shakespeare corpus (1.1 million characters, 65 distinct), a context of 128 characters, 5 seeds up to 600 epochs ([`p5-6`](#p5-6), [`p5-7`](#p5-7)).

**[Double descent](../../concepts/double-descent.md)** — the work inherits the dispute about the existence of emergent abilities: the objection of Schaeffer et al. that the jumps are created by the evaluation metric itself is given in full and accepted as a requirement on the method ([`p4-2`](#p4-2), [`p4-3`](#p4-3), [`p13-6`](#p13-6)).

It is worth saying separately what the work does **not** contain. There is no connection with the internal mechanism: only external statistics of the generated text are measured, and their correspondence to the internal representations is called an open question ([`p13-7`](#p13-7)). There is no check of universality: one architecture, one corpus, a character-level tokenisation ([`p13-7`](#p13-7)). And there is no quantitative description of the transition: no critical exponents, no finite-size scaling analysis, no assignment to first- or second-order transitions — all of it is referred to further work.

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — grokking as the nearest correspondence; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — progress measures and an internal rearrangement at a stationary accuracy; [Rubin et al. 2024](../2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks/2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks.card.md) — the first-order transition and the effective potential, taken as the theoretical support; [Truong et al. 2026](../2604.13123.spectral-entropy-collapse-as-a-phase-transition-in-delayed-generalisation/2604.13123.spectral-entropy-collapse-as-a-phase-transition-in-delayed-generalisation.card.md) — a different order parameter (the entropy of the representations), measured inside the model rather than from its output.

**[Scaling laws](../../concepts/scaling-laws.md)** — the work gives an objection limiting the whole line of abrupt abilities: many step-like curves are produced by discrete evaluation metrics rather than by abrupt internal rearrangements ([`p4-2`](#p4-2)). In that case there is no violation of a smooth law — there is a non-linear metric.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is written by a schoolchild and an industry engineer; a third of the text is a literature survey, which is unusually much for a preprint.

**The main method is simple, cheap and sensible.** The generated text is cut into windows of 21 words, the counts are compared against a Poisson reference, and two independent deviations are measured ([`p6-10`](#p6-10)). Nothing beyond this is needed: neither access to the weights nor probes inside the model.

**The separation of correct and incorrect words gives an unexpected mirror picture.** The correct words move from nearly Poissonian to sub-Poissonian, the incorrect ones exactly the other way round ([`p6-12`](#p6-12), [`p10-5`](#p10-5)). Such a counter-movement of two classes is markedly more convincing than a single curve.

**The agreement of several measures is the main argument, and it is built correctly.** The kinks of the dispersion, the peak of the KL, the collapse of the incorrect vocabulary, the acceleration of the correct one and the jump in word length all fall within one narrow window of epochs ([`p11-5`](#p11-5)). It is precisely the coincidence of non-redundant measures, rather than any of them separately, that separates a rearrangement from an artefact of the metric.

**The objection of Schaeffer et al. is taken into account in deed, not in word.** Continuous internal statistics are taken instead of binary task thresholds, and this is named outright as an answer to the reproach ([`p4-3`](#p4-3), [`p13-6`](#p13-6)). For the corpus this is a rare case in which a known methodological objection is built into the design rather than mentioned in the survey.

**The observation about the temporary worsening is valuable in itself.** At step 250 the model emits **more** errors than at step 200, and only then rearranges itself ([`p12-4`](#p12-4)). The practical conclusion is stated outright: a stall or a spike of the error may mean an approaching breakthrough rather than a failure ([`p12-8`](#p12-8)).

**"Phase transition", however, remains an image here.** No critical exponents, no finite-size scaling and no check for hysteresis is carried out; the assignment to first or second order is deferred. The authors themselves include this in their plan of further work.

**The measure of word correctness is coarse.** "Correct" is any word from the corpus, "incorrect" any other; valid coinages and rare forms fall among the errors. The limitation is admitted ([`p13-7`](#p13-7)), yet both main curves rest on that division.

**The setting is unique.** One architecture, one corpus, a character-level tokenisation — as against the sub-word one used in real models; the carry-over is not checked ([`p13-7`](#p13-7)). The claim that the phenomenon is a property of learning in general rests on five seeds of one configuration.

**There is no connection with the internal mechanism.** All the measures are external; whether the kinks coincide with a rearrangement of the attention, the embeddings or the gradient flow was not checked and is referred to further work. The "internal rearrangement" is therefore an interpretation rather than a measurement.

**The literature survey is disproportionate and in places repeats itself.** Sections II-A–II-C set out textbook statistical physics, and the conclusions of sections IV-H, V-A and VII repeat the same three questions almost word for word.

**Its place in the corpus.** The work is not about grokking, but it is a close neighbour of it: the same image (a long plateau and a sharp rearrangement), the same theoretical support (the first-order transition of Rubin et al.) and the same dispute about the existence of emergent abilities. Two observations are valuable: the counter-movement of the statistics of correct and incorrect words, and the temporary worsening before the rearrangement. The price: the "phase transition" is not measured quantitatively, the measure of correctness is coarse, the setting is unique, and there is no connection with the internal mechanism.

## Common misreadings and overstatements

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text: places where this paper restates someone else's results with an error, or without the qualifications their authors attached. The "details" links in the "Common misreadings and overstatements of this paper" sections of the cited works' cards lead here.

###### mc-2301-05217
**Nanda et al. (2301.05217) — a wholesale confabulation of the source.** The work is cited under the correct arXiv identifier, but everything else is invented. Non-existent authors stand in the bibliography: `"S. Agarwal, A. S. Morcos, and A. Krishnamurthy, 'Progress Measures for Grokking via Mechanistic Interpretability'"` — the real authors are Nanda, Chan, Lieberum, Smith and Steinhardt. Methods that are not in the paper are ascribed to them in the text: `"applying singular value decomposition, neuron activation analysis, and embedding alignment … sudden drops in representational entropy"` — the target's real progress measures are [restricted loss and excluded loss](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md#p2-3). And the conclusion is inverted: `"supporting the view that grokking is an internal reorganization event, not gradual refinement"` — the central thesis of the cited work is exactly the opposite: ["grokking arises from the gradual amplification of structured mechanisms encoded in the weights"](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md#p1-2). Taken together, this suggests the source was cited from memory or generated without being read.

###### mc-2310-03789
**Rubin et al. (2310.03789) — an equilibrium theory paraphrased as a dynamics with a barrier and hysteresis.** Five confabulated attributes in one paraphrase: `"provided the first formal statistical-physics model of grokking, directly linking training dynamics to phase transitions"`, `"As regularization strength or training time increases, the minima exchange stability"`, `"demonstrating hysteresis and mixed phases"`, `"one corresponding to memorization … and another to generalization"`, `"Rubin et al. demonstrate rigorously that … gradient descent steps—can yield abrupt, global changes"`. In Rubin [the theory is equilibrium and Bayesian, governed by the sample size, the noise and the width](../2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks/original/2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks.md#p2-1) — there is no training time and there are no gradient-descent steps in it; the words hysteresis and barrier do not occur in the original at all, and [the saddles in the mixed phase are degenerate](../2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks/original/2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks.md#p6-6); [the zero saddle is "unaware of the teacher" rather than memorising](../2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks/original/2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks.md#p5-5); and the statements are derived within approximations rather than "rigorously proved". Combined with the confabulation of the source for Nanda et al. (see the item above), this is a marker of a systematically unreliable bibliography in this paper.
