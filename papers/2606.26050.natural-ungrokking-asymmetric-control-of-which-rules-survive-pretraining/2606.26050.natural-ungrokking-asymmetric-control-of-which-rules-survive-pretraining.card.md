# Li, Sreedhar 2026 — Natural Ungrokking: Asymmetric Control of Which Rules Survive Pretraining

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Juliana Li**, **Diya Sreedhar** (Harvard University).

arXiv [2606.26050](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26050), June 2026 (v1), 23 pages, 5 figures, 12 tables. A talk at the "Foundations of Deep Generative Models" workshop at ICML 2026. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. The subject is "natural ungrokking": a language model of 11.5M parameters in the middle of an ordinary pre-training run on a stationary web corpus learns the rule of pronoun gender ($0.94$ on held-out probes by step 925) and loses it by steps 3500–4400 of the same run, even though the evidence for the rule remains in the stream. The fate of a rule is predictable from the frequency of its support in the corpus; the loss is a displacement (the contrastive margin crosses zero at the very same 100-step checkpoint as the behavioural collapse); and the control is asymmetric — flipping the support into counter-evidence kills the rule with an exact dose monotonicity in two unrelated rules, whereas injecting support back, up to an evidence ratio 450 times above the surviving level, returns nothing. Everything is pre-registered: 22 predictions passed, 12 failed, 8 not evaluable.

## Concepts covered

**[Anti-grokking / the collapse of generalisation](../../concepts/ungrokking.md)** — a new carrier of the reverse sign: unlike the ungrokking of Varma et al., in which generalisation retreats when the dataset is shrunk below the critical size BETWEEN runs, here the ability dies WITHIN a single run on stationary data while the evidence for the rule remains in every epoch; "natural" means precisely that the trigger is not an edit of the dataset but the frequency structure of the corpus itself ([`p1-6`](#p1-6)). The analysis: a terminological caveat — the emergence of the rule in the middle of the run is nowhere shown to be grokking (there is no memorisation phase with a failing generalisation), and the root is borrowed for the sake of the reverse sign rather than of a reverse phenomenon; the claim of "no trace in the loss curve" is repeated three times, but there is not a single loss curve in the paper and not one registered test checks it.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the second half of the curve, of which the corpus has so far seen only the ascending side: the rule emerges and collapses by a predictable law — a densely supported one survives (9/9 TinyStories runs at any budget), a rarely supported one is displaced (0 web runs), and $D/N$ changes the depth of the fall but never once flips the outcome; the same "emerged and collapsed" curve is visible in the public Pythia 70M–1.4B and OLMo-1B, with the depth of the collapse ordered by scale (Spearman $\rho=0.894$, the collapse disappearing by 410M) ([`p3-8`](#p3-8)). The analysis: the lattice axis is realised through the identity of the corpus, and the registered quantitative theory of a critical frequency is failed by both of its ranking predictions, while the frozen support counter is admitted to be blind on web text (the class of girl-prompts tokenises to zero events) — the qualitative law stands, the quantitative form is open; and the direct corpus neighbour 2606.00230 (the ascending half of the same curve on minimal pairs) is unknown to the paper.

**[Catastrophic forgetting](../../concepts/catastrophic-forgetting.md)** — a rare counterexample to the customary frame: forgetting with no distribution shift, no withdrawal of data and no new data — a competing surface prior (the corpus preference for *he*) displaces the rule while the construction remains solved; the displacement reaches down to the representation — the decodability of the gender of the prompt at the position of the prediction falls to chance ($0.56$) on the web against a ceiling ($0.96$) on TinyStories, and a decomposition of the logit gap shows that it is the contextual contribution that carries and loses the margin while the direct embedding one is pressed to zero ([`p4-6`](#p4-6)). The analysis: the temporal alignment (the margin crossing zero at the same checkpoint as the collapse) — the central argument — rests on two seeds of three (the third does not pass the instrumental guard); and the held-out conflict slice of the focal family has 12 items, so an accuracy of "$0.94$" at such an $n$ is coarser than it looks.

**[Circuit efficiency](../../concepts/circuit-efficiency.md)** — the competition of circuits, carried over from efficiency to measurable statistics of the data, with a causal check in both directions: flipping the support in place (the token count preserved, the rest of the corpus byte-identical) kills the rule with $\rho_{\mathrm{kill}}=-1.00$ and repeats on a second rule at five doses within one corpus ($0.96\to0.00$, strictly monotone); the reverse injection at an evidence ratio of up to $3565$ against the $7.9$ of the surviving level returns the behaviour under neither a uniform, nor an early, nor a late schedule — while the carrying head is partially reassembled ([`p5-6`](#p5-6)). The analysis: the asymmetry compares unequal edits — the killing changes exactly the tokens the battery tests, whereas the restoration deliberately pours in non-overlapping names (an anti-prompting control), so that the failure of the return may be a property of the design of the experiment; and in the surviving runs the margin is carried by a single head of the last layer ($0.75\text{–}0.90$ of the attribution), while in the collapsed ones there is no carrier at all.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong points — a loss on stationary data, a dosed edit of the corpus and an honest scoreboard.** The phenomenon is caught with no distribution shift and no withdrawal of data, with controls against the deflationary explanations (the optimiser cell $0.927$; the nouns fall in the same way as the names; the agree condition remains solved — what is lost is the rule, not the construction); the $n$-gram baseline shows that surface evidence for the rule is present in the stream throughout the training, so the loss is a fact about the dynamics of the learning; the dosed flipping of the evidence at a preserved token count is a new causal instrument; and the pre-registration with a 22/12/8 scoreboard and two falsifiers that actually fired is a model for the whole corpus.

**The quantitative law is failed, the instrument is blind, and the asymmetry is asymmetric by construction.** Both ranking forms of the frequency theory are failed; the rescuing ratio of $0.46$ for the killing dose is taken from a bigram counter adopted after the blindness of the registered one was diagnosed; "the near-parity dose does not kill" leans on the displaced column, whereas only 1/3 of a seed remained restored; the middle dose, annulled by a gate, is resurrected in the prose ("counted at face value it would have been right too"); the abstract carries "450×" — the larger of two incommensurable units of one knob; the interventions are set up only at 11.5M parameters, and both batteries are synthetic.

**How the work will be cited** ("it is shown that the survival of pre-training abilities is predictable and controllable") — more precisely: on one focal and one second rule at 11.5M parameters a qualitative law is shown (the densely supported survives, the rare one is displaced), together with the displacement mechanism on two sound seeds and a one-sided controllability; the registered quantitative form of the law is failed, and the instrument measuring the frequency of the support on a web corpus is admitted to be blind.
