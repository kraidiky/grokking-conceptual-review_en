## Concepts covered

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — two new task-independent ones are proposed: the synergy and the redundancy of the neurons, computed through the O-information of Rosas et al. ([`p2-4`](#p2-4)). The argument against the existing measures is direct: the circuits of Nanda et al. require human labour, do not carry over to scale and are tied to the task, while the L2 norm and the Fourier gap are rules of thumb ([`p1-4`](#p1-4), [`p2-2`](#p2-2)).

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — read as an emergent phase transition brought about by synergistic interactions of the neurons **as a whole**, which pairwise measures do not catch ([`p1-6`](#p1-6)). Training at a small weight decay divides into five eras: feature learning, emergence, divergence, delayed emergence and decoupling ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)).

**[The emergence of abilities](../../concepts/emergence.md)** — the work takes the side of emergence in the dispute with Schaeffer et al. over whether the jump is created by the evaluation metric itself ([`p1-3`](#p1-3)), and proposes measuring emergence from within: synergy is exactly that excess of the joint contribution over the sum of the individual ones which is spoken of in connection with emergence ([`p1-5`](#p1-5)).

**[Phase transition](../../concepts/phase-transition.md)** — the transition is recognised by a change of regime on the Pareto frontier: the model rapidly trades redundancy for synergy, the size of the synergistic subnetwork grows, and a peak is visible in the test loss ([`p3-3`](#p3-3)).

**[The sparse subnetwork](../../concepts/sparse-subnetwork-lottery-ticket.md)** — the object of measurement is the subnetwork with the greatest synergy; it is extracted and trained separately with the other neurons zeroed out, and its quality is compared against the original model and against the complementary subnetwork ([`p4-5`](#p4-5)).

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — a controlling knob. At 0.1 the additional eras of divergence and delayed emergence arise; at 2.0 the emergence era sets in at once and the decoupling follows it ([`p3-6`](#p3-6)). But too large a decay (10 and 50) kills the grokking outright ([`p4-1`](#p4-1)).

**[Initialisation scale](../../concepts/initialization-scale.md)** — a second knob, taken from Omnigrok: a multiplier $\alpha=8$ at zero weight decay and a held norm also produces an emergence era ([`p3-9`](#p3-9)). The result is therefore not reducible to the action of the regularisation alone.

**[Memorisation](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — the initial era is described through the measures: a low synergy and a high redundancy, that is, the network learns the features separately and extracts simple patterns with similar properties ([`p3-2`](#p3-2)).

**[The compression of representations](../../concepts/manifold-representation-compression.md)** — the final era: the synergy falls, the redundancy rises, the test accuracy rises; the model has found a generalising subnetwork and is removing the superfluous coupled interactions for the sake of compression ([`p3-5`](#p3-5)).

**[Predicting grokking](../../concepts/correlation-traps.md)** — the practical claim: an early small peak of synergy precedes the grokking, and its absence means that no grokking will occur ([`p4-1`](#p4-1), [`fig-5`](#fig-5)). The authors themselves call this something requiring further checking ([`p4-3`](#p4-3)).

**[Mechanistic interpretability](../../concepts/mechanistic-interpretability.md)** — the work calls for a change of paradigm: from decomposing a network into a sum of isolated parts to acknowledging emergent properties arising out of the interactions between them ([`p5-4`](#p5-4)).

**[The edge of stability](../../concepts/edge-of-stability.md)** — among the plans: to relate the observed eras to the eras of the early training dynamics (Kalra & Barkeshli) and to the edge of stability, which might explain the loss oscillations found by Notsawo et al. ([`p5-7`](#p5-7)).

It is worth saying separately what the work does **not** contain. There is no scale: one two-layer fully connected network on modular addition at $p=97$, 250 neurons, 5 seeds ([`p2-3`](#p2-3)) — a limitation admitted first in the list ([`p5-1`](#p5-1)). Nor is there a reliable method of extracting the subnetwork: the features are binned into 10 bins by agglomerative clustering at sklearn's default settings ([`p2-6`](#p2-6)), and the authors themselves write that this may fail to capture the structure of the features ([`p5-2`](#p5-2)). Finally, the causality is claimed only as preliminary: the difference between the synergistic subnetwork and its complement is small ([`p4-9`](#p4-9)).

## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is declared preliminary by the authors themselves ("ongoing work"): five pages of main text and two appendices of plots.

**The measure is chosen sensibly and answers the claim.** If emergence is an excess of the joint effect over the sum of the individual ones, then it must be measured by a quantity that computes exactly that; the O-information is such a quantity and, unlike the pairwise mutual information, carries over to many variables ([`p1-5`](#p1-5), [`p2-4`](#p2-4)).

**The method is task-independent and requires no manual analysis.** This is a direct answer to the weak point of progress measures based on circuits, and the authors state it without circumlocution ([`p1-4`](#p1-4)).

**The division of the training into eras gives a coherent description.** Five eras at a small weight decay and three at a large one ([`p3-1`](#p3-1), [`p3-6`](#p3-6)) — the picture is consistent: the divergence arises where there are not enough features to be joined, and disappears under stronger regularisation.

**Two independent knobs give one and the same thing.** Both a large weight decay and a large initialisation at zero decay lead straight to the emergence era ([`p3-9`](#p3-9)). The result is therefore not reducible to the action of regularisation alone.

**A check of causality is at least begun.** The synergistic subnetwork is extracted, trained separately and compared with its complement ([`p4-5`](#p4-5)) — most works on progress measures do not do even this.

**The main claim, however — predicting grokking — rests on eyeballing.** "An early small peak of synergy precedes the grokking" is shown on several curves; no threshold, no lead in steps and no rate of correct predictions is given. The authors themselves write that further experiments and statistical checks are needed ([`p4-3`](#p4-3)).

**The causality is not established, and this is admitted.** In the models with an emergence era the difference between the synergistic subnetwork and its complement is small ([`p4-9`](#p4-9)); the section heading meanwhile poses the question of causality while the section's conclusion says "causally related" — the claim is stronger than what is shown.

**The subnetwork extraction method is fragile.** The features are binned into exactly 10 bins by agglomerative clustering at default settings ([`p2-6`](#p2-6)); both the exhaustive enumeration of sets and every synergy value depend on that number, yet the sensitivity to it was not checked.

**There is a single setting.** One two-layer network, one task, $p=97$, 40 % of the data, AdamW ([`p2-3`](#p2-3)). The influence of AdamW's internal regularisation is named by the authors themselves as a possible confound ([`p5-1`](#p5-1)).

**The text is unproofread in places.** In the section on the initialisation it says "we additionally train models at weight decays of 10 and 50" ([`p4-2`](#p4-2)) — evidently copied from the neighbouring paragraph, where weight decay really is at issue; further in the same paragraph alpha 1 and 5 are mentioned, whereas the caption of figure 5 gives 1, 8 and 50. The captions of figures 6 and 7 coincide word for word although the figures differ.

**The number of eras depends on the authors' own labelling.** The era boundaries are drawn by the appearance of the synergy and redundancy curves; no quantitative rule of division is given, and the whole analysis rests on it.

**Its place in the corpus.** The work is preliminary and knowingly small in scale, but it brings into the corpus something it did not have: a progress measure that computes the **jointness** of the neurons rather than their properties taken separately, and one tied neither to the task nor to a manual analysis of circuits. Three things are valuable: the method itself (the O-information as a progress measure); the division of the training into eras, in which the divergence and the delayed emergence are explained by a shortage of features to be joined; and the observation that an early peak of synergy precedes the grokking. The price: one tiny setting, a division into eras by eye, a prediction with no threshold and no lead, a causality that is only claimed, and a text with several obvious misprints.

## Common misreadings and overstatements

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text: places where this paper restates someone else's results with an error, or without the qualifications their authors attached. The "details" links in the "Common misreadings and overstatements of this paper" sections of the cited works' cards lead here.

###### mc-2210-01117
**Omnigrok (2210.01117) — a conflation of inducing and removing.** `"scaling the initialization weights of the model with a factor $\alpha>0$ while constraining the L2 weight norm to be constant during training can induce grokking"`. Two opposite instruments of the cited work are conflated in one phrase: grokking there is induced by the large initialisation on its own, whereas constraining the norm is a technique for *removing* it: ["training with a constrained weight norm is able to almost eliminate grokking"](../2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data/original/2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data.md#p1-9). The paper's own experiment is set up by the correct recipe — α=8 and zero weight decay, with no norm constraint — so the conflation lives only in the verbal description of somebody else's result; but in that form the recipe for inducing grokking is unreproducible.
