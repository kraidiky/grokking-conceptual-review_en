# Kam, Cadet, Bessafi, Cadet 2026 — Algebraic Representability as the Limiting Regime of Grokking: An Exactly Solvable Model with Holomorphic Activations

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Chon-Fai Kam** (University Paris City & University of Reunion; Università degli Studi di Palermo), **Xavier Cadet** (Dartmouth College), **Miloud Bessafi** (University of Reunion), **Frederic Cadet** (University Paris City & University of Reunion; PEACCEL).

arXiv [2607.13749](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13749), July 2026 (v1), 15 pages, 5 figures, 4 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. The subject is the limiting regime of the connection between capacity and grokking: a two-layer complex network with the holomorphic activation $\sigma(z)=z^{k}$ on an encoding by roots of unity has an expressible class algebraically locked into a $(k{+}1)$-dimensional subspace of the characters of $(\mathbb{Z}_{p})^{2}$ at any width. An exact Fourier criterion of representability is proved (a linear-phase target $ma+nb$ is representable ⟺ $m+n=k$), together with the non-representability of non-linear-phase targets via Gauss sums and a lower bound on the training loss that does not depend on the width — a prohibition on memorisation itself. 584 of the 585 runs agree with the algebraic prediction (99.8 %): the outcomes are binary — an instant success or a total failure, with no MEM and no GROK. The bridge to ordinary networks is a bottleneck in a ReLU network (FAIL → MEM → GROK with a shrinking gap) and an ablation of the encoding: with learnable embeddings the criterion widens into a rank barrier $\operatorname{rank}\leq k{+}1$, and only the full-rank task $ab$ remains non-representable.

## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the limiting regime of the capacity spectrum: before the race of the two time scales (the speeds of memorisation and of generalisation after Song and Ye) a precondition must hold — the memorisation must be possible; once the expressible class is squeezed down to an immovable algebraic object, the question "when will the network grok" degenerates into "can it represent the target at all", the outcomes are binary (74 INST / 510 FAIL, not a single MEM or GROK), and the bridge to ordinary networks is a bottleneck in a ReLU network: $d\leq 2$ — FAIL, $d\in\{4,8\}$ — MEM, $d\geq 16$ — GROK with a monotonically shrinking gap ([`p10-1`](#p10-1)). The analysis: the absence of MEM is declared a discovery, but it follows directly from lemma 2 — the 510 FAIL runs are one phenomenon counted 510 times, and the confusion matrix mixes what the theorem guarantees with what is measured; the single discrepancy ($a+5b$ at $k=6$) is removed by a change of protocol for one cell, yet entered the count of 99.8 % as an error — a double counting in the theory's favour; and the bridge is built on one task, three seeds and a budget that determines the MEM verdict.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the encoding by roots of unity as a natural parametrisation: $a\mapsto\omega^{a}$ turns addition mod $p$ into a multiplication of complex numbers, and harmonic analysis on $(\mathbb{Z}_{p})^{2}$ makes the expressible class fully explicit — $k+1$ characters $\chi_{\ell,k-\ell}$ on the diagonal $u+v\equiv k\pmod{p}$; the subtraction $a-b$ gives $(1,p-1)$ with the sum $\equiv 0$ and requires $k=p=97$ — practically non-representable; the rank barrier (proposition 1) does not depend on the basis: $\operatorname{rank}Y\leq k+1$ under any learnable embeddings, and $\omega^{ab}$ — a $\sqrt{p}$-fold DFT matrix of full rank — is non-representable under any encoding whatever ([`p5-11`](#p5-11)). The analysis: all the experiments are at the single $p=97$ and $k\leq 6$, there is no sweep over $p$ although all the criteria depend on it; proposition 1 is only a necessary condition, and sufficiency is shown only for rank-1 targets (the intermediate ranks $2..k{+}1$ are not checked); and how the complex output is decoded into a class of $\mathbb{Z}_{p}$ at a 99 % threshold is described only indirectly.

**[Fourier features and circuits](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — the architecture as a rigid band-pass filter in the Fourier domain: exactly $k+1$ frequencies of the set $S_{k}$ are let through, and growing the width neither moves nor widens the band — it only raises the amplitude resolution inside the channels; the non-linear-phase targets are non-representable geometrically — their Fourier support is either a line ($a^{2}+b$: the line $\{(u,1)\}$ via a Gauss sum) or the whole plane ($ab$: $|\widehat{\omega^{ab}}(u,v)|=1/p$ everywhere), and no single-point band can cover an extended support ([`p5-1`](#p5-1)). The analysis: the ReLU baseline on the same tasks gives a mirror picture — it fails not one of the tasks, and the targets that give the holomorphic network a FAIL give the purest grokking with a gap of 3700–7500 steps: the same arithmetical difficulty is a hard floor of representability in one class and a delay of a race of dynamics in the other; the conjecture of Doshi et al. about the division of modular polynomials into learnable and unlearnable ones has the same form as theorem 1, and its non-representable side is proved here (in the holomorphic setting).

**[Generalisation bounds](../../concepts/generalization-bounds.md)** — a lower bound on the TRAINING loss, a rarity for the corpus: theorem 3 gives $\min L\geq\delta^{2}-O(N^{-1/2})$ with probability $1-O(1/N)$, where $\delta^{2}$ is the out-of-band spectral energy of the target, and the bound does not depend on the width — a network with $H=10\,000$ behaves like one with $H=10$; for $ab$ the fraction of the energy in the band is $(k+1)/p^{2}<0.1\%$, that is, the loss is indistinguishable from that of an untrained network ([`p6-11`](#p6-11)). The analysis: the proof refers to an argument "in the appendix", but the paper has no appendix (15 pages, with the bibliography following section 6 directly) — the uniform bound over $g\in\mathcal{F}_{k}$ and the injectivity of the restriction to the sample remain a sketch; the notation $\delta^{2}$ is overloaded — in (6) it is the out-of-band energy at a fixed encoding, while in corollary 1 and the caption of fig. 5 the same symbol names the rank residue $1-(k+1)/p$; and in §4.5 "38 of 39 on every seed" argues with the paper's own numbers 186/190 and 177/190 plus six GROK cells.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong points — a new axis of analysis and a neat bridge.** "Representability against memorisation" is singled out as a separate axis preceding all the competitive explanations of grokking; the Fourier criterion is proved in both directions with explicit constructions (a Vandermonde matrix for the achievability); the rank barrier gives an encoding-independent form and an exact prediction for the ablation with learnable embeddings, which comes true (the ladder $m+n=k$ falls apart, $ab$ remains the only failure); the bottleneck traces FAIL → MEM → GROK with a shrinking gap, tying the algebraic limit to the capacity spectrum of Song and Ye; and the auxiliary nature of the holomorphic architecture is admitted outright.

**A phantom appendix, overloaded notation, and a recount of what is guaranteed.** The proof of theorem 3 refers to a non-existent appendix; $\delta^{2}$ denotes two different quantities; there are three internal disagreements of numbers (38/39 "on every seed" against 186/190; the 7000/5000 of the text against the 7250/5250 of the table); the 510 FAIL runs are a theoretical guarantee counted as 510 confirmations; the INST/GROK thresholds (<200 / >500 steps) are assigned by hand with an intermediate category "GROK?" in the figure; and all of it is at a single $p=97$.

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that grokking dissolves into representability") — more precisely: for one two-layer holomorphic architecture with the activation $z^{k}$ at $p=97$ a Fourier criterion of representability and a lower bound on the loss are proved, and the connection with ordinary networks is shown by a single empirical ablation (the bottleneck) on one task.
