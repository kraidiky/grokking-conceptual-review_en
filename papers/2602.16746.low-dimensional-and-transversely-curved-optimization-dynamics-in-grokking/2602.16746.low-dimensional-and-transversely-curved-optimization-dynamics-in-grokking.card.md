## Concepts covered

**[The loss landscape and basins](../../concepts/loss-landscape-basins.md)** — the work's central thesis: grokking is a **prolonged confinement** of the trajectory on a low-dimensional subspace, during which the transverse curvature accumulates as a barrier until the trajectory escapes into a generalising solution ([`p3-2`](#p3-2)). The picture "confinement → growth of the barrier → escape" unfolds into four phases of a dynamical system: a contraction onto a slow manifold, a transverse instability, a critical transition, and a rearrangement ([`p17-6`](#p17-6)).

**[The sparse subnetwork / lottery ticket](../../concepts/sparse-subnetwork-lottery-ticket.md)** — the low dimensionality here is measured rather than postulated: a PCA of the trajectories of the attention weights gives **68–83 % of the variance in a single principal component** in all 36 conditions ([`p6-7`](#p6-7)). The author calls this subspace an *execution manifold*. The controls without weight decay also give a high PC1 %, but a random-walk null model stands $5\text{–}20$ standard deviations away ([`p6-8`](#p7-1)), so the concentration is not an artefact of the smoothness of the trajectory.

**[Spectral analysis](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — the mechanism is found in the spectrum of the weights and set out as a **chronology**: first the lower gap $g_{23}$ contracts, then the leading modes become nearly degenerate ($\sigma_{1}\approx\sigma_{2}$), then the commutator defect rises sharply, then the matrix commutator $\Vert[W_{Q},W_{K}]\Vert_{F}$ peaks, then one mode begins to dominate, and only then does the test accuracy rise ([`p13-7`](#p13-7), [`p14-1`](#p14-1), [`fig-19`](#fig-19)). The near-degeneracy creates an **instability of orientation**: at close singular values the corresponding vectors are free to rotate, and different mini-batch orders push the basis in different directions ([`p14-7`](#p14-7)).

**[Causal interventions](../../concepts/causal-ablation-intervention.md)** — the work does not stop at correlation and checks three falsifiable hypotheses ([`p12-2`](#p12-2)). **Necessity is confirmed**: projecting the gradient onto the learned PCA directions at a strength $s=1.0$ kills the grokking in all 12 seeds across four operations, with a monotone dose response ([`p12-5`](#p12-5), [`fig-14`](#fig-14)). **Sufficiency is rejected**: directed nudges along the commutator with amplitudes up to $500\times$ the norm of a gradient step do not speed the grokking up in any of the 27 runs ([`p12-7`](#p12-7), [`fig-16`](#fig-16)). **Selectivity is confirmed**: random projections of the same dimension at intermediate strengths give nothing ([`fig-15`](#fig-15)).

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — the commutator defect as a quantity is elegantly and cheaply built: it measures the non-commutativity of two gradient steps over different mini-batches and to leading order is proportional to the Lie bracket of the gradient vector fields, **requiring no computation of a Hessian** ([`p5-1`](#p5-1), [`p5-4`](#p5-4)). The onset of its growth leads the generalisation by 600–1600 steps at $\eta=10^{-3}$ (a sign test with $p=2^{-12}$) ([`p9-2`](#p9-2)).

**[Phase transition](../../concepts/phase-transition.md)** — the warning window obeys a power law $\Delta t\propto t_{\mathrm{grok}}^{\mkern3mu\alpha}$ with $\alpha=1.27\pm 0.03$ ($R^{2}=0.97$, $n=43$) over a $300\times$ sweep of the learning rate ([`p11-4`](#p11-4), [`fig-11`](#fig-11)). Since $\alpha>1$, the window grows **super-linearly**: at $\eta=3\times 10^{-5}$ the defect begins within the first 5 % of the training.

**[The learning rate](../../concepts/learning-rate.md)** — the learning rate plays the role of **damping**, and this is the corpus's first isolation of three regimes: at small $\eta$ the motion is over-damped (an alignment of the trajectory with the curvature below the random level, the defect accumulating slowly), at large $\eta$ under-damped (an alignment above the level, a fast transition), and between them critically damped ([`p11-5`](#p11-5), [`p11-7`](#p11-7), [`fig-13`](#fig-13)). The "groks — does not grok" boundary is meanwhile **invariant** to the learning rate ([`p11-2`](#p11-2)).

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the setting is canonical (a two-layer transformer, $p=97$, a 50/50 train-test split, about 290 thousand parameters), with six operations of which four grok, while $a^{2}+ab+b^{2}$ and $a^{3}+ab$ never do ([`p4-1`](#p4-1), [`tab-1`](#tab-1)). These are the same two operations as in Furuta et al. — a coincidence the corpus would do well to keep in mind.

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — it serves here not as an explanation but as a missing ingredient: the same grokking operations without weight decay exhibit a comparable acceleration of the defect **without generalising** ([`p18-2`](#p18-2)). Hence a direct conclusion: a growth of the curvature alone is not enough, a regularisation pressure is needed too.

**[Mechanistic interpretability](../../concepts/mechanistic-interpretability.md)** — the connection is offered as an interpretation rather than shown: the formation of execution manifolds corresponds to a concentration of the computation in low-dimensional subspaces, and grokking "marks a transition from distributed representations to a circuit-like organisation" ([`p19-5`](#p19-5)). Not one circuit is isolated in the work.

**[Flat minima](../../concepts/goldilocks-zone.md)** — the same move explains the robustness to compression: after the grokking the curvature is locked into directions the optimiser does not visit, and the solution is therefore more robust to perturbations along the learned subspace ([`p19-2`](#p19-2)). This is an argument rather than a measurement: neither quantisation nor compression was checked in the work.

It is worth saying separately what the work does **not** contain. There is no predictor: the author writes outright that the available geometric signals **do not combine** into a composite indicator distinguishing grokking from non-grokking operations in advance ([`p18-2`](#p18-2)). Nor is there a theory: an analytical model of the accumulation of the defect and of the formation of the manifold is named an open problem ([`p19-8`](#p19-8)).

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the setting; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — the grokking circuits, named in the survey; [Omnigrok](../2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data/2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data.card.md) — the control of the weight norm; [Merrill et al. 2023](../2303.11873.a-tale-of-two-circuits-grokking-as-competition-of-sparse-and-dense-subnetworks/2303.11873.a-tale-of-two-circuits-grokking-as-competition-of-sparse-and-dense-subnetworks.card.md) and [Varma et al. 2023](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — the explanations through subnetworks and efficiency; [Kumar et al. 2024](../2310.06110.grokking-as-the-transition-from-lazy-to-rich-training-dynamics/2310.06110.grokking-as-the-transition-from-lazy-to-rich-training-dynamics.card.md) — the lazy-to-rich transition; [Thilak et al. 2022](../2206.04817.the-slingshot-mechanism-an-empirical-study-of-adaptive-optimizers-and-grokking/2206.04817.the-slingshot-mechanism-an-empirical-study-of-adaptive-optimizers-and-grokking.card.md) — the slingshot; [Grokking as a first-order phase transition](../2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks/2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks.card.md) — another work of the corpus reading grokking in the language of phase transitions.

**[The execution manifold](../../concepts/execution-manifold.md)** — the work gives the notion a name and a measure. What is at issue is not a subnetwork but the number of directions along which the trajectory moves: grokking corresponds to a *"prolonged confinement on a low-dimensional subspace of weight space"*, out of which the trajectory eventually emerges ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)). The difference from a sparse subnetwork is substantial — there one counts how many weights are needed, here how many degrees of freedom are engaged, and that quantity is measured by the fraction of variance on the first principal component ([`p6-7`](#p6-7)).

**[Criticality / the critical point](../../concepts/criticality-critical-point.md)** — the same picture without the word itself: the delay is described as a confinement in a **metastable** regime, and generalisation sets in when the trajectory leaves it ([`p3-2`](#p3-2)). The kinship with the language of critical points is substantive rather than nominal: no control parameter is named, no threshold is measured, and the work makes no claim about a critical exponent.

**[Negative results](../../concepts/negative-results.md)** — the work presents its negative result on a par with the positive ones: nudges along the commutator do not accelerate the generalisation, and therefore *"the accumulation of the defect alone is not sufficient to induce grokking"* ([`p12-7`](#p12-7)). The value lies in how it is formulated — not "no effect is visible" but "the effect falls within the seed-to-seed spread".

**[Seed variance and reproducibility](../../concepts/seed-variance-reproducibility.md)** — the spread serves here not as a caveat but as a threshold of significance: it is precisely through it that the negative result acquires its force ([`p12-7`](#p12-7)). The same spread props up the positive part too — the fraction of variance on the first component stays within 68–83 % in all 36 conditions ([`p6-7`](#p6-7)).
## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is unusual for the corpus in its construction: one measurable notion (the commutator defect), one geometric picture and — a rarity — a full set of interventions checking it causally.

**The causal part is the best thing in the work.** Three hypotheses are stated falsifiably and each is checked by its own experiment ([`p12-2`](#p12-2)). The answer turns out asymmetric and therefore substantive: an orthogonal gradient flow is **necessary** (suppressing it kills the grokking with a dose response) but **not sufficient** (nudges of up to $500\times$ give nothing). The negative result is given in full rather than hidden.

**A random-subspace control is obligatory and it is there.** In a space of some 290 thousand dimensions any $K$-dimensional subspace catches about $\sqrt{K/P}$ of a random vector, and without a comparison against a random basis a $\rho\approx 1.000$ would mean nothing ([`p6-3`](#p6-3)). The ratio of the execution to the random level of $1.8\text{–}2.9\times$ and a check of robustness to the choice of $K$ ([`p8-2`](#p8-2)) make the conclusion meaningful. The same control is applied to the projections, to a sign flip and to a reverse analysis — the methodological care here is above the corpus average.

**The invariance measure is empty in itself.** $\rho\approx 1.000$ in **all** 36 conditions, the non-grokking ones included ([`p7-2`](#p7-3)), and the author honestly writes that it is "identically 1" and therefore distinguishes nothing ([`p18-2`](#p18-2)). All the work is done by the ratio to the random level and by the magnitude of the defect.

**The word "invariance" is used with a caveat, and rightly so.** It is said outright: the invariance is assessed through near-orthogonality over finite windows rather than in the strict sense of dynamical systems, and the language of differential geometry is applied descriptively to a discrete stochastic optimisation ([`p6-2`](#p6-2)). Such a caveat is met with rarely in the corpus and deserves noting.

**The chronology is established statistically, but the separation is made after the fact.** The onset of the defect leads the grokking in all 12 runs ($p=2^{-12}$) ([`p9-2`](#p9-2)). The non-grokking operations, however, also give a growth of $30\text{–}50\times$, and although by **cumulative** magnitude the groups do not overlap ($513\times$ against $47\times$), at early steps the curves do overlap ([`p9-3`](#p9-3)). The author draws the right conclusion from this himself: the onset is a necessary precondition rather than a predictor.

**The power law is fitted over 43 points of one setting.** $\alpha=1.27\pm 0.03$ at $R^{2}=0.97$ ([`p11-4`](#p11-4)) is a good fit, but all the points are obtained on one architecture, one modulus and six values of $\eta$. The claim of a "super-linear growth of the warning window" holds within that sweep and is not checked outside it.

**The three damping regimes are the most notable new idea and the least supported place.** An over-damped, a critically damped and an under-damped regime are named ([`p11-7`](#p11-7)), and the difference is measured by a single quantity — the alignment of the trajectory with the curvature — on **two** operations and at **three** checkpoints ([`p11-5`](#p11-5)). The analogy with damping remains an analogy: no equations of motion are written out.

**The reliance on the author's own preprints calls for attention.** The work builds on Xu (2026a) and relates itself in detail to the intra-signal-gap frame of Xu (2026b), including references to its theorem 12.22 and remark 12.22 ([`p16-2`](#p16-2)). Both works are by the same author and are absent from the corpus, so part of the chain is unverifiable for a reader of the wiki.

**The scale of the setting is modest, and this is admitted.** Two- and three-layer transformers, about 290 thousand parameters, modular arithmetic modulo 97 ([`p19-6`](#p19-6)); a full reproduction takes about 9 hours on a single Apple M-series machine ([`p20-3`](#p21-2)). Preliminary experiments on Dyck languages and SCAN are mentioned as ongoing, that is, they are not in the paper.

**Its place in the corpus.** The work gives the corpus a cheap and reproducible quantity — the commutator defect — which detects a rearrangement of the landscape hundreds and thousands of steps before anything is visible in the accuracy. Its main contribution lies not in explanation but in **discrimination**: it is shown that a growth of the curvature is necessary and not sufficient, and hence that explanations of grokking by the geometry of the landscape alone are knowingly incomplete without a regularisation pressure.
