# Zhang, Chan, Shang, Zhang, Yang 2026 — The Grokked Illusion: True Equilibrium Mitigates Catastrophic Forgetting

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Xiaotian Zhang**, **Lai Shun Chan**, **Yue Shang**, **Ge Zhang** (City University of Hong Kong; University of Pennsylvania), **Entao Yang** (Innovation Campus Delaware, Air Liquide).

arXiv [2607.29503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29503), July 2026 (v1), 9 pages, 1 composite figure (4 files), 1 table. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. The subject is whether the "advantage of high entropy" carries over from generalisability to robustness: two networks with the same weight norm ($\Vert w\Vert=30$, the Goldilocks zone) and the same 100 % test accuracy on $x+y\bmod 67$ — one trained by AdamW, the other selected by Wang–Landau molecular dynamics as a maximum-entropy equilibrium — are further trained to memorise 500 new noisy examples while the original data are kept in the training. The AdamW network falls from 100 % to ~75 % test accuracy on the original task while the equilibrium one holds ~95 % ("the grokked illusion"); the gap narrows monotonically as the noise draws structurally closer to the original task. The mechanism of the advantage is explained through the effective rank: in the equilibrium network it is far higher in the attention and MLP matrices both before and after the injection ($W_{Q}$: 96.8 against 2.2), serving as a representational reserve.

## Concepts covered

**[Catastrophic forgetting](../../concepts/catastrophic-forgetting.md)** — a new experimental design: a network that has already mastered a task is required to **fully** memorise 500 new noisy examples in addition (a threshold of 99.8 % training accuracy on the mixture), with the original training data remaining in the training and being fitted at 100 %; at an equal "volume of what has been learned" AdamW loses a quarter of its test accuracy (100 % → ~75 % on random noise) while the equilibrium network holds ~95 %, and the gap narrows along the ladder of structural closeness ($x^{2}+y\bmod 37$: 84 % against >98 %; $x+y\bmod 37$: 97 % against ~100 %) ([`p3-10`](#p3-10)). The analysis: "catastrophic forgetting" is a stretch here — what collapses is the generalisation at a preserved fit to the training data, not the memory of the training sample (the authors stress this but do not change the term); it is not said whether the constraint $\Vert w\Vert=30$ holds during the injection — if it does not, what is being compared is no longer states at an equal norm; and the design itself (an injection of noise with the original data retained) is a ready test of the quality of a solution, independent of how that solution was obtained, and it is the most valuable thing in the work.

**[Effective theory and statistical mechanics](../../concepts/effective-theory-statistical-mechanics.md)** — the Boltzmann entropy of a network state $S=\log V(\mathcal{L}_ {\text{train}},\mathcal{A}_ {\text{test}})$ as a global measure of volume in parameter space: the equilibrium states are selected by Wang–Landau molecular dynamics ($1.2\times 10^{8}$ sampling epochs to the convergence of the histogram) on the hypersphere $\Vert w\Vert=30$, and the claimed "advantage of high entropy" is extended from generalisability to robustness — a large basin volume lets the parameters shift a long way and still remain within the same high-entropy region ([`p2-4`](#p2-4)). The analysis: the link "a larger volume → the shift stays inside → the function is preserved" is declared a hypothesis in the introduction, and the volume after the injection is not measured — there is no intervention testing it; the costs are incommensurable ($1.2\times 10^{8}$ WLMD epochs against 120 AdamW epochs), and the question "what would ordinary training give at an equal compute budget" is not posed; and the supporting work on the advantage of high entropy is by the same group, as is the predecessor 2505.11411, so that the key support remains a self-citation.

**[Spectral analysis](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — the effective rank (the exponential of the entropy of the normalised spectrum of singular values, Roy & Vetterli) as the explanation of the robustness: in the equilibrium network it is far higher before the injection in all four attention matrices and both MLP layers ($W_{Q}$ 96.8 against 2.2, $W_{K}$ 97.4 against 7.2, $W_{\text{in}}$ 116.1 against 48.1), and after the injection it falls in both — **more in absolute terms** in the equilibrium one, at that — but the residual level is still higher, which is presented as the "representational reserve" that absorbs the new data ([`p4-2`](#p4-2)). The analysis: the exceptions are stated honestly — at the unembedding the levels before the injection are close, and afterwards the equilibrium one is **lower**, while at the embeddings there is almost no advantage; the link between the ER and the robustness remains correlational (admitted, with the interventions deferred to future work); and the corpus's line runs opposite in sign — in 2310.05918 and 2604.13123 the same quantity measures the progress towards generalisation and it is its **fall** that is counted a good, whereas here a **high** level is declared the good, and there is no direct argument in the text.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the "grokked illusion": solutions with the same saturated test accuracy on a task where grokking is usually observed differ in fragility, and by the test metrics that difference is invisible — a perfect generalisation does not entail an equal robustness ([`p2-2`](#p2-2)). The analysis: grokking itself as a delayed generalisation is not measured in the work at all — there is not a single test-accuracy curve over epochs before the injection and not a single sign of a delay; the notion is redefined along the way ("solutions appear to grok (saturated test performance)") contrary to Power et al., on whom the work leans; and the AdamW network being compared was obtained under a fixed norm in 120 epochs, whereas by Omnigrok it is precisely landing in the Goldilocks zone that removes the delay — the "grokkedness" of that state is not shown, it is called grokked by its final accuracy, and the implication of Omnigrok is never discussed.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong point — a design that distinguishes what the test cannot.** An injection of noise with the original data kept in the training separates two solutions identical in weight norm and test accuracy — a ready test of the "quality" of a solution, independent of how it was obtained; the ladder of the structural closeness of the noise gives a monotone trend rather than a single contrast; the cosine proximity of the parameters uncovers a counter-move — the equilibrium network shifts further in parameter space ($W_{Q}$: 0.8383 against 0.9964) and yet forgets less; and the layer-wise exceptions (the unembedding, the embeddings) are not hidden.

**There is no appendix, there is one network of each kind, and the numbers disagree.** The appendix is promised four times (the full spectra, the results of AdamW-standard and AdamW-full, the hardware, the hyperparameters) but is absent in v1 — two of the four claimed settings are shown nowhere; one pre-trained network of each kind is compared (the ten seeds vary only the injection, and there is neither a spread over trained networks nor a repetition of the sampling); "about 95 %" in the abstract against "approximately 98 %" in the introduction for the same condition; "similar training loss" at $\ln\mathcal{L}\approx-1$ against $\approx-2$ (a factor of three); the statement about the cosine proximity ("higher for AdamW") is refuted by the paper's own table in three parameter groups out of nine; and the arithmetic of the vocabulary does not add up (the indices 68…104 are 37 positions against the 38 symbols named).

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that grokked models are fragile") — more precisely: on a single modular addition, with one pair of networks and three kinds of noise, it is shown that a state selected by the maximum of the entropy loses less test accuracy than an AdamW state at an equal weight norm; and the section on the implications for large language models leans on six references to agentic benchmarks with a single checked modular task, while the authors' caveat "remains to be tested at LLM scale" will be lost at the first citation.
