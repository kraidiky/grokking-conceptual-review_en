# Chan, Zhang, Shang, Zhang, Yang 2026 — Tunneling the Loss Landscape: Bypassing Memorization with Monte Carlo Parameter Swapping

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Lai Shun Chan**, **Xiaotian Zhang**, **Yue Shang**, **Ge Zhang** (City University of Hong Kong; University of Pennsylvania), **Entao Yang** (Innovation Campus Delaware, Air Liquide).

arXiv [2608.01833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01833), August 2026 (v1), 9 pages, 2 figures, 1 algorithm. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 0. The subject is a direct test of the glassy picture of grokking on the training dynamics itself: a triple of measures (the parameter mobility PM, the inter-trajectory replica correlation RC and the fractal dimension of the trajectory FD) shows that after 99 % on the training set is reached the mobility collapses by orders of magnitude, the response to a perturbation becomes history-dependent (ageing), and the trajectory contracts into an almost ballistic channel with $D_{f}\approx 1$ — that is, memorisation is a glass-like kinetic arrest. Hence the intervention SAM-Swap: a random exchange of parameter values within a layer, triggered by an FD threshold, which cuts the grokking time from $t\approx 3000$ to $t\approx 650$ epochs.

## Concepts covered

**[The memorisation phase](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — the plateau receives a dynamical diagnosis instead of a descriptive one: exactly when 99 % training accuracy is reached the fractal dimension of the trajectory collapses to $D_{f}\approx 1$, that is, the path in weight space becomes straight and channel-like, while the mobility falls from $\sim 1$ to $\sim 10^{-2}$ and on to $\sim 10^{-4}$; at the same time the replica correlation levels off — the network is not merely moving slowly but moving in a narrow corridor with no branchings available ([`p4-3`](#p4-3)). The analysis: the observation $D_{f}\to 1$ at the memorisation era itself is the most valuable thing in the work, but both glassy measures exist only in a "probed" network (a Gaussian noise $\sigma=10^{-5}$ injected into every parameter), without which $RC\equiv 1$ and $D_{f}$ is trivially 1 — what is measured is the response to an injected noise rather than the intrinsic dynamics of AdamW; and the RC plateau lies at $q\approx 0.97$, with the whole main panel occupying 0.96–1.00, so the "two-step relaxation" plays out within three per cent of the quantity.

**[Effective theory and statistical mechanics](../../concepts/effective-theory-statistical-mechanics.md)** — the glassy measures are carried over into parameter space literally: RC as the Pearson correlation of the vectorised layer weights of replicas budded off a single state at a time $t_{\rm w}$, and FD from the power relation $\Delta R^{2}(\tau)\propto\Delta s(\tau)^{\alpha}$, $D_{f}=2/\alpha$; ageing is measured directly — for replicas at $t_{w}=10^{2},10^{3}$ the first decay is suppressed and the correlation stays near unity, that is, the longer the system has lived the less it responds to a perturbation ([`p4-2`](#p4-2)). The analysis: the "quantitative agreement" with glassy dynamics claimed in the abstract is not backed by a single fit to a glassy law — there is no relaxation time $\tau_{\alpha}$, no stretched exponential, no Arrhenius or VFT dependence and no temperature, so the agreement remains a similarity in the shape of the curves; and the ageing is measured at a constant absolute $\sigma=10^{-5}$ while the weight norm grows, so the relative strength of the perturbation falls of its own accord, with no separating check.

**[The role of gradient noise](../../concepts/gradient-noise.md)** — a comparison of four settings on a deliberately deterministic base (a full batch, no dropout): the reference Adam+probe, AdamW+probe, Adam with a large Gaussian noise ($\sigma=10^{-2}$) and Adam with the exchange; all three perturbed settings reach generalisation earlier than the reference, and the conclusion is generalised to the claim that the speed-up is invariably tied to a random exploration of parameter space, "like diffusion in physics" ([`p5-5`](#p5-5)). The analysis: in figure 2a plain large Gaussian noise generalises earlier than the proposed SAM-Swap, and this is nowhere noted in the text; the reference (i) does not reach generalisation at all within 10000 epochs, so the formula "(ii), (iii) and (iv) generalise earlier than the reference" conceals the fact that the reference never generalises; weight decay is included among the "perturbations" although it is not random; and the closing link "diffusion raises the entropy, the entropy is tied to generalisation" remains an argument — neither the entropy nor a diffusion coefficient is measured here.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the delay is declared a property of the trajectory rather than of the landscape: by the reference work the generalising solutions are abundant and available, but the optimisation cannot reach them, and so the time to generalisation depends on which regions of parameter space the dynamics is able to visit rather than on which solutions exist ([`p7-3`](#p7-3)). The analysis: the title introduces a barrier through which one has to "tunnel", whereas both the reference work and the text itself hold that there are no macroscopic barriers and the delay is purely kinetic — the word "tunneling" does not occur once in the body of the paper; the hyperparameters of the reference curve are tuned for the shortest grokking, so the AdamW reference is already the best available rather than a typical one; and everything is shown on one task $(x^{2}+y)\bmod 67$ with one single-layer transformer.

**[Accelerating grokking](../../concepts/accelerated-grokking.md)** — the speed-up is achieved by destroying part of what has been learned: the exchange intervention substantially shortens the grokking time ([`p4-9`](#p4-9)). This is an argument for the picture of a kinetic arrest — if the delay were an accumulation of something useful, corrupting the state would lengthen it.

**[Grokking time](../../concepts/grokking-time.md)** — the result is reported in epochs: about $650$ against roughly $3000$ for AdamW ([`p4-9`](#p4-9)). The comparison is worth reading with the correction that the reference curve is tuned for the shortest grokking.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong point — three measures separating "slow" from "locked".** The formulation of the frame is strict: a low PM means nothing by itself (the model may crawl because of the learning-rate schedule), a high FD means nothing by itself (in the non-generalising Adam+probe it is the highest, up to 2.0), and a low RC means nothing by itself (it can be zeroed by resetting the weights, with no generalisation to follow) — only the combination is meaningful, and both counterexamples are given by the authors themselves. Replicas as a working instrument for measuring the history dependence of the training, and an exchange that preserves the distribution of values within a layer, are transferable techniques.

**The comparison is inhomogeneous, and there is no appendix.** The exchange is applied to Adam without weight decay yet compared with AdamW; the numbers $t\approx 650$ and $t\approx 3000$ come with no spread over the 20 runs, and the error bars on the mobility and FD panels are removed "for readability"; the rollback threshold $L_{\text{cap}}$ is taken equal to the loss at $t=0$, that is, it is knowingly enormous — almost nothing will ever be rejected; neither the frequency of invoking the exchange nor a schedule appears in the algorithm, while in figure 2 the FD of the perturbed runs stays right at the threshold of 1.1 to the end of the training, so that the "state-dependent" trigger ceases to be selective once it has fired. Twice the reader is referred to a "technical supplement" that is not in the submission. The range of lags is declared to be $\tau=1,\ldots,[t/2]$ and it is said immediately afterwards that every lag fits entirely inside a window $T=20$: at $t\gg 40$ these are incompatible. In the section on the geometry it is said at once that the late growth of the FD reflects the presence of several nearby minima and that the system is strongly locked in a single valley "like a trap in a sharp minimum" — and a "sharp minimum" contradicts the reference work, in which the generalising solutions occupy the largest volume.

**An unnoticed corollary of the paper's own construction.** An exchange within a layer preserves the set of the layer's values exactly, and hence its norm too: a speed-up of the generalisation with no change of the weight norm is a ready separating test for the line "the norm sets the time" (2210.01117, 2606.13753, 2606.18465). The authors did not notice this and did not measure it.

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that grokking is a glassy dynamics"; "SAM-Swap speeds grokking up fivefold") — more precisely: on one task at $p=67$, on a single-layer transformer, in a network into which noise is necessarily injected, three measures behave similarly to glassy ones, and the proposed exchange beats AdamW but loses to plain large noise.
