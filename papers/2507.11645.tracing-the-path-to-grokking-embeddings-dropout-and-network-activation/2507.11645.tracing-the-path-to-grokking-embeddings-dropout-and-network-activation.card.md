## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the paper's claim: several practical metrics that predict and analyse delayed generalisation on modular addition ([`p2-3`](#p2-3)); together with [2411.05353](../2411.05353.controlling-grokking-with-nonlinearity-and-data-symmetry/2411.05353.controlling-grokking-with-nonlinearity-and-data-symmetry.card.md) it forms the "control and foresee" pair of the Waterloo line. The substantive conclusion: the bimodality of the embeddings at ±0.4 depends neither on the scale nor on the profile of the initialization — "grokking from the symmetries of the data rather than from overtraining" ([`p8-1`](#p8-1)).

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — five precursor quantities: a local maximum of the variance of the test accuracy over 100 stochastic passes with dropout 0.3 at the threshold of generalisation ([`p3-4`](#p3-4), [`fig-1`](#fig-1)); the dropout robustness curve (DRC) — after the generalisation the test accuracy is insensitive to dropout up to 0.2 ([`p4-2`](#p4-2), [`fig-2`](#fig-2)); the codiagonals of the cosine proximity of the embeddings, visible long before the grokking ([`p5-3`](#p5-3), [`fig-3`](#fig-3)); a contraction of wide initial distributions at the start of training (related to Notsawo et al., [`p8-2`](#p8-2)); and the fraction of inactive neurons ([`p12-2`](#p12-2)). None of them is carried through to a predictor of the timing with a number.

**[Structured representations](../../concepts/structured-representation-learning.md)** — the same thought as in Liu et al. (2205.10343) in different dress: instead of circular PCA patterns — the codiagonals of the cosine proximity and a bimodal distribution of the embeddings with peaks around ±0.4, arising synchronously ([`p5-3`](#p5-3), [`p6-2`](#p6-2), [`fig-4`](#fig-4)). The cut-off: with the embeddings frozen the distribution stays Gaussian, the proximity map unstructured, and the grokking is suppressed ([`p7-1`](#p7-1)).

**[The initialization scale](../../concepts/initialization-scale.md)** — a detailed study of the Omnigrok axis: the grokking delay grows almost linearly with the scale factor ([`p8-1`](#p8-1), [`fig-5`](#fig-5)); moderate positive shifts of the initialization shorten the delay while an excessive one suppresses the test accuracy ([`p10-2`](#p10-2), [`fig-9`](#fig-9)); shifting the initialization of a single layer (the embeddings or the remaining weights) markedly strengthens the delay ([`p12-1`](#p12-1), [`fig-10`](#fig-10)); with constant embeddings and ordinary remaining weights training proceeds, and the converse does not.

**[Sparse subnetwork and the lottery ticket](../../concepts/sparse-subnetwork-lottery-ticket.md)** — the fraction of inactive neurons falls during memorisation and grows during generalisation: the optimizer finds symmetries that reduce the effective degrees of freedom ([`p12-2`](#p12-2), [`fig-11`](#fig-11)) — a direct support for the reading of Merrill et al. about a dense subnetwork passing into a sparse one.

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — the minimum of the fraction of inactive neurons comes earlier at a larger weight decay, which agrees with the known dependence of the delay on the decay ([`p13-1`](#p13-1)); and the magnitude of the embedding peaks falls as the weight decay grows ([`p7-1`](#p7-1)).

**[Regularisation variants](../../concepts/regularization-variants.md)** — dropout as a measuring instrument rather than a lever: MC dropout gives the precursor variance ([`p3-4`](#p3-4)), and the DRC gives robustness to structural perturbations as a new axis of diagnosis ([`p4-2`](#p4-2)), complementing the input robustness of 2311.06597.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong point is the cut-off with frozen embeddings.** If the embedding weights are not trained, the distribution stays Gaussian, the proximity map unstructured and the grokking suppressed — the necessity of training the embeddings in this setting is shown directly. The bimodality is robust: a ReLU added after the embeddings shifts the distribution into the positive range at the same distance between the peaks — "the structure is intrinsic to the learning rather than an artefact of the activation".

**No seeds and no error bars.** As in 2411.05353, all the curves are single runs; and the "precursors" are nowhere checked for a lead with a number: there is not a single prediction of a time with an error. By 2025 the ground of "metrics that predict grokking" is already densely occupied by the corpus's line of precursors with numbers (2306.13253, 2606.12966, 2604.20923, 2602.16967) — and there is no comparison with them.

**Internal roughnesses of the recovered text.** The numbering of the cases in §3.3 is out of step (the "cases 0 to 3" are enumerated, but the red line is called "case 4"); in §3.2 the proximity map of fig. 3 is twice called "Fig. 1(a)/(b)"; the bibliography is duplicated ([3] and [12] are one work by Varma et al.); the caption of fig. 8 refers to "same as in Figure 5" while by content it continues fig. 6; and the self-citation [4] carries a name error ("Yevick et al." instead of Salah & Yevick).

**The authors' hedges.** "Could have significant practical implications" for large networks is an argument; the peaks at ±0.4 "presumably intrinsic to the symmetries" of the data is a supposition on which the conclusion "grokking from symmetries" is built.

**How the work will be cited** ("metrics that predict grokking are proposed") — more precisely: on single runs of one task it is shown that five quantities correlate in time with the threshold of generalisation; none is carried through to a predictor of the timing with a number, and there is no comparison with the corpus's existing precursors.
