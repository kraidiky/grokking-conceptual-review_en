# Xu 2026 — Global Low-Rank, Local Full-Rank: The Holographic Encoding of Learned Algorithms

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Yongzhong Xu** (independent; the code: github.com/skydancerosel/holographic_encoding).

arXiv [2602.18649](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18649), February 2026, 14 pages, 3 figures, 6 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 0, in the corpus — 1. A sole-author work of the Xu line (2602.16746, 2602.16967, 2602.18523): on multi-task modular arithmetic (addition, multiplication and $x^{2}+y^{2}$ modulo 97, a shared trunk with three heads; 3 sizes × 5 values of weight decay) three ways of reconstructing the model from a compressed weight change are compared — a layer-wise SVD, a joint SVD and a PCA of the trajectory. A "principle of holographic encoding" is claimed: a grokked solution is globally low-rank in the space of training directions (3–5 trajectory components) and locally full-rank in parameter space (80–100+ singular components per matrix). The cleanest observation: spectral energy is no substitute for functional compressibility — the functional part lies in the tail of the spectrum, and weight decay drives it deeper without changing the shape of the spectrum.

## Concepts covered

**[Spectral analysis](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — the central measurement move: the rank that accumulates 90 % of the Frobenius energy of $\Delta W_{i}$ (44–52 % of the full rank) is set against the rank at which the accuracy is recovered (80–100+) — at a rank of about 50 some ${\sim}88$% of the energy is captured while the accuracy is near chance ([`p5-4`](#p5-4), [`fig-2`](#fig-2)). The conclusion: the concentration of spectral energy will not do as a substitute for functional compressibility, since the functional components lie in the tail of the spectrum. The analysis notes: all the trunk matrices were truncated simultaneously and to a single rank, there is no experiment with a single matrix truncated, and so the accumulation of error from the full-rankness of each matrix is not separated out.

**[The manifold and the compression of representations](../../concepts/manifold-representation-compression.md)** — the claimed duality: the training proceeds along a low-dimensional invariant subspace (the first trajectory component explains 90–97 % of the variance; $k^{ * }=2\text{–}6$ components suffice for 95 % of the reference accuracy, [`p4-6`](#p4-6)), while the finished weights do not compress to a low rank, whence a warning for LoRA-like methods on grokked networks ([`p11-5`](#p11-5)). The analysis undermines the second half of the conclusion: one "trajectory component" is a full parameter vector of the trunk, so that an "extremely compressed representation of 3–5 components" is five times larger than the model itself, and figure 3 shows the opposite of its own caption.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the work rests entirely on grokked runs (all 15 conditions grok, and weight decay speeds the transition up from ${\sim}157$ thousand to ${\sim}25$ thousand steps in the base model, [`p3-6`](#p3-6)), but there is not a single training curve in the paper, the memorisation phase is not shown, and — most importantly — there is not one control with a non-grokking model (neither $\lambda=0$, nor random labels, nor an early checkpoint), so the "holographic" property is not separated from a property of any trained network, and the connection with grokking remains a claim.

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — a transferable observation of "the same energy, a different functionality": the spectral entropy of $\Delta W$ stays around 0.87 at every $\lambda$, yet the recovery accuracy at rank 64 falls from ${\sim}70$% at $\lambda=0.1$ to ${\sim}5$% at $\lambda=1.0$ — a stronger weight decay does not change the distribution of the energy over the singular values but drives the functional part of the computation deeper into the tail of the spectrum ([`p11-1`](#p11-1)); at the same time it compresses the trajectory into fewer components.

**[The loss landscape](../../concepts/loss-landscape-basins.md)** — a geometric formulation of the "manifold vs. fiber" duality: the control variables define a low-dimensional manifold in parameter space along which the solution lies, while at each of its points the solution occupies a full-dimensional fibre in the space of the individual matrices ([`p9-6`](#p9-6)). The low dimensionality of the trajectory is inherited from 2602.16746 — but there it was supported by a random-walk null model and a control on a random subspace, whereas here there is neither.

**[The phase diagram](../../concepts/phase-diagram.md)** — the "phase structure" here refers to the regimes of the trajectory rather than to regions of the hyperparameter space ([`p3-6`](#p3-6)). The word is the same, the subject is different, and the conflation of these two senses is a usual source of confusion in the corpus.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The cleanest thing — "energy against function".** The separate measurement of the concentration of the Frobenius energy and of the recovery of the accuracy (at rank ~50 some ~88 % of the energy is captured while the accuracy is far from the reference; the recovery begins around ranks 80–100) is a transferable quantity independent of the paper's contestable constructions. The second transferable point is that weight decay changes not the shape of the spectrum (the entropy is ≈0.87 throughout) but the depth at which the functional part lies. The heads, meanwhile, compress completely (>99 % at rank 64) — the incompressibility is a property of the shared trunk.

**The selectivity experiment contradicts both its own exposition and the main thesis.** It is carried out in weight space — the leading eigenvectors of the gradient covariance over the parameters are subtracted from the final weight vector — but it is expounded as a separability "in activation space"; there is not a single quantity measured on activations in the paper. Worse: the gradient-covariance directions are computed from the final model, that is, statically, and they isolate the task-specific structure almost perfectly (SI ≈ 0.99) — which is incompatible with the thesis that "no static decomposition isolates task-specific structure". The "contradiction of separability" is created by comparing two static decompositions, one of which works.

**Figure 3 shows the opposite of its caption.** The caption promises that the PCA of the trajectory gives >95 % at "orders of magnitude fewer parameters", yet in all three panels the trajectory-PCA points lie to the right of the layer-wise SVD — they spend more parameters: one trajectory component is a full parameter vector of the trunk. With this the "extremely compressed representation" of section 6.2 collapses too. The trajectory-PCA reconstruction is itself close to a tautology: $\Delta W_{\text{final}}$ enters the matrix from which the components are built, and there are no null models (components of another run, a random subspace of the same dimension). The joint SVD is built questionably — matrices of different shapes are stretched into rows and padded with zeros, the rank is at most $n$, and the column "Joint ($r=n$)" tautologically coincides with the reference accuracy.

**The numbers of the introduction do not agree with the tables.** The "mean accuracy ≈1 %" for the layer-wise SVD in fact refers to the joint one (table 4 gives 0.033–0.869); ">95 % on 3–5 components" against 0.574 at $k=3$ (the base model, $\lambda=0.3$); "a selectivity >0.98 and an overlap <0.08" against the 0.97 and 0.120 of the tables; the "trunk-interior selectivity index 0.65–0.88" is defined nowhere; and the text of 3.2 disagrees with its own table 3 and figure 2. With the previous work of the same series (2602.18523) the numbers are incompatible on the very same runs: there a reconstruction from PC1–PC3 gave chance-level accuracy, here Traj($k=3$) gives 0.574–0.993.

**The five corollaries (interpretability, LoRA, emergence, safety, continual learning) are arguments**: not one is checked by a single experiment. The number of seeds is never once stated; every table carries one number per condition.

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that LoRA does not work on grokked networks", "circuits are stored holographically") — more precisely: on three transformers of up to 2.2 million parameters, on modular arithmetic modulo 97, with all the trunk matrices truncated simultaneously to a common rank and without a single control with a non-grokking model, it is shown that the accuracy collapses before the Frobenius energy is exhausted.
