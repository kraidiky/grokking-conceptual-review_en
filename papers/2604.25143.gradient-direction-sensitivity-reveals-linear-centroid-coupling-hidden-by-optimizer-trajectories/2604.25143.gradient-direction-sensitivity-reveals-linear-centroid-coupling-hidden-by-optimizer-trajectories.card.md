# Xu 2026 — Gradient-Direction Sensitivity Reveals Linear-Centroid Coupling Hidden by Optimizer Trajectories

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Yongzhong Xu** (independent author; the code — github.com/skydancerosel/grokking-integrability).

arXiv [2604.25143](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25143), April 2026 (v1), 15 pages, 5 figures, 5 tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 1, in the corpus — 0. A single-author methodological correction by the author to his own "Spectral Edge Thesis" (2603.28964): the sliding SVD of the AdamW *updates* (update SED) systematically misses the directions of feature formation, and the right object is the sliding SVD of the *gradients* (gradient SED), decomposed by task in the multi-task setting. The coupling is measured through the linear centroid hypothesis (LCH) of Walker et al.: the ratio $R_{k}$ of the centroid response along the SED direction to that along a random direction grows from 3–9 (update) to 110–330 (gradient) in the single-task setting and from $\leq 1$ to 20–45 in the multi-task one. The causal intervention yields a clean negative result: restricting the attention update to *any* rank-3 (SED or random) speeds grokking up by about 2.3 times — the speed-up is set by the rank, not by the direction.

## Concepts covered

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the transition is taken from Power et al. as given and serves as the background: the curves are aligned against it, and its time is the measure of the response in the intervention; the main causal outcome is that restricting the attention update to any rank-3 roughly halves the grokking step ($C/A=2.20\times$, $E/A=2.36\times$ under a gradient projection), while removing a rank-3 (SED or random) does not hinder grokking; at $\lambda=0$ not one of the conditions groks within 8000 steps ([`p11-2`](#p11-2)). The analysis: the fraction of the training sample is not given anywhere — for a work whose measured quantity is the grokking step this is a substantial omission; the A controls in tables 4 and 5 disagree on the same seeds (3450/3025 against 3525/3000) with no explanation, and it is precisely on the difference $B/A$ that the conclusion about an artefact rests; works on speeding grokking up (GrokFast and others) are not mentioned, although the main practical result is a twofold speed-up.

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — a reversal the paper itself does not notice: under update SED the peak of $R_{k}$ falls in the pre-grokking band and collapses at the transition (that is, the old "wrong" measure worked as a progress measure), whereas under gradient SED the peak of 110–330 persists after grokking too — the new "right" measure ceases to be a progress measure ([`p4-6`](#p4-6)). The analysis: the only quantity tied to the transition event itself is the rank-90 of the centroid matrix (a drop of $60{+}\to 20$–$50$), which is the same under both diagnostics and is in no way related to the main measure $R_{k}$; this contrast is not discussed in the paper.

**[Spectral analysis (SVD/ESD/FVE)](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — a direct trial of two spectral estimators on the same checkpoints, probes and perturbation: the SVD of a window of differences $\Delta_{t}$ against the SVD of a window of gradients $g(t)$ over the attention parameters ($P_{\text{attn}}=131{,}072$; $W=20$, $K=3$); the ablation over $W$ shows that at $W=1$ the basis degenerates into the instantaneous gradient with a ceiling of $R_{1}=648$, and the sliding window merely singles out the temporarily stable part of that signal at the price of a monotone decrease of $R_{1}$ ([`p5-1`](#p5-1)). The analysis: the author himself admits the quasi-tautology — the gradient $\nabla_{\theta}L$ and the centroid $\nabla_{x}\ell_{y}$ are built out of the same Jacobians, so that a perturbation along the gradient moves the centroids "mechanically" by the chain rule; in a 131072-dimensional space the random denominator of $R_{k}$ is small by construction; the caption of table 2 declares the $W$ axis monotone, yet $W=10$ gives 448 against 394 at $W=5$ — the non-monotonicity is masked by a footnote about a different measured quantity.

**[Optimiser (Adam/AdamW/SGD)](../../concepts/optimizer-adam-adamw-sgd.md)** — four sources of contamination of the spectral direction by the AdamW update relative to the raw gradient: the momentum (mixes in the past dozens of steps), the elementwise adaptive scaling (erases the relative magnitudes of the coordinates), the weight decay (gives a $\theta$-collinear drift) and, in the multi-task setting, the mutual cancellation of the four gradients, which leaves a compromise direction orthogonal to the feature subspace of any single task — on a multi-task transformer update SED gives $R_{k}\in[0.1,0.9]$ throughout the run even though all four operations grok ([`p9-2`](#p9-2)). The analysis: the decomposition into four sources is given by argument, the contribution of each separately is not measured; the claim of being "four to five times larger than the strongest single-task value" compares the multi-task gradient SED with the single-task update SED — under a homogeneous comparison the multi-task 20–45 is an order of magnitude below the single-task 110–330.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong points — the self-correction and a clean negative result.** The author publicly corrects his own earlier diagnostic ([3], 2603.28964), declaring it an artefact of the estimator, and honestly states the $W=1$ ceiling: the sensitivity of the centroids is set by the direction of the gradient as such, and the "spectral edge" carries no load in the single-task setting — only the multi-task result is declared load-bearing (the per-op decomposition as the cure for gradient cancellation). The intervention gives a clean negative result, rare for the corpus: the direction of the subspace does not matter, the rank does; the standard full-rank attention update is redundant.

**A measure with a small denominator, and internal inconsistencies.** $R_{k}$ is a ratio to the median over 20 random directions in a 131072-dimensional space, where a random direction is nearly orthogonal to any meaningful subspace; the range of the main result is named differently in different places (the abstract — "100–650", the text — "110–330"); the spread across operations is "$3.0\times$" in the text against "$1.82\times$" in the caption of the same table; the limitations say that $W$ and $K$ are "not formally ablated", although table 2 ablates exactly those; "statistically indistinguishable" — with no criterion and no intervals, on three seeds.

**A decorative bibliography.** Half of the reference list is never cited in the text: Power et al., Nanda et al., Cohen et al., Tian, Loshchilov–Hutter stand in the list without a single anchor; the founding work on grokking is not mentioned in the body although the whole setting is taken from it; 2 of the 10 sources are self-citations.

**How the work will be cited** ("a rank restriction speeds grokking up twofold"; "the gradient SED is strongly coupled to the LCH features") — more precisely: at $p=97$, on one architecture, three seeds, with a projection of the attention only and at $\lambda=1.0$, restricting the update to *any* rank-3 roughly halves the grokking step, while at $\lambda=0$ there is no grokking at all; and the coupling is measured by a quantity whose quasi-tautology the author himself admits.
