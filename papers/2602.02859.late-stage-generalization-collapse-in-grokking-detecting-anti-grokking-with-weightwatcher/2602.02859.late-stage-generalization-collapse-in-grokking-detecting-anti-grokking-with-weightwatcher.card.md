## Concepts covered

**[Anti-grokking](../../concepts/anti-grokking.md)** — the work introduces the notion itself and gives it empirical content: a third, late phase in which the test accuracy collapses back to chance while the training accuracy remains flawless ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)). Outwardly it resembles the pre-grokking phase, but it is built differently — and the difference is visible in the weight spectra ([`p1-8`](#p1-8)). To see it the training is extended to $10^{7}$ steps, that is, two orders of magnitude longer than usual.

**[Heavy-tailed self-regularisation (HTSR)](../../concepts/heavy-tailed-self-regularization-htsr.md)** — the frame of the analysis. The power-law exponent $\alpha$ of the layer-wise spectrum is divided into regimes: $\alpha\gtrsim 5{-}6$ — a random-like layer, $2\lesssim\alpha\lesssim 5{-}6$ — an accumulation of correlations and good generalisation, $\alpha=2$ — the ideal, $\alpha<2$ — overfitting ([`p3-11`](#p3-11)). SETOL theory adds a justification: $\alpha=2$ corresponds to a state resembling a phase boundary between perfect generalisation and overfitting ([`p3-13`](#p3-13)).

**[Correlation traps](../../concepts/correlation-traps.md)** — the main indicator and the main contribution. A weight matrix is shuffled **entrywise**, which destroys all correlations; if eigenvalues far beyond the Marchenko–Pastur edge remain in the spectrum after that, they are traps ([`p4-9`](#p4-9), [`p4-11`](#p4-11)). The key point: before the anti-grokking phase there is **not a single** trap — neither in the pre-grokking nor in the grokking phase — and then there are several at once ([`p6-7`](#p6-7)).

**[Spectral analysis](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — the whole method is built on the empirical spectral density of the layer-wise matrices and on comparing it with a Marchenko–Pastur null model ([`p3-8`](#p3-8)). It matters that neither $\alpha$ nor the traps require access to the training or the test data ([`p1-4`](#p1-4)).

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — the comparison is conducted honestly and with a negative outcome for the rivals: the activation sparsity, the absolute weight entropy and the approximate local circuit complexity track the first two phases but give no warning of the collapse ([`p6-3`](#p6-3)). The argument is strengthened by an indication of **why** they will not do: they have no theoretically established cut-off, and the growth of $\Lambda_{AS}$ in the pre-grokking and in the anti-grokking phases is indistinguishable ([`p6-5`](#p6-5)).

**[Memorisation](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — the work distinguishes **kinds** of memorisation, which the corpus had not done before. In the MLP the pre-grokking phase is a weakly correlated memorisation ($\alpha>2$) and the anti-grokking phase is an overfitting to prototypes; in the transformer on modular addition all the layers are strongly overfitted in every phase, and the grokking is marked by a *rule memorisation* — one or two very large eigenvalues instead of a formed power-law tail ([`p2-4`](#p2-4), [`p7-4`](#p7-4)).

**[Ungrokking](../../concepts/ungrokking.md)** — the demarcation from Varma et al. is drawn precisely: there the loss of generalisation arises under **retraining on a smaller** dataset and is explained by weight decay, here on the **original** dataset, under long training and **without** weight decay ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)).

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — plays the role of a suppressor: at WD>0 half as many traps arise and the collapse is barely noticeable ([`p6-7`](#p6-7)). At WD=0 the weight norm grows and the grokking happens all the same — one more independent piece of evidence against explaining grokking by the norm ([`p1-6`](#p1-6), [`p11-11`](#p11-11)).

**[Catastrophic forgetting](../../concepts/catastrophic-forgetting.md)** — in the transformer the anti-grokking is built the opposite way from the MLP case: $\alpha$ does not fall below 2 but **rises** above 2, the power-law tail shortens, and this is read as catastrophic forgetting ([`p7-5`](#p7-5), [`p8-4`](#p8-4)).

**[Mechanistic interpretability](../../concepts/mechanistic-interpretability.md)** — the traps are tied to content: the leading right singular vector of the first layer travels from structureless noise through a smooth ring-like pattern to **digit-like** patterns, and the rows of $W_{1}$ in the anti-grokking phase look like distinct patterns of digits ([`p6-8`](#p6-8), [`p17-7`](#p17-7)).

**[Fourier features and circuits](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — appendix H checks the transformer from the Fourier side too: in all three phases the same six frequencies dominate, but in the grokking phase the peaks are sharp while in the anti-grokking phase the appearance of the embeddings becomes broadband with the sharp core of the rule preserved ([`p22-2`](#p22-2), [`p22-4`](#p22-4)).

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** and **[real data](../../concepts/vision-real-world-data-mnist-cifar.md)** — both of the corpus's canonical settings: a three-layer MLP on 1000 MNIST examples and a single-layer transformer on addition modulo 113 ([`p10-2`](#p10-2), [`p20-1`](#p20-1)).

**[Phase transition](../../concepts/phase-transition.md)** — the picture is three-phase, and the transitions are marked not by the accuracy curve but by spectral quantities ([`p1-10`](#p1-10), [`p1-11`](#p1-11)).

It is worth saying separately what the work does **not** contain. There is no intervention: the traps are detected but neither removed nor deliberately introduced, so the causality remains unfounded ([`p8-5`](#p8-5)). There is no analysis of why the anti-grokking is built oppositely in the MLP and in the transformer ([`p8-4`](#p8-4)). And there is no check on large models: the observation about layers with $\alpha<2$ in gpt-oss-20b and 120b is given as an aside ([`p8-6`](#p8-6)).

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the original phenomenon; [Liu et al. 2022](../2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning/2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning.card.md) and [Omnigrok](../2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data/2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data.card.md) — the MNIST setting and the explanation through the weight norm, with which the work argues; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — the modular-addition setting and progress measures; [Varma et al. 2023](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — ungrokking, from which the work demarcates itself; [Merrill et al. 2023](../2303.11873.a-tale-of-two-circuits-grokking-as-competition-of-sparse-and-dense-subnetworks/2303.11873.a-tale-of-two-circuits-grokking-as-competition-of-sparse-and-dense-subnetworks.card.md) — the competition of circuits; Humayun et al. 2024 (delayed robustness; there is no card in the wiki); [Liu et al. 2023](../2310.05918.grokking-as-compression-a-nonlinear-complexity-perspective/2310.05918.grokking-as-compression-a-nonlinear-complexity-perspective.card.md) — compression as an explanation.

## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work is experimental and instrumental: one new phenomenon, one ready-made tool, two canonical settings extended by two orders of magnitude.

**The main contribution is the phenomenon itself, and it was obtained cheaply: it sufficed not to stop the training.** Both settings of the corpus, extended to $10^{7}$ steps, collapse the test accuracy back to chance at a flawless training accuracy ([`p1-3`](#p1-3)). This changes the picture from "two phases" to "three" and poses an awkward question to every work on accelerating grokking: where exactly should one stop?

**The trap indicator is elegantly built and checkable.** Entrywise shuffling destroys **all** correlations while preserving the multiset of magnitudes; if a large eigenvalue survives this, the cause lies in individual outsized entries rather than in a learned structure ([`p16-4`](#p16-4)). Appendix F proves a sufficient condition through the BBP regime: a single entry with $|W_{pq}|/\sqrt{N}\to\theta>\sigma(1+\sqrt{\gamma})$ suffices.

**The separation of the indicators by strength is done honestly.** The traps are named the main indicator and $\alpha$ a secondary and weaker one ([`p5-3`](#p5-3)); and where $\alpha$ is unreliable (the transformer, whose whole spectrum is power-law) this is said outright ([`p4-5`](#p4-5), [`p20-3`](#p20-3)).

**The negative comparison with other people's measures is the second most valuable result.** Golechha's three measures track the first two phases and give no warning of the collapse ([`p6-3`](#p6-3)). It matters that the argument does not reduce to "our measure is better": it is pointed out that the other measures have no theoretical cut-off, and that the growth of $\Lambda_{AS}$ in the pre-grokking and in the anti-grokking phases is therefore indistinguishable ([`p6-5`](#p6-5)).

**But no causality is shown.** All the relations are observational: the traps **coincide** with the collapse ([`p6-7`](#p6-7)). Not one intervention — neither removing a trap by editing the weights nor introducing one deliberately — is carried out, although SETOL theory predicts precisely a causal connection ([`p8-5`](#p8-5)).

**The two cases of anti-grokking are opposite in sign, and this is not explained.** In the MLP $\alpha$ falls below 2 (overfitting), in the transformer it rises above 2 (forgetting) ([`p8-4`](#p8-4)). What remains common is only the presence of traps. This weakens the claim of a single phase: two different failures are gathered under one name.

**The identification of the traps with prototypes is vivid but qualitative.** The singular vectors in the anti-grokking phase look like digits ([`p6-8`](#p6-8), [`p17-7`](#p17-7)) — an argument convincing to the eye, but neither the share of the errors explained by these prototypes nor a matching of particular traps to particular examples is given.

**The number of runs is not stated everywhere, but the spread is given.** The tables carry $\pm$ over seeds, and appendix C has a repeat run (see figure 11). The main figure 1 is meanwhile a single run, and the phase boundaries are drawn by eye.

**The reproducibility setup is good.** All the hyperparameters are given in a table, and the version of WeightWatcher (0.7.5.5), the hardware and the computation time are named ([`p10-2`](#p10-2)). The tool is open, which matters for an instrumental work.

**The observation about large language models is offered as a lead.** In gpt-oss-20b and 120b there are unusually many layers with $\alpha<2$ ([`p8-6`](#p8-6)); the traps there are "not shown", and no connection with quality is measured. This is a direction, not a result.

**The limitations are acknowledged outright and substantively.** It is said that $\alpha\approx 2$ does not guarantee optimality, that there are well-trained models with $\alpha\gg 2$, and that the mapping of $\alpha$ values onto quality levels depends on the setting ([`p8-7`](#p8-7)). For a work promoting its own tool this is a rare measure of restraint.

**Its place in the corpus.** This is the wiki's only work showing that grokking has a **third** phase and that generalisation can be lost simply by continuing the training. The method is valuable too: both measures are computed from the weights alone, with no data — that is, they are applicable where there is no test set at all. The price: the absence of an intervention (and hence of causality), two oppositely built cases under one name, and a qualitative identification of the traps with memorised prototypes.

###### mc-2205-10343
**Liu et al. (2205.10343) — a systematic bibliography error: the whole of Omnigrok is hung on the earlier work.** Onto the reference "Liu et al., 2022" are strung here the l2 analysis of the weight norm, the "lower-norm solutions" from weight decay and the MNIST setup with a truncated sample — all of it the material of [Omnigrok](../2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data/2210.01117.omnigrok-grokking-beyond-algorithmic-data.card.md), which is absent from the paper's bibliography altogether; 2205.10343 itself [introduced the Goldilocks zone in the hyperparameters](../2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning/original/2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning.md#p1-2) and [the first MNIST demonstration](../2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning/original/2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning.md#p9-2), but not the l2 analytics. The diagnosis is confirmed by a textually related work of the same group (2506.04434): there the same sentences carry the correct key liu2023omnigrok — when the text was carried over, the reference "slid" onto the earlier work.
