## Concepts covered

**[The NTK](../../concepts/neural-tangent-kernel-ntk.md)** — the work gives the corpus a **proof** rather than an observation: permutation-equivariant kernel methods on modular addition cannot generalise until they have seen a constant fraction of all the data ([`p3-4`](#p3-4)). And the same network in the kernel regime does reach zero training error (theorem 3.1). Overfitting without generalisation turns out to be a consequence of two theorems at once rather than an accident of the settings.

**[From lazy to rich](../../concepts/lazy-to-rich-kernel-to-feature-learning.md)** — the concluding formulation: the results "strongly support the view of grokking as a consequence of a transition from kernel-like behaviour to the limiting behaviour of gradient descent" ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)). This dichotomy is named the most promising theory of grokking ([`p2-1`](#p2-1)), and the work carries it through to numbers: an $\Omega(1)$ fraction of the data in the kernel regime against $\tilde{\omega}(p^{2})$ and $\tilde{\mathcal{O}}(p^{5/3})$ in the rich one.

**[Generalisation bounds](../../concepts/generalization-bounds.md)** — both sides of the gap are proved. From below, a new general framework for analysing the population $\ell_{2}$ loss of predictors of intrinsic dimension $n$ ([`p3-5`](#p3-5)), which also covers modular addition with $m$ summands. From above, "optimistic rates" through the Rademacher complexity in regression and a PAC-Bayes bound through the margin in classification.

**[Margin maximisation](../../concepts/margin-maximization-implicit-bias.md)** — the implicit bias of the late phase is not taken at random: the $\ell_{\infty}$ norm is chosen because it is precisely what Adam regularises (AdamW converges only to KKT points of the problem with an $\ell_{\infty}$ constraint). In classification the bound is built through the $\ell_{\infty}$-normalised margin ([`p10-2`](#p10-2)), in regression through the small $\ell_{\infty}$ norm of the interpolating solution.

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — the connection with grokking is drawn carefully: in Power et al. grokking is more pronounced under a decoupled weight decay, and recent works show that Adam implicitly regularises the $\ell_{\infty}$ norm of the weights ([`p4-8`](#p4-8)). Hence the choice of an explicit $\ell_{\infty}$ regulariser in the experiments — of strength $10^{-4}$ in regression and $10^{-20}$ in classification.

**[Initialisation scale](../../concepts/initialization-scale.md)** — an intervention confirming the mechanism: reducing the initialisation scale **mitigates** grokking to the point where the gap between training and test disappears ([`fig-2`](#fig-2)). The explanation is direct: small weights have to grow in order to fit the data, and that growth takes the network out of the kernel regime **before** it overfits. The price is that the training becomes harder.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the task is analysed in two settings at once: regression ($p^{3}$ points, a squared loss) and classification ($p^{2}$ points, a cross-entropy) ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)). The architecture is a two-layer MLP with a quadratic activation, the same as in Gromov.

**[Sparse parity](../../concepts/sparse-parity.md)** — the introduction lists where grokking is met with beyond modular arithmetic: sparse parities, image classifiers, greatest common divisors, matrix completion, $k$-sparse linear predictors ([`p1-3`](#p1-3)).

**[The slingshot effect](../../concepts/slingshot.md)** and **[representation learning](../../concepts/structured-representation-learning.md)** — both are mentioned in the survey as explanations that the work neither refutes nor uses ([`p1-4`](#p1-4)): its own explanation lies on another plane — in the properties of the task rather than in the dynamics of the optimiser.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the notion receives what nobody else in the corpus offers: an **impossibility proof** for a whole class of methods. Not "we observed that networks first overfit" but "no permutation-equivariant kernel method can generalise at $n<Cp^{3}$".

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the original task; [Gromov 2023](../2301.02679.grokking-modular-arithmetic/2301.02679.grokking-modular-arithmetic.card.md) — the same architecture and the analytical construction, to which there is a direct objection here (see below); [Kumar et al. 2023](../2310.06110.grokking-as-the-transition-from-lazy-to-rich-training-dynamics/2310.06110.grokking-as-the-transition-from-lazy-to-rich-training-dynamics.card.md) — the lazy-to-rich transition hypothesis, which this work proves for modular addition; [Rubin et al. 2024](../2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks/2310.03789.grokking-as-a-first-order-phase-transition-in-two-layer-networks.card.md) — the same conclusion about the inability of the random-feature regime to grok, but obtained from the theory of phase transitions; [Nanda et al. 2023](../2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability/2301.05217.progress-measures-for-grokking-via-mechanistic-interpretability.card.md) — the Fourier solution found by reverse engineering.

**[Quadratic networks](../../concepts/quadratic-networks.md)** — the quadratic activation here is a premise of the theorem: two-layer quadratic networks with a zero training loss and a bounded $\ell_\infty$ norm generalise from substantially fewer points ([`p1-2`](#p1-2)).

**[The internal symmetry of the task](../../concepts/intrinsic-task-symmetry.md)** — here the symmetry works the other way round: the task is *fundamentally hard* for permutation-equivariant kernel methods precisely because of the symmetries inherent in it ([`p3-4`](#p3-4)). The proof proceeds by a lower bound rather than by measurement, and therefore constrains a whole class of methods.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). This is the most demonstrative work in the corpus: nearly everything in it is theorems, and it is therefore important to separate what is proved from what is supported by experiment.

**An impossibility is proved rather than observed.** The main result is negative and therefore strong: **no** permutation-equivariant kernel method generalises on modular addition until it has seen an $\Omega(1)$ fraction of all the points ([`p3-4`](#p3-4)). The reason is the symmetry of the task: learning the original labelling is exactly as hard as learning all its permuted versions at once. This explains why overfitting was observed in the most varied architectures: the architecture is not the point.

**The other half of the gap is proved too.** Solutions with a zero error and a small $\ell_{\infty}$ norm generalise from $\tilde{\omega}(p^{2})$ examples (regression) and $\tilde{\mathcal{O}}(p^{5/3})$ (classification); such solutions exist — a Fourier construction at $h=8p$ is exhibited, and remark F.1 shows that for odd $p$ some $4p$ neurons suffice.

**The weak place is the junction between the halves.** That gradient descent **actually arrives** at a solution with a small $\ell_{\infty}$ norm is not proved: there is theorem [51], to the effect that under a small regularisation the maximal normalised margin converges to the unregularised one, and there is an experiment showing that the $\ell_{\infty}$ norm of the learned weights does not grow with $p$. A rigorous chain "the kernel regime → the exit → a small norm" the work does not give, and it does not claim to.

**An experiment confirming the mechanism.** Reducing the initialisation scale mitigates grokking to the point where the gap disappears ([`fig-2`](#fig-2)) — exactly what the kernel-regime explanation predicts: small weights are forced to grow, and that growth takes the network out of the regime before it overfits. And the eNTK measurement: before the training set is fitted it barely changes, afterwards it changes sharply ([`p10-1`](#p10-1)).

**A direct objection to Gromov.** Footnote 1 says: he claims that the analytical solution he found is the one gradient descent arrives at, "but in our experience this appears not to be the case". This is the corpus's first direct empirical objection of one card to another, and it is left in a footnote with no supporting data.

**Limitations named by the authors themselves.** A large $\ell_{2}$ loss does not entail a large classification error, and a lower bound **in accuracy** for permutation-equivariant kernels remains open. The theorems concern the cause of grokking rather than techniques for accelerating it: grokking can be removed by reducing the initialisation, but the final generalisation is only slowed down by that.

**A gap between what is proved and what is checked.** Theorem C.2 on equivariance is stated for linear update rules; Adam is covered by corollary C.7 with a sketch of an inductive proof. All the experiments, meanwhile, are run with Adam, AdamW and normalised GD.

**Its place in the corpus.** With Kumar et al. the relation is direct: their lazy-to-rich transition hypothesis is here **proved** for modular addition. With Rubin et al. it is a coincidence of conclusion by entirely different means: there the random-feature regime does not grok for phase-transition reasons, here kernel methods do not generalise for reasons of symmetry. With Gromov it is a shared architecture and a dispute about what exactly descent finds.

## Common misreadings and overstatements of this paper

Patterns of misreading or overstatement of this work, as observed in the citing papers of the corpus. Each entry says what exactly gets distorted in the restatement and leads to the authors' own paragraphs, where the qualification that goes missing still stands. The specific wordings are examined in the citing works' cards, in their "Common misreadings and overstatements" sections, where the "details" links lead.

**"It is proved that grokking is a transition from the kernel regime to the rich one" (an over-extension).** The work has two theorems on opposite sides of the gap: [an impossibility for permutation-equivariant kernel methods](#p3-4) and [the existence of generalising solutions with a small l∞ norm](#p3-5); that gradient descent actually arrives at such a solution, however, is shown only by experiment. Paraphrases like "explains grokking through two-phase dynamics" present the junction of the phases as proved. The details: [2509.17738](../2509.17738.flatness-is-necessary-neural-collapse-is-not-rethinking-generalization-via-grokking/2509.17738.flatness-is-necessary-neural-collapse-is-not-rethinking-generalization-via-grokking.card.md#mc-2407-12332); a milder echo: [2510.04930](../2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking/2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking.card.md).

**The lower bound carried over to "models in general" (erroneous).** The bound "a constant fraction of the data is needed" governs kernel methods alone; the work's main result is opposite in spirit — [in the rich regime a fraction of order 1/p suffices](#p3-5). The paraphrase "models need to be trained on a constant fraction … to generalize" turns half a theorem into a conclusion about all models. The details: [2410.03569](../2410.03569.making-hard-problems-easier-with-custom-data-distributions-and-loss-regularization/2410.03569.making-hard-problems-easier-with-custom-data-distributions-and-loss-regularization.card.md#mc-2407-12332).

**A distortion of the technical machinery (erroneous).** The work is credited with "piecewise-linear MLPs" and an "analysis of weight decay" — even though [the activation is quadratic](#p3-1) and [the regulariser is an explicit l∞ one emulating the implicit bias of Adam](#p4-8). A related mild distortion is "relies on very wide networks": the widths are moderate, and the impossibility result is not about width at all. The details: [2504.03162](../2504.03162.beyond-progress-measures-theoretical-insights-into-the-mechanism-of-grokking/2504.03162.beyond-progress-measures-theoretical-insights-into-the-mechanism-of-grokking.card.md#mc-2407-12332); a milder echo: [2509.21519](../2509.21519.provable-scaling-laws-of-feature-emergence-from-learning-dynamics-of-grokking/2509.21519.provable-scaling-laws-of-feature-emergence-from-learning-dynamics-of-grokking.card.md).

**"Reducing the initialisation accelerates grokking" (an over-extension, empirically contested).** In the authors' account [reducing the initialisation scale mitigates grokking to the point where the gap disappears — but at the price of slower training](#fig-2), not "accelerates" it. The group attribution of a regime-gap hypothesis is contested by the citing work itself: in their runs grokking persists under a calibrated initialisation. The details: [2504.13292](../2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model/2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model.card.md#mc-2407-12332).

Examples of careful citation: [2601.19791](../2601.19791.to-grok-grokking-provable-grokking-in-ridge-regression/2601.19791.to-grok-grokking-provable-grokking-in-ridge-regression.card.md) — the result plus a precise statement of the unproved junction; [2604.00316](../2604.00316.breaking-data-symmetry-is-needed-for-generalization-in-feature-learning-kernels/2604.00316.breaking-data-symmetry-is-needed-for-generalization-in-feature-learning-kernels.card.md) — "what is proved about kernels" and "what happens at the exit" kept separate; [2504.13292](../2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model/2504.13292.let-me-grok-for-you-accelerating-grokking-via-embedding-transfer-from-a-weaker-model.card.md) — an exact quotation alongside an honest empirical challenge and a correct distinction between works by same-named authors.
