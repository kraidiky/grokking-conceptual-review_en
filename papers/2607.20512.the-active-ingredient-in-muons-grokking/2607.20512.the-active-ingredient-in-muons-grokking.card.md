# Wang 2026 — The Active Ingredient in Muon’s Grokking

See also: [the global concept index](../../concepts/index.md).

**Yufeng Wang** (independent researcher).

arXiv [2607.20512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20512), July 2026 (v1), 7 pages, not a single figure — the whole experiment lives in seven tables. Citations by Semantic Scholar — 1, in the corpus — 1 (2608.07436). The work is wholly secondary in conception and honest about it: the ready-made claim of 2504.16041 that "Muon groks faster than AdamW" is taken apart by a 2×2 ablation. The outcome: the active ingredient is the **orthogonalisation** of the momentum (the Newton–Schulz iteration) rather than the spectral scaling ("orthogonalisation only" is indistinguishable from the full Muon, "scaling only" is no faster than AdamW and is highly scattered); the orthogonalising variants reach the grok at an about threefold smaller largest singular value and with an embedding spread over the frequencies; and reducing the number of iterations from five to one speeds the first crossing of the threshold up but makes the grokked solution fragile (6/8 seeds collapse to 0.23–0.38). The methodological moral, demonstrated on the author's own variant: claims about the speed of grokking flip over when the measure is changed — hence every number is given in two measures, "the first crossing" and "the sustained grok".

## Concepts covered

**[Optimisers (Adam/AdamW/SGD)](../../concepts/optimizer-adam-adamw-sgd.md)** — a direct continuation and a partial refutation of 2504.16041: of the pair of ingredients postulated there, "a constraint on the spectral norm plus an orthogonalised momentum", only the orthogonalisation finds support — M1, "orthogonalisation only" ($1744\pm 113$), is indistinguishable from the full Muon ($p{=}0.21$) and outruns AdamW ($p{=}0.010$), whereas M2, "scaling only", is no faster than AdamW and is extremely scattered ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)); the predecessor's terminological sleight of hand is removed too — it is said outright that spectral_scale is a scalar per matrix rather than a projection onto the spectral norm ([`p2-3`](#p2-3)). The analysis: the "2×2 design" is incomplete — the cell "raw momentum without the multiplier" was never run, and AdamW took its place; and the "inertness" of the scaling conflicts with §5.4, where on subtraction the canonical Muon loses to AdamW precisely "because of the spectral term".

**[Spectral analysis (SVD/ESD/FVE)](../../concepts/spectral-analysis-svd-esd-fve.md)** — the orthogonalising variants reach the grok at an about $3\times$ smaller largest singular value ($5.5$ and $6.2$ against $15.9$ for AdamW) ([`p3-7`](#p3-7)); the work's only genuine cross-check is on the movement of the embeddings: the relative change $\lVert\Delta E\rVert/\lVert E_{\text{init}}\rVert\approx 1.0$ for all the variants, while the inverse participation ratio diverges ($0.085$ for AdamW against $0.022$), so that "it simply moves the embeddings less" is ruled out ([`p6-4`](#p6-4)). The analysis: the singular value is a single scalar with no spread and with no indication of which matrix it belongs to; and the power-averaged IPR is admitted by the author himself to be a coarse substitute for a mode-by-mode analysis.

**[Fourier features](../../concepts/fourier-features-circuits.md)** — two different grokked solutions by frequency composition: Muon and Lean5 leave the spectrum of the embeddings spread out (a Fourier IPR of $0.022$–$0.025$), whereas AdamW and "scaling only" concentrate it in a few modes ($0.085$); the fragile $ns{=}1$ is intermediate ($0.069$), and its instability is read as a partial slide towards a concentrated code ([`p3-7`](#p3-7)). The analysis: $0.085$ corresponds to about a dozen effective modes — far from the sparse set of a few frequencies by which Nanda et al. describe a grokked circuit; and a finer measure in the citing 2608.07436 confirms the direction.

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — a ready measuring convention for all works on acceleration: the double measure "first crossing" / "sustained grok", with a reversal of the conclusion demonstrated on the author's own proposal — Lean1 ($ns{=}1$) is the fastest by the first crossing ($1395$ against $1750$, $p{=}0.005$), but collapses after the grok on 6/8 seeds, and by the sustained measure it is worse than the canonical Muon ([`p4-3`](#p4-3)); the speed–stability frontier is governed by the pair (the number of iterations, the learning rate) ([`p4-4`](#p4-4)). The analysis: the budget of the main experiment is not named, and "stability to the end of the training" is a property of the length of the run: the citing 2608.07436 shows a collapse at the canonical five iterations too, on horizons from a hundred thousand steps.

**[Grokking time](../../concepts/grokking-time.md)** — the work shows that a single measure of time is not enough: the steps-to-grok are given both by the first crossing of the threshold and by the sustained grok ([`p2-4`](#p2-4)), and the ordering of the variants differs between the measures.

**[Seed variance and reproducibility](../../concepts/seed-variance-reproducibility.md)** — the spread contests a gain reported as a single number: at a lower learning rate the variant groks in only one seed out of four ([`p6-1`](#p6-1)).
## What is shown and what is conjectured

These are the card editor's remarks, not the authors' text, drawn from a numbered pass over the paper's claims.

**The strong point — exemplary measurement hygiene on a small task.** No fewer than 5 seeds per setting, $t$-tests with a Holm–Bonferroni correction, two pre-fixed principal comparisons (both of which survive the correction), and a count in FLOPs and in wall time alongside the steps — a direct correction of everything the corpus reproaches 2504.16041 for; a bit-exact reproducibility by seed with 28 tests, and internal cross-checks that agree ($ns{=}0$ repeats M2, $ns{=}5$ is bit-identical to M0). The cross-check on the movement of the embeddings is a model of how to dispose of an obvious objection. The double measure with the conclusion reversed on the author's own variant is the main thing to be inherited.

**The ablation is narrower than it sounds.** The fourth cell of the 2×2 design (a raw momentum without the multiplier — ordinary SGD with momentum) is missing, and AdamW took its place — a method with an entirely different update rule; the conclusion that "the spectral scaling is inert" is close to an identity ($\gamma$ is a constant scalar per matrix, absorbed into the learning rate; for square matrices $\gamma{=}1$ exactly) and conflicts with the author's own §5.4, where the spectral term costs Muon a significant loss on subtraction — only the weak form "removing it does no harm" holds. "Pre-registered" is used three times with no reference to a registered plan. The handling of the runs that did not grok is not stated; in appendix A the value for Muon at lr$=10^{-3}$ coincided with §3.1 down to a single step when the number of seeds was changed (all the neighbouring numbers shifted) — with no explanation; and the abstract on the arXiv page disagrees with the text (it has "does not isolate", the misprint "Moun", and calls the measure sustained-grok).

**Stability is a property of the horizon.** "M0/M1 are stable" and "five iterations is the robust choice" are checked on a horizon of thousands of steps; the later 2608.07436 (which cites this work in four places) shows a post-grokking collapse at five iterations too, over 100k+ steps, so that the work's own methodological moral applies to itself. The accounting of the cost of a step is suspiciously lenient: a Muon step is dearer than an AdamW one by only a factor of 1.05 in FLOPs, against 1.75–1.80 in the measurements of 2608.07436 (part of the difference is explicable by the single-layer model, but the rule for charging the Newton–Schulz iteration is not written out as a formula).

**How the work will be cited** ("it is proved that the active ingredient of Muon is the orthogonalisation") — more precisely: on a single-layer transformer with $d{=}128$, modulus 97, 4–8 seeds and a horizon of thousands of steps, it is shown that switching the constant multiplier $\gamma$ off changes nothing while switching the Newton–Schulz iteration off destroys the speed-up; the cell "raw momentum without the multiplier" was never run at all.
