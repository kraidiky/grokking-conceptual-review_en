## Concepts covered

**[Progress measures](../../concepts/progress-measures.md)** — the work proposes a measure of a different kind from the rest of the corpus: not an internal quantity of a trained network but a **spectral signature of the training curve** ([`p3-1`](#p3-1)). The training loss over the first 400 steps is taken, its non-oscillatory components are removed, and the spectral energy — the Hjorth activity — is computed. Its value lies in the measure being computed **before** the grokking and costing four hundred steps instead of ten thousand.

**[The loss landscape and basins](../../concepts/loss-landscape-basins.md)** — the central subject of the work. Three hypotheses are advanced: the model gets stuck in local solutions; the model crosses an ill-conditioned valley; or both at once ([`p4-1`](#p4-1)). One-dimensional slices along the direction "current point → minimum" show **two minimisers** of the training loss, of which only one minimises the validation loss: during the memorisation the model sits in the first, and grokking sets in when it manages to break out of it.

**[The slingshot effect](../../concepts/slingshot.md)** — the oscillations of the training curve are identified with the slingshots of Thilak et al., and the work adds a **landscape-level explanation** to them: this is an escape from the sharp ridges surrounding local optima. The measurable signs of a slingshot are enumerated: a sudden rise and fall of the loss, a jump of the relative cosine similarity of the weights, and an obstruction in a slice of the landscape.

**[The edge of stability](../../concepts/edge-of-stability.md)** — the connection is drawn explicitly through the condition $\lambda_{t}(i)>2/\epsilon_{t}$, under which a descent step **increases** the loss ([`p12-12`](#p12-12)). Hence also the progressive sharpening of Cohen et al.: $\max_{i}\lambda_{t}(i)$ grows up to $2\epsilon_{t}^{-1}$, and the model enters the edge-of-stability regime.

**[The memorisation phase](../../concepts/memorization-phase.md)** — working definitions of four moments are given: $t_{1}$ — the training accuracy first exceeds zero, $t_{2}$ — it reaches 100 %, $t_{3}$ — the validation accuracy first exceeds zero, $t_{4}$ — it reaches 100 % ([`p2-9`](#p2-9)). Grokking is defined as a large delay $t_{4}-t_{2}$; the authors caveat honestly, however, that for simplicity they call any generalisation grokking.

**[Critical dataset size](../../concepts/data-fraction-critical-dataset-size.md)** — the moment of generalisation follows a power law $t_{4}(r)=ar^{-\gamma}+b$, and the fitted values are given: $(\gamma,a,b)=(7.73,1.09\times 10^{15},1442.63)$ for modular addition and $(1.18,1.85\times 10^{6},0)$ for $S_{5}$. The law is used utilitarianly — as a criterion for when to stop training, having decided that there will be no grokking.

**[Double descent](../../concepts/double-descent.md)** — grokking is proposed to be seen as a **very slow double descent** or as an extreme case of gradient starvation ([`p7-3`](#p7-3)); it is noted that the validation loss does indeed behave accordingly — it falls, rises, and then drops to zero.

**[The learning rate](../../concepts/learning-rate.md)** — the conclusion of Jastrzębski et al. is reproduced and applied: the control parameter of SGD is not the batch size in itself but the **ratio** of the learning rate to the batch, and larger values of it lead into wider minima ([`p1-4`](#p1-4)). Raising the learning rate speeds grokking up to a threshold that depends on the weight decay.

**[Weight decay](../../concepts/weight-decay.md)** — the phase diagrams are built precisely in the coordinates "weight decay × learning rate", and generalisation is most often observed at **small** values of both ([`p3-4`](#p3-4)). This is worth setting against Omnigrok, where weight decay on the contrary speeds grokking up.

**[Optimisers](../../concepts/optimizer-adam-adamw-sgd.md)** — the base setting is on AdamW ([`p2-8`](#p2-8)), and in appendix D six optimisers are compared on the Rosenbrock and Rastrigin functions as toy models of grokking. The conclusion: the adaptive methods (rmsprop, rprop, adam, adamax) get through the Rosenbrock valley and plain SGD does not; the global minimum of Rastrigin is reached by **none**.

**[From lazy to rich](../../concepts/lazy-to-rich-kernel-to-feature-learning.md)** — it is measured that the model spends most of its time in the **lazy regime**: the cosine distance between the weights at adjacent steps is nearly constant except at the slingshots. This makes the picture "a long near-standstill interrupted by jumps" quantitative.

**[The compression of representations](../../concepts/manifold-representation-compression.md)** — appendix C measures the **intrinsic dimension** of the activation manifolds layer by layer (by the TWONN and MLE methods): it grows at the very start of the training, then falls with strong oscillations and stabilises. The dimension of the last layer correlates with the oscillations of the quality.

**[Low-pass filtering of the gradient](../../concepts/gradient-low-pass-filtering.md)** — an echo of Grokfast, which appeared a year later, and the two should be distinguished: here a low-pass filter is applied to the **loss curve** for the sake of measurement, there to the **gradient** for the sake of intervention. Both works start from the same idea: the slow component of the dynamics is related to generalisation.

**[Modular arithmetic](../../concepts/modular-arithmetic.md)** — the setting of Power et al.: $x\cdot y \bmod 97$ and multiplication in $S_{5}$, a two-layer transformer, about $4\cdot 10^{5}$ parameters ([`fig-1`](#fig-1)).

**[Grokking](../../concepts/grokking.md)** — the notion is moved into an engineering plane: not "what is it" but "how to tell in advance". The claim is framed carefully — the spectral signature is called not a measure of capacity but a quantity whose relation to generalisation may prove informative whether it is positive or negative ([`p3-4`](#p3-4)).

Cards of corpus papers: [Power et al. 2022](../2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets/2201.02177.grokking-generalization-beyond-overfitting-on-small-algorithmic-datasets.card.md) — the task, the setting and the notation; [Liu et al. 2022](../2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning/2205.10343.towards-understanding-grokking-an-effective-theory-of-representation-learning.card.md) — the taxonomy of phases (confusion, memorisation, comprehension), which the work adopts in full ([`p6-1`](#p6-1)); [Slingshot](../2206.04817.the-slingshot-mechanism-an-empirical-study-of-adaptive-optimizers-and-grokking/2206.04817.the-slingshot-mechanism-an-empirical-study-of-adaptive-optimizers-and-grokking.card.md) — the observation about the oscillations, to which a landscape explanation is given here; [Grokfast](../2405.20233.grokfast-accelerated-grokking-by-amplifying-slow-gradients/2405.20233.grokfast-accelerated-grokking-by-amplifying-slow-gradients.card.md) — the same idea about the slow component, but turned into an accelerator.

**[The phases: comprehension, grokking, memorisation, confusion](../../concepts/comprehension-confusion-phases.md)** — here the labelling runs over training time: three successive phases of one run ([`p2-9`](#p2-9)). This is not the quartet that labels the plane of settings, and the coincidence of the word "phase" in the two labellings is a constant source of confusion.

**[The phase diagram](../../concepts/phase-diagram.md)** — the work leans on the observation for whose sake the diagram is needed: grokking lives only within a certain range of hyperparameters ([`p2-1`](#p2-1)), and hence predicting the transition is meaningful only within that range.
## What is shown and what is conjectured

The card editor's remarks following the analysis of the claims (`…claims.txt`). The work's title promises more than its content — and this is worth bearing in mind, because it is usually cited by its title.

**There is no predictor in the work.** There is a resemblance between two heat maps in the coordinates "weight decay × learning rate": one of the validation accuracy after 10 thousand steps, the other of the spectral energy of the loss over the first 400 steps ([`p3-4`](#p3-4)). There is no decision rule with a threshold, no rate of correctly predicted runs, and no type I and type II errors. The only correlation coefficient in the whole paper refers to a comparison of two intrinsic-dimension estimators, not to the prediction of grokking.

**The authors are more cautious than the title.** The spectral signature is called outright not a measure of capacity, and it is caveated that a relation to generalisation may prove informative whether it is positive or negative. It is separately admitted that large learning rates strengthen the oscillations but that this **does not lead** to grokking — that is, the oscillations are not a sufficient sign, and no rule separating the useful oscillations from the useless ones is proposed.

**"Grokking" here means any generalisation.** A definition through the delay $t_{4}-t_{2}$ is given and then explicitly discarded for simplicity ([`p2-9`](#p2-9)). What is predicted, then, is not the delay but the fact of generalisation itself.

**The most valuable picture is the two minima in one slice.** A one-dimensional projection along the direction "current point → minimum" shows two minimisers of the training loss, of which only one minimises the validation loss ([`p4-1`](#p4-1)). The memorising and the generalising solutions turn out to be visible simultaneously, and grokking to be an escape from the first. The authors' caveat is apt: this is a one-dimensional slice, and two minima in it need not be two minima in the full space.

**A negative result stronger than the positive ones.** Clipping the gradient norm weakens the spikes but does not cancel the generalisation — only slows it. Hence it follows that the slingshots **accompany** grokking rather than causing it — a refinement of Thilak et al. that the work itself does not stress.

**The link of the Hjorth parameters with the landscape is drawn carefully.** The activity equals $m_{0}(L)$, the mobility is the activity of the gradient norm, and the complexity is the activity of the Hessian spectrum. This is no ornament: three parameters of a signal turn out to be three quantities of the optimisation.

**The power law is fitted over a narrow range.** $t_{4}(r)=ar^{-\gamma}+b$ with $\gamma=7.73$ and $a\approx10^{15}$ for addition; there is no check on held-out values of $r$, and the authors themselves say that the law breaks down as $r\rightarrow 0$. It is used utilitarianly — as a criterion for when to stop training — and in that capacity it works.

**The toy models remained an analogy.** The Rosenbrock and Rastrigin functions are proposed as models of grokking, but neither the spectral signature nor $t_{4}(r)$ is checked on them. There is one honest result there: the global minimum of Rastrigin is reached by none of the six optimisers.

**Its place in the corpus.** The work adopts the phase taxonomy of Liu et al. in full and carries it over from the phase diagram onto the training trajectory. To the slingshot of Thilak et al. it gives a landscape explanation. And it anticipates Grokfast: the same idea about the slow component of the dynamics — but here a low-pass filter is applied to the loss curve for the sake of measurement, and there to the gradient for the sake of intervention. A divergence from Omnigrok is worth noting separately: here generalisation is more frequent at a **small** weight decay, whereas there weight decay speeds grokking up; the paper makes no comparison.

## Common misreadings and overstatements of this paper

Patterns of erroneous or over-extended citation of this work observed in the citing papers of the corpus. Each entry describes what exactly is distorted in the paraphrase and leads to the authors' own paragraphs, where the lost caveat stands. The analysis of particular formulations is in the citing works' cards, in their "Erroneous citations" sections, to which the "details" links lead. The card predicted that "it is usually cited by its title" — the patterns below bear this out.

**"The oscillations are an established predictor" (an over-extension).** There is no quantitative predictor in the work: the evidence is [a resemblance between two heat maps, presented as "we conjecture … can hint us"](#p3-1), with the caveats that [the spectral signature is not a measure of capacity and the sign of the relation is not established](#p3-4) and that ["large learning rates may amplify the oscillations, but this does not lead directly to grokking"](#p3-4). Paraphrases such as "identified … as predictors" and "proposed the oscillations as an explanation" drop all the hedges at once. The details: [2506.21551](../2506.21551.grokking-in-llm-pretraining-monitor-memorization-to-generalization-without-test/2506.21551.grokking-in-llm-pretraining-monitor-memorization-to-generalization-without-test.card.md#mc-2306-13253); milder echoes: [2410.04489](../2410.04489.grokking-at-the-edge-of-linear-separability/2410.04489.grokking-at-the-edge-of-linear-separability.card.md), [2311.06597](../2311.06597.understanding-grokking-through-a-robustness-viewpoint/2311.06597.understanding-grokking-through-a-robustness-viewpoint.card.md), [2506.05718](../2506.05718.grokking-beyond-the-euclidean-norm-of-model-parameters/2506.05718.grokking-beyond-the-euclidean-norm-of-model-parameters.card.md) — a self-citation by the first author stronger than the original modality.

**Placement in the wrong genre (erroneous).** The work is put in the line of mechanistic interpretability and reverse engineering — even though it contains [a spectral analysis of the loss curve and slices of the landscape](#p3-4), with not a single analysis of circuits. The details: [2402.16726](../2402.16726.towards-empirical-interpretation-of-internal-circuits-and-properties-in-grokked-transformers-on-modular-polynomials/2402.16726.towards-empirical-interpretation-of-internal-circuits-and-properties-in-grokked-transformers-on-modular-polynomials.card.md#mc-2306-13253).

**"A shift towards flatter minima" (erroneous).** The work is credited with a conclusion about a movement towards flat minima — exactly the opposite of its picture: [an ill-conditioned valley, sharp structures around the optima, large condition numbers](#p4-1). The details: [2512.03437](../2512.03437.grokked-models-are-better-unlearners/2512.03437.grokked-models-are-better-unlearners.card.md#mc-2306-13253).

**A drift of priority over the oscillations (an over-extension, mild).** "Previous work discovered oscillation in the loss" — the authors themselves [attribute the observation and the name of the slingshots to Thilak et al.](#p2-11). An echo: [2408.08944](../2408.08944.information-theoretic-progress-measures-reveal-grokking-is-an-emergent-phase-transition/2408.08944.information-theoretic-progress-measures-reveal-grokking-is-an-emergent-phase-transition.card.md). And a separate feature that nobody caveats: [grokking in the work means any generalisation](#p2-9) — "predicting grokking" here does not mean predicting the delay. Plus a confusion of authors: in one citation Pezeshki (the work's third author) is named as the first — [2603.24746](../2603.24746.grokking-as-a-falsifiable-finite-size-transition/2603.24746.grokking-as-a-falsifiable-finite-size-transition.card.md).

Examples of careful citation: [Varma et al.](../2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency/2309.02390.explaining-grokking-through-circuit-efficiency.card.md) — a paraphrase plus an explicit boundary of divergence ("applies even where there are no slingshots or oscillations"); [2511.04760](../2511.04760.when-data-falls-short-grokking-below-the-critical-threshold/2511.04760.when-data-falls-short-grokking-below-the-critical-threshold.card.md) — "has been attempted"; [2510.04930](../2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking/2510.04930.egalitarian-gradient-descent-a-simple-approach-to-accelerated-grokking.card.md) and [2402.15175](../2402.15175.unified-view-of-grokking-double-descent-and-emergent-abilities/2402.15175.unified-view-of-grokking-double-descent-and-emergent-abilities.card.md) — "proposed as predictors" / "potential indicators".
